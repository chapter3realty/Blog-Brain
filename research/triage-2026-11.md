# How we decide whether a page needs work

Owner, 2026-11-08: "lets not just re design everything for the fun of it lets be strict and really understand what needs to be redesigned we dont want thin bad content on our pages we also dont want overly cluttered or bad for SEO/GEO type blogs on the site but alot of those you say need redesigned may be fine blogs not sure how to judge this though and im okay with redesigning almost everything if it really needs it."

The score from `tools/score.js` does not decide this. The pages that earn the most clicks score 75 to 82 (`reports/scores-2026-11-08.md`). A reviewer reads every page and looks at it. Each page gets exactly one verdict, and every verdict needs evidence: a quote from the page or a named screenshot.

## The five verdicts

1. **Keep.** The page answers its question early and well, looks calm, and nothing a reader needs is missing. Change nothing.
2. **Title only.** The page is good, but its title or description does not say what people search, or drops the searched words. Fix those two lines.
3. **Light fix.** One to three named problems a reader would notice, such as a wall of text, a heading that hides the answer, a broken-looking box or a confusing chart label. Fix only those.
4. **Redesign the look.** The content is good, but the page is cluttered or hard to read: stacks of boxes, a giant headline, photos that dominate, a heavy contents box. It gets the approved treatment (the five pilots and the nine of 2026-11-08), keeping every word.
5. **Rebuild the content.** The page is thin. It does not fully answer the question its title asks, it has no local facts another site could not write, a fact is out of date or wrong, or a reader would leave still needing something basic. It goes through the full line: research, an independent verifier, then build.

## Tests for each verdict

- **Thin:** Would a buyer reading only this page still need to search elsewhere for the basic answer? Is there at least one fact from local records that only we have?
- **Cluttered:** In the first two screens, how many separate boxes, badges and widgets are there? Does anything compete with the answer?
- **Search and AI answers:**
  - Does the title carry the words people search?
  - Is the answer in the first screen?
  - Do the headings ask the questions people ask?
  - Does the page compete with a sibling for the same search?
- **Earning clicks:** A page with real clicks in Search Console gets at most a title or a light fix. The exception is a problem a reader would notice.
