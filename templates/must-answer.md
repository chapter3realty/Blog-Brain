# Must-answer lists

A page does not go to the owner until it answers every item on its list, or the item is on the owner question list with the date it was asked. Build each list from what people type into Google about the subject (autocomplete, People Also Ask, Search Console queries), not from what the research happened to find. Audit, 2026-10-11: three of four 55+ pages shipped to review without the monthly HOA fee, the first thing buyers search.

## How to build the list for any page

The lists below are examples, not the rule. For every new page:

1. Type the subject into Google and write down every suggestion (the subject, then the subject plus each letter if it is a big topic). Read "People also ask". Check Search Console for queries that already reach the site.
2. Add what the owner hears from customers about it (`stories/stories.json`, owner answers).
3. Group them by what the reader wants to know. Rank by how many suggestions point to each group.
4. Add the questions every reader of this kind of page has (the reader's job, `reports/Page shapes by kind of page.md`): for a cost page, the total and who pays; for a process, the steps and how long; for a place, where it is and what daily life is like.
5. Add the words readers use in the suggestions ("amenity center", "pros and cons", "reviews"). The page uses them once, in plain context (CRAFT 1).
6. Write the list into the page's brief. Research it first, in order.
7. When a cold reader says "I came for X and the page never told me", X joins the list that day.

## 55+ community page

1. Monthly HOA fee, and what it pays for.
2. What homes cost to buy (recent sales) and what new ones start at, if the builder still sells.
3. The homes: sizes, years built, one story or two, bedrooms.
4. The age rule in plain words, and what it means for family and guests.
5. Amenities and what a normal week is like (clubs, events, activity director).
6. Is it gated?
7. Can I rent it out? Pets? Golf carts? Fences?
8. Where it is: the beach, groceries, hospital, airport, in minutes, with traffic noted.
9. Property tax for a home you live in.
10. Flood: does a lender require insurance, and what it usually costs.
11. What owners find hard (the drawbacks, kindly).
12. Homes for sale now: a link to the listings for this community.

## Topic guide page

1. The answer to the title question, in the first two sentences.
2. What it costs the reader, or what they get.
3. The steps, in order, if there are steps.
4. What can go wrong, and how to avoid it.
5. Who to call or what to do next.
