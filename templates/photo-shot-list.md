# Photo shot list: one community, one phone

For the Chapter3 team. One visit, about an hour. Our own photos beat any photo we can find, and they need no credit to a stranger. The rules behind this list are in `rules/images.md`.

## Before you go

- [ ] Gated or private community? Call the HOA or the sales office first. Ask: "May we photograph the entrance, the clubhouse outside, the pool and a street for our website?" Write down who said yes and when.
- [ ] If no one says yes, shoot only from public roads and public sidewalks.
- [ ] Clean the phone lens. Turn on the grid in the camera settings.

## The shots

Take each one three times. Hold the phone sideways (landscape) for every shot.

## How to shoot

- **Light:** morning, before 10, or the hour before sunset. Keep the sun behind you. No midday shots if you can help it.
- **Level:** keep the horizon straight. Use the grid.
- **No people.** Wait for them to walk out of the frame. If someone is far away and tiny, that is fine.
- **No faces, no license plates, no house numbers.** Turn so they are not in the shot, or come back later.
- **No logos as the subject.** A store sign may be in the shot, small. Never fill the frame with it.
- **No art as the subject.** Skip statues and murals.
- **Do not go inside** a home, a fitness room or a clubhouse without a clear yes from the person in charge.
- **Full size.** Use the phone's normal camera. No filters, no zoom past 2x, no portrait mode.

## After

- [ ] Upload the photos to the team folder, one folder per community, the same day.
- [ ] Write one line per photo: what it shows and where you stood. Example: "Clubhouse, from the visitor parking lot, 8:40 am."
- [ ] Write who gave permission, if you were inside a gate.
- [ ] Delete any photo with a face or a license plate you can read.

We add each photo to `photos.json` with `"license": "own"`, your name as the author and your line as the source.
