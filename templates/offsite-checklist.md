# Off-site checklist

Pages alone do not rank a new brokerage. On 2026-10-01 the site appeared for 1 of 20 target queries. About 18 of 127 pages showed in the indexes that could be reached. There was no Google Business Profile and no review anywhere. See `audit/evidence/offsite-search.md`.

Do part 1 once, in order. Repeat part 2 monthly. Most items need the owner's or Tim's accounts.
