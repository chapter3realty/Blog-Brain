# Research brief template

Copy into `batches/<batch>/facts/RESEARCH-BRIEF.md` and fill in the subject. Researchers fill one fact ledger (`templates/facts.md`) per page. A different agent verifies every row.

## Start from the buyer, not the records

List the questions in the order a buyer asks them. Research in that order. Records come last and only where a buyer question needs them. (Owner, 2026-10-06: "lead with what a buyer would want to know rather than how many acres were annexed by the city".)

For a community or neighborhood page, the buyer's questions are usually:

1. What can I buy there, and what does it cost? Homes built and for sale now, home sizes, price range (MLS once available).
2. What do I pay every month or year? HOA dues and what they include; property tax on a typical price; insurance, including flood insurance for its flood zone.
3. What is life like there? Clubhouse and amenities; **events and clubs, how many a month** (public calendars, newsletters, the HOA's or builder's lifestyle page); what is in walking or golf-cart distance; the beach, groceries, the hospital.
4. Can I? Have family visit or stay (age rules), have pets, fence a yard, rent the home out, use a golf cart.
5. What do I pay once? Fees at closing to the HOA.
6. What could go wrong? Flood zone, wind insurance area, pending assessments, dues increases. These go after the good news on the page, but they are researched with the same care.

For a topic page, write the buyer's questions the same way: what they get, what it costs them, whether they can, what to watch for.

## Rules

- Primary sources only (`templates/facts.md`). A listing site or search summary finds a source; it is never one.
- Find a live source. web.archive.org cannot be reached by the verifier, so an archived-only fact ends up unusable.
- For property tax, pull the county Treasurer's bill for one parcel in the right tax district. It settles the millage in one row.
- For a 55+ community, read the recorded age rule first. If it cannot be read, say so before researching the rest.
- **Monthly HOA dues are the buyer's first money question** (buyer reads, 2026-10-06: three of four pages failed for lack of them). A per-home amount is rarely online for a builder-run HOA: builders say only that payments "do not include" dues, and association sites keep budgets behind a login. Look first. If no primary source gives the amount by the end of day one, put it on the owner question list at once (MLS listings, a call to the manager, or a closing file), so the answer is back before the draft.
- Observable, dated facts only about a named builder, HOA or building. Describe property, rules and places, never the people who live there.

## Also record

- **Waiting for MLS:** resale prices, days on market, price per square foot.
- **Search check:** the top results for the page's question, what each answers, and what a buyer asks that none of them answers.
- **Not verified:** what was found only on a listing site or blog, with what was tried.
