# Batch <N> questions for the owner and Tim

Send this **before** drafting. Answers can be typed or sent as a voice memo; transcribe them verbatim.

Record the answers in the website repo at `research/<cluster>/owner-answers-batch<N>.md`, with two sections: "Checked" and "Applied".

## Rules for writing these questions

- **At most 5 per page.** Most of them ask for experience.
- **Ask about what happened, not what he thinks.** "What happened the last time a client...?" beats "Do you think buyers should...?"
- **Ask for the number he uses**, and where it comes from.
- **Never draft an answer for him to approve, and never draft a quote for Tim** (T5). Ask, then wait.
- **Keep every story anonymous.** No names, buildings, genders or dates that identify a client.
- **A Tim story is a Tim story.** If it happened before Chapter3 existed, it is attributed to Tim, never to "Chapter3" or "we" (website A11e).

## Question shapes that produce usable answers

| Shape | Example | What it becomes on the page |
|---|---|---|
| Last time | "What happened the last time a client bought a condo with a special assessment pending?" | A "Chapter3's files" sentence (T2) |
| First check | "What is the first thing you look at in the board minutes?" | "Our broker reads ... before a client offers." (T2) |
| Number | "What do dues run in an oceanfront building built before 1990, in your experience? From which files?" | A sourced figure, or a "Chapter3's files" range |
| Mistake | "What do out-of-state buyers get wrong most often on this?" | A section, or an FAQ entry |
| Business | "Do we help with X? If a reader asks for Y, what do we say?" | A CTA, and the scope of the page |
