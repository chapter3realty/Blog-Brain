## Who searches, and what they decide

A person who wants a piece of land here: a lot to build a house on, a few acres for a manufactured home, room for horses or a shop, or a tract to hold. Many come from out of state. The decision is **whether this parcel can hold the home they want, and at what price an acre, before they make an offer.** The deciding fact: **in South Carolina the state does not do a perc test. A state inspector reads the soil and issues a septic permit, and the county will not issue a building permit without it.** Price is the second fact: an acre costs far less in the county's rural west and on bigger tracts.

## Questions to answer, in the reader's words

1. How much does an acre of land cost in Horry County? (suggest: "how much is an acre of land in conway south carolina") **Our count: median asking price per acre by area and by tract size.**
2. Where is land cheapest near Myrtle Beach? (suggest: "cheap land for sale horry county sc", "cheap land for sale conway sc")
3. Do I need a perc test to buy land in Horry County? How do I get one? What does it cost? (suggest, five phrasings) **SC DES: no perc test; a soil and site evaluation; $150 application; the permit is good five years.**
4. Can I put a septic tank on any lot? What if it fails? (DES: not all land is suitable; an engineered system can cost thousands more)
5. Is there public water and sewer, or do I need a well? (DES: call the local government; GSWSA serves rural areas west of the Waccamaw; our count of land listings that say public water, sewer, septic approved)
6. Can I put a mobile home on land in Horry County? (suggest: "horry county mobile home permit", "unrestricted land for sale") **Zoning: FA and CFA allow a manufactured home on half an acre; SF districts allow none; MSF districts do. Deed restrictions can forbid it.**
7. What is the minimum lot size in Horry County? (suggest: "horry county zoning", zero suggestions for "minimum lot size") **Half an acre in FA and CFA, 1 acre in LFA and RE, 5 acres in AG4 and AG5.**
8. Can I live in a camper on my land? (suggest gave none; it is a common forum question) **Only on vacant LFA, FA or CFA land on or north and west of the Waccamaw River: two campers, 15 days a month.**
9. Is the land in a flood zone or wetlands? (suggest: "horry county wetlands map", "horry county flood zone map") **Our count of land listings in a FEMA high-risk zone at the listing point; the 3-foot rule; wetlands go to the Army Corps.** Depth on checking a flood zone belongs to /buyers/flood-zone-check/.
10. Does the lot need road frontage? (suggest: "horry county driveway permit") **50 feet of frontage on a street for a new lot; a 60-foot setback on named state highways.**
11. What does it cost to build on land: permits and impact fees? (suggest: "horry county building permit fees", "horry county impact fees") **Building permit $0.20 plus $0.50 per square foot plus $25 zoning; impact fees about $1,615 on a 1,500 sq ft house; mobile home permit $150.** Depth on impact fees belongs to /invest/horry-county-growth-roads-permits/.
12. Does land get a lower tax rate? Farm or timber land? (suggest: "land tax horry county", 10 a month) **Agricultural use: timber needs 5 acres, other farm use 10 acres or income; rollback tax for the current year and three before if the use changes. Your home and up to 5 acres get the 4 percent rate.**
13. How do I get a loan for land? (suggest: "land loans sc", "do you need 20 percent down to buy land") **We never answer this. One line: our affiliated lender, BrickWood Mortgage, a separate company, can explain land and construction loans. No rate, no term, no down payment.**
14. Is land a good investment? (suggest) Not answered as an investment claim. The page states asking prices and what the land allows.
