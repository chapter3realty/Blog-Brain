# Fact ledger: /buyers/relocating/myrtle-beach-vs-charleston/

Researched 2026-11-13. **Every row is unverified until an agent that did not research it checks it** (CLAUDE.md). Raw files and the scripts are in `<wt>/data/2026-11-d/myrtle-beach-vs-charleston/` (`<wt>` = `scratchpad/wt-139`): `pull.sh` fetches everything into `raw/`, `analyze.py` writes `findings.json`. Charts are to be drawn from `findings.json`, never typed.

Geography. "Myrtle Beach metro" is the Census Myrtle Beach-Conway-North Myrtle Beach, SC metro area (CBSA 34820), which is all of Horry County. "Charleston metro" is Charleston-North Charleston, SC (CBSA 16700): Berkeley, Charleston and Dorchester counties. Zillow names the same two metros "Myrtle Beach, SC" and "Charleston, SC". Compare metro with metro, or county with county; never mix them in one sentence.

ACS = U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 1-year estimates, 2025 (released September 2026). Table pages (person-readable): `https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT1Y2025.<table>?g=310XX00US34820,16700`. API used: `https://data.census.gov/api/access/data/table?id=ACSDT1Y2025.<table>&g=<geo>`.
