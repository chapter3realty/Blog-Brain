## The reader and the decision

A buyer choosing between the two South Carolina coast metros before a move, usually from out of state. They know Charleston by name. They are deciding where to buy, and they want to know what the same money buys, what owning costs each year, how close the beach is, how hot the summer is, and whether they can earn a living. Kind of page: a comparison of two options (CRAFT 3): verdict by situation, a side-by-side table, then one section per difference with its deciding fact.

## The answer in two sentences

The typical home in the Myrtle Beach area is worth about $98,000 less than in the Charleston area, the median property tax bill is about $750 a year lower, there are more than 360 public beach accesses, and about a third as many 90-degree days. Charleston has about twice the jobs and pays more, so the saving goes furthest when your income or savings come with you.
