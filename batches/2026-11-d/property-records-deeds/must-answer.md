## The must-answers, ranked

1. **Where is the Horry County deed search, and is it free?** AcclaimWeb at acclaimweb.horrycounty.org. Free to search; accept the disclaimer; no account. Button on the first screen.
2. **How do I search Horry County deeds by name?** Last name first, no punctuation ("SMITH JOHN"), at least 3 letters, both buyer and seller sides, back to 1984. A company is spelled as recorded without periods ("D R HORTON INC").
3. **Can I see the deed itself online?** No. Horry County shows the index online, not the document. The document is viewed and printed in Conway, ordered by mail ($8 for the first 4 pages), or we pull it. **This is the deciding fact** (rows 10 and 13) and the reason the offer is worth taking.
4. **How do I search by address?** No address box in the deed index. Address to owner and PIN on the parcel map, then name or book/page. Link gis-map and the hub's lookup tool.
5. **How do I find a mortgage, a satisfaction, a lien, a plat or an easement?** Book type and document type filters; the exact type names (MORTGAGE SATISFACTION (011), MECHANICS LIEN (015), EASEMENT (062), PLAT - LEGAL SIZE (006), RESTRICTIONS (082), LIS PENDENS DEED (135)). What is not in it: judgments, property tax, HOA lien details.
6. **How far back do Horry County land records go online?** Name search from 1984 (county's words). Index entries reach about 1980 by book and page; earlier books show as non-indexed pages and are searched by book and page or on paper in Conway.
7. **How do I read a deed?** Grantor (seller), grantee (buyer), consideration (price), legal description (lot, subdivision, plat book), TMS or PIN, derivation (prior deed), deed stamps ($3.70 per $1,000; one line, link net-proceeds). Why a third of deeds show $0 (row 20).
8. **What does a copy cost, and how do I get one?** In person, by mail, certified. Table.
9. **Does Horry County have a property fraud alert?** Yes, the Recording Notification Service, free, email. Georgetown's Property Fraud Alert is free and sends texts too.
10. **Register of Deeds office: phone, hours, address.** One small card.
11. **Georgetown County (Pawleys Island, Litchfield).** Its own search (georgetowndeeds.com), name format, and the one difference that matters: Georgetown shows document images online when it has them; Horry does not.
12. **Is Chapter3 the county?** No. Said plainly near the button and in the office card.

## The deciding fact

Horry County's index is online and free, but the documents are not: "Horry County does not provide Images of Documents to be viewed via the Internet at this time. You must visit the public viewing area located at 1301 Second Avenue, Conway, SC to view and print documents." Re-read on 2026-11-13 (row 10). The page is built on it: the search finds that a document exists, its date, book and page, parties, price and short legal description. Reading the document means Conway, the mail, or us.
