Data folder (website working copy): `data/2026-11-d/palace-resort/`. The spec reads every count, fee, price and chart value from these files:

Sources: Horry County GIS layer 24, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24/query; deed sales from `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json` (AcclaimWeb DEED (001), recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, the window /invest/condo-buildings/ used) and `data/2026-11-n/buyers-builders/raw/deeds-co.json` (2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31).
- `flood.json`: FEMA flood zones under the Palace's parcel footprint (`pull_flood.py`; Horry County GIS layer 19).
- `tax.json`: the county's current assessments, in aggregate (`pull_tax.py`).
- `raw/pages/`: the resort's own pages read 2026-11-13 (palaceresort.com home, accommodations, amenities, faq, waterpark-access, rental-management, contact, groups, myrtle-beach, robots.txt), the CVU (skyscrapercenter.com) record, and a text note on the competitor page (its listings are not saved).

- `raw/google-suggestions-2026-11-13.txt`: demand only.
