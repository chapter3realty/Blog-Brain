## Not used

- MSC search page: "a National Flood Insurance Program's flood insurance policy could cost less than $400 per year". A premium figure; this page states none.
- MSC search page: "5 times more likely to experience flood than a fire". A national figure about Zone X; coastal-insurance owns that point.
- Any listing site by name. The suggestions show buyers expect a listing site to show the zone ("zillow flood zone", "is zillow flood zone accurate"); the page answers that with our own search's numbers and names no company.
- A per-ZIP flood table: FEMA's zones follow ground height, not ZIP codes. "By zip code" is answered in the FAQ with "check the address".
