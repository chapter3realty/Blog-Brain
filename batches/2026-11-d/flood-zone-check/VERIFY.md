| 24 | VERIFIED | Zone X is not in 64.3(b)'s mandatory list, and 4012a(b) applies only in special flood hazard areas. | 25 | CORRECTED | The notice rule verifies for regulated and federal agency lenders: 42 U.S.C. 4104a(a)(1) and (3), with the contents (a warning that the building is in a special flood hazard area, and the insurance requirement). The deadline in the statute is "a reasonable period in advance of the signing of the purchase agreement, lease, or other documents involved in the transaction". The regulation sets it as "within a reasonable time before the completion of the transaction" (12 CFR 22.9(c), OCC; the other bank regulators have the same rule). **"It comes after your offer is accepted" is not in either rule.** It is an inference, and the statute's "purchase agreement" wording points the other way. What is sure: the notice comes from a lender as a condition of the loan, so a buyer gets it only through a loan, and a cash buyer gets none. Earlier ledgers (CM 21, FLD 7) say "before signing" without the purchase-agreement words; that framing is fine, but do not add a time order with the offer. | If the house is in the high-risk zone, a regulated lender must tell you in writing, a reasonable time before the loan closes. The notice comes with the loan, so a cash buyer gets none [42 U.S.C. 4104a(a); 12 CFR 22.9]. |

| 26 | CORRECTED (wording) | 44 CFR 60.3(b)(5) verifies, with its condition: "Where base flood elevation data are utilized, within Zone A ... The join is sound. 59.1 names "the elevation certificate, other certifications, or other evidence of compliance required in § 60.3(b)(5)".
