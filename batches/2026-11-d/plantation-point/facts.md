Definitions used in every row:
- **Plantation Point** = the county parcels whose legal description starts with a Plantation Point section name (PLANTATION PT, PLANTATION POINT, PLNTN PT ..., PLNTN POINT ADD 4 PH I), plus the one parcel owned by PLANTATION POINT HOA, **minus The Landing**. 368 parcels.

- **House** = a parcel with a county building value over $0.
- **Last 12 months** = deeds recorded 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31.
