## The reader and the decision

A buyer who typed "plantation point myrtle beach", usually after seeing one house. Likely a move-up or relocating buyer with $700,000 to $2 million, or a buyer who wants the location for less (The Landing). They want to know what the place is, what is special, what the homes really sell for, and whether there is an HOA. Worried about: an HOA, flood, the "Myrtle Beach" address being outside the city. Next question: "show me what is for sale."

Decision: **is Plantation Point the neighborhood to tour, and which lot (waterway, fairway or pool)?**

## The answer in two sentences

Plantation Point is 352 houses on lots of about half an acre between the Intracoastal Waterway and the Myrtlewood golf courses, west of the US 17 Bypass at 38th Avenue North and outside the city limits; half the houses have an in-ground pool. Houses sold for about $785,000 in the past year, no home for sale lists a monthly HOA fee, and The Landing next door sells smaller houses for about $280,000.
