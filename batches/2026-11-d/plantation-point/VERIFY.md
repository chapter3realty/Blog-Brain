## A. The outline is wrong: The Landing is missing 44 houses

`pull_county.py` keeps a parcel only when its legal description starts with one of six spellings. The county spells The Landing at least nine other ways: "THE LANDING PLANT" (34 parcels), "THE LANDING AT" (2), "LAND AT PLANT PT" (2), "LANDING PLANTATION", "THE LANDING @ PLANT PT", "LANDING PLANT POINT", "LANDING PLANT PT", "THE LANDING LT #6571" and "LANDING L PLAN PT" (1 each). That makes 44 houses on Plantation Dr (19), Landing Rd (14), Shipyard Rd (8) and Lighthouse Way (3). All 44 are land use 101, in the same SF 6 zoning areas as the other 95, and in tax district 800. After I added them, the SF 6 areas hold no other parcel. **The Landing has 139 houses, not 95.**

Three more fixes:

- "PLANTATION; LT 2 BL B" is a house on Arundel in the SF 10 area. It belongs in Plantation Point (+1 house).
- "PLANTATION POINT; UTILITY LT" (land use 123) has a building value but only an outbuilding and a commercial footprint, no house. It is not a house (-1 house). The other land use 123 parcel, LOT 47 BL J, is a house and stays.
- "PLNTN POINT ADD 4 PH I; PT POND 2" is part of a pond, not an empty lot (-1 empty lot, -1 waterway lot, -1 lot touching the AE zone). It also means there is at least one pond in the community (gap 3).

Corrected outline: Plantation Point 367 parcels (352 houses, 14 empty lots, the HOA's common area). The Landing 139 parcels, all houses. All 506 are outside the Myrtle Beach town limits, in tax district 800, with "Myrtle Beach" as the mail city.

**For the writer:** fix the section regex in `pull_county.py` (the where clause and SECT need `LANDING PLANT`, `LAND AT PLANT`, `LANDING L PLAN`, `THE LANDING AT;`, `THE LANDING @`, `THE LANDING LT`, `LANDING PLANTATION` and `^PLANTATION;`). In `analyze.py`, require a Residential footprint for a house and leave "POND" parcels out of the empty lots. Then re-run the line. Until then, use the corrected numbers below. `pull_listings.py` draws The Landing's outline from the same cache. Re-run, it still finds 1 Landing listing and 6 in Plantation Point.
