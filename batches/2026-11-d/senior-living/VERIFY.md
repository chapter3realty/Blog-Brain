## For the writer and the lead

- **Row 19: the CCRC count is 33, not 34.** `build.py` reads the workbook from `min_row=5`. Row 5 is the header row ("Licensee", "Organization" ...), so `ccrc.statewide` comes out as 34. The fix is `min_row=6`. I did not edit the script (the verifier edits only the ledger). Whoever owns the data folder should fix it and re-run before any chart or line uses `ccrc.statewide`.
- **Row 11: "the nearest homes are in Little River" is wrong.** That is true only for the north end of North Myrtle Beach. At the south end, two Myrtle Beach homes are about as close.
- **Row 30: do not print** the beds-per-1,000-seniors ratio.
- **Row 33** brought back wording that the Surfside Beach verifier corrected (lesson B30).
- **Source link, rows 8 to 14 and 28:** the quotes "provides the most up-to-date information" and "Licensed health care facilities and services available in the map" are not at `dph.sc.gov/public/data-maps-reports/find-health-facility`. That page only embeds the map. They are at https://dph.sc.gov/professionals/healthcare-quality/find-facility. Link to that page, or to both.
- **Sibling check:** /buyers/55-plus-communities/ says Brightwater has "assisted living, memory care, and skilled nursing on the same grounds". DPH lists Brightwater Assisted Living (Alzheimer Unit: Y) and Brightwater Skilled Nursing Center, both in 29579. That is consistent with this ledger. The hub's entrance fee figures have no verified row. This page rightly does not repeat them.
