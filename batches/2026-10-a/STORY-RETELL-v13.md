Method: I read each story once, out loud, at normal speed, on the 390 px preview, with no rereading. Then I covered it and wrote what happened in one sentence. Each retell is compared with the writer's retell in STORY-SIMPLE.md.

## Notes

- **Grande Dunes.** The story has three numbers: "two grandsons", "90 nights" and "two weeks". The kit counts only digits, so it lets this through. The reader never adds or compares any of them, and the test is about counting, so it passes. If the owner still finds it hard, cut "two" from "two grandsons" first.
- **Seasons.** "The government's flood map" and "the high-risk flood area" are plain enough to read once. The section above the story explains the flood zone, so the story does not have to.
- All four use the **Example:** label and the present tense. In all four, the agent acts before the offer or the contract, using a service the story bank records. No story has a price or an "if", and none has a second deadline.
