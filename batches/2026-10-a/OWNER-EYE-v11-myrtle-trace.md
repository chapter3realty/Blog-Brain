## What I would send back

### 1. The date in the sources line, for the third time

- "Read October 5, 2026." is still in the sources line. The date is at the top. Take it out.
- This line does not come from the page. `tools/mkpage.js` in the website repo writes `Read ${shown}.` into every sources line. Fix it there, with a patch in `website-patches/`, so it leaves every page. Do this before the page goes to the owner. He has asked twice.

### 2. "Its riverwalk"

- "Main Street in Conway is about 10 minutes away by car. Its riverwalk is a wooden boardwalk along the water, shaded by trees." "Its" reads as Main Street's riverwalk.
- Say "Conway's riverwalk is a wooden boardwalk along the water, shaded by trees."

### 3. A card that says nothing

- Bocce and shuffleboard: "Leagues, each with its own schedule." That tells me nothing.
- Say "A bocce league and a shuffleboard league you can join." (row 80).

### 4. The walkways are still in the wrong place

- "There is also line dancing, a coffee group and a craft fair. There are wide walkways between the houses." It is its own sentence now, but it still sits in the events paragraph, and it does not say what the walkways are for.
- Move it to the homes section, after the curving streets: "Wide paths between the houses are open to every resident." (row 46). If that does not fit, cut it. A buyer would not miss it.

### 5. Smaller things

- Month lead: "you can play bingo, swim, fish and join a league, all on the neighborhood's calendar." Swimming and fishing are not on a calendar. Say "In a normal month you can play bingo, swim, fish and play in a league. The rest is on the neighborhood's calendar."
- "The houses are on curving streets among tall pines and ponds". No row in the ledger says pines. If nothing says pines, say "among trees and ponds".
- Insurance CTA: "An agent at Chapter3 gets you a homeowners insurance quote". We do not write the quote. Say "helps you get", the way the other three pages say it.
- "Trucks go in the garage." Say "Park your truck in the garage."
- FAQ: "Every fish goes back in the water." Say "You let every fish go." The card already says it that way.
- The FAQ fee answer leaves out the gates, which the cost section lists. Make the two lists the same.
- Not a send-back, still my preference: the orange line is still "A 55+ neighborhood close to the hospital." I would rather it say what living there is like. The hospital is already in the icon row.

## What did the page forget that a buyer types into Google?

- **Is Myrtle Trace South part of Myrtle Trace?** A buyer who searches for Myrtle Trace homes will see Myrtle Trace South and Myrtle Trace Grande listings. They have their own HOAs, fees and rules. An earlier version said so (REVIEW.md, M9); this one does not. Add one FAQ: "Is Myrtle Trace South part of Myrtle Trace? No. Myrtle Trace South and Myrtle Trace Grande are separate neighborhoods, each with its own HOA. The fee and rules on this page are for Myrtle Trace." (rows 68 and 69).
- **When does the pool open?** The card says it closes at the start of October. If we know the spring opening, add it. If not, leave it out, as the page does.
- **Can I play golf at Burning Ridge?** Owner question. Leave it out until he answers.
- Golf carts and guest stays: the ledger found no rule. Leave them out, as the page does.
- Adult children: "The others in the home can be any age" answers it.
- How far is Conway: answered now. Good.
