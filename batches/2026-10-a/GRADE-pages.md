# Grade: the four 55+ community pages, version 7

Judge: an outside reader who did not write or review these pages. Date: 2026-10-11.

Read: the built previews in the scratchpad (`draft/chapter3realty/buyers/55-plus-communities/<slug>/index.html`), the phone (390 px) and desktop (1280 px) screenshots, `CRAFT.md`, `voice/RULES.md`, `PLAN.md`, `RETRO.md`, and the fact ledgers where a claim looked wrong.

Compared with: what a buyer finds today for the searches in the brief. Google results for these names are mostly listing pages (agent IDX sites, Compass, Coldwell Banker, Century 21) and 55places.com. I read the four 55places community pages in full. Delwebb.com allows reading but its pages return only menus to a fetch. Reddit and homeguidemyrtlebeach.com block this agent in robots.txt, so I did not read them.

## The short verdict

The pages are careful, plain and legally clean. They are not yet better than what ranks. A buyer who searches "Del Webb North Myrtle Beach HOA fees" gets the number from any listing page and from 55places in one line. Our page never gives it. Three of the four pages fail the single most common question a buyer asks.

Our real advantage is facts nobody else prints: the recorded age rule in detail, the flood risk lot by lot, the property tax, the city tax, the one-time fees, the rental minimum. Those facts are on the pages, but they are scattered through long sections and never gathered where a reader or an AI answer can find them.

The photos show beaches and towns, never the community. Every page opens with a beach or river photo and a caption that admits the photo is not the place. Competitors show 14 to 26 photos of the actual clubhouse and streets.

## The ten most important weaknesses, ranked

1. **No monthly HOA dues on three of four pages, and no dues column in the comparison table.** It is the first number every buyer looks for, the RETRO already found it as the top gap, and every competitor prints it. Fix: take the dues from recent closed MLS sales when the export arrives (one week), or from the HOA's current budget or a resale certificate. Write "about $300 a month" with what it covers. Add a dues column to the comparison table. Until then, get the figure by phone from each HOA manager. Do not ship a 55+ page without it.

2. **No photo of any community.** Each page opens with an area photo and a caption that says it is not the place. That breaks P10 and reads as if we have never been there. Fix: an agent drives to each community and photographs the entrance sign, the clubhouse from the street, a typical street and the pool where it can be seen from a public spot. Put the clubhouse photo in the amenities section and a street photo in the homes section. Cut the decorative top photo.

3. **The homes section is thin on every page, and wrong-sized on one.** No size range, bedroom range, year built or lot type in one place. Del Webb NMB says the smallest design is about 2,200 square feet, which tells a $450,000 buyer to leave. Fix: a short "Homes at a glance" list on each page: sizes from about X to Y square feet, 2 to 4 bedrooms, built from year to year, garage, one story. On Del Webb NMB say "Pulte's current plans start near 2,200 square feet; older homes here start near 1,350."

4. **Seasons is missing its basics.** Lawn care and what the dues include, the gate, builder and years, sizes, the lifestyle director, the clubs, the clubhouse size. Fix: one research pass on Seasons alone. The owner said he does not know if the gate works: an agent can drive there in an afternoon and look. Name the clubs from the HOA calendar the way the Myrtle Trace page does.

5. **No key facts box.** The PLAN asked for one table of key facts. The facts that set us apart are spread over eight sections. Fix: under the short answer, one table: age rule, homes, years built, monthly dues, what dues include, one-time fee, property tax, city tax yes or no, flood insurance needed, gated, rentals, pets. This is the block a 62-year-old scans and an AI answer quotes.

6. **No link to the homes for sale.** Every competitor shows current listings, and the site has a "Search listings" button in the header. Fix: in the price section and the final CTA, "See the homes for sale in Myrtle Trace now", linked to the listing search filtered to that subdivision. Offer listing alerts by email.

7. **Missing amenities that people search for.** Pickleball and tennis on Del Webb NMB, the neighborhood's own clubhouse at Grande Dunes, the public Resort Course at Grande Dunes. Fix: list every amenity the HOA or builder names, in the existing icon cards. Correct the Grande Dunes golf FAQ: one private club and one public course.

8. **The invented "Example" stories cost more than they give.** All four use the same shape: N years in a northern city, one wish, the purchase, a scene on the first day ("coffee to the beach at seven", "a name tag on her sweater"). Each fills a phone screen and adds no fact. Readers who notice they are invented trust the page less, and the matching shape is the template the owner asked us to stop using. Fix: keep each to two sentences that carry the advice, or cut it. On Seasons, move the real Blackmoor story up to the top half and give it the space.

9. **The calls to action are many and vague.** Four or five per page, with labels that change each time ("Speak to an expert", "Let us make it simple", "Talk to a specialized agent", "Call to learn more"), then a second generic site CTA under the page CTA. The only offer is to "read the HOA documents with you". Phone only, office hours only. Fix: one offer per page that fills the page's own gap, for example "We will send you the current HOA fee, the rules and the last 10 sale prices in Myrtle Trace." Give it a short form and the phone number. Use the same button label on all four pages.

10. **Drive times are no-traffic estimates stated as plain facts, and the caveat is in the sources line.** "Beach about 15 minutes" from Myrtle Trace and "about 10" from Seasons will run longer in summer. Fix: give a range ("15 to 25 minutes, longer in summer traffic") in the copy, and say which beach.

### Also worth fixing

- Every heading repeats the community's full name. "What are the new homes like in Del Webb North Myrtle Beach?" fifteen times reads as written for a machine. Use the full name in the H1, the short answer and the FAQ, and "here" or the short name elsewhere.
- Small text: quick-fact cards and icon cards are about 14 px, some labels 11 px, map labels smaller. CRAFT says 18 px on a phone.
- On desktop the first screen holds only the title block; the short answer is below the fold, and the text column uses half the width.
- The same fact appears two or three times per page (flood insurance in the cost section and the FAQ; the Ocean Club membership three times on Grande Dunes).
- Grande Dunes shows $665,000 in the body and $630,000 in the table for the same community. Explain the table figure or make them agree.

## How to tell the next version is better

Search each name with "HOA fees", "gated", "floor plans" and "pickleball". If the page answers each one in its first screen of that section, with a number, it beats 55places. Today it does that for Myrtle Trace only.
