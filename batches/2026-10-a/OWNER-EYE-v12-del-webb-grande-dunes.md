## What I would send back

### 1. "For the villas in the section called the Heel Tract, the HOA takes care of the roofs, gutters and outside paint."

- Now it names the section, but "the Heel Tract" is a county land name. No buyer knows it, and no buyer can tell from it whether the villa they like is in it. It is the same problem as "Pulte and its three plans".
- The ledger cannot say "most villas" yet (row 26 covers the Heel Tract; later villa phases are unconfirmed). So do not say "most".
- Want: "For some villas, the HOA takes care of the roofs, gutters and outside paint. Those owners pay a little more for it." The CTA right under it already says an agent finds out what the HOA keeps up on the home you like. That answers "is mine one of them?"
