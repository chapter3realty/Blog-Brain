How to open the sources:

- **Register of Deeds (AcclaimWeb).** Open https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/ and press "I accept the conditions above." Then use Search > Name (for example SEASONS AT PRINCE, MBSC SEASONS, LEVITT AND SONS OF HORRY) or Search > Book/Page (Book type D or P). A details link such as `https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/details/documentdetails/2201762/undefined/1/200` works in the same browser session after the disclaimer. The index is public. The document images are not: "Horry County does not provide Images of Documents to be viewed via the Internet at this time."
- **The charter text.** The only readable copy found is the association's own posted replica, archived from seasons55.com: https://web.archive.org/web/20170606010636/http://www.seasons55.com/editor_upload/File/Community%20Management/Policies%20%26%20Rules/communitycharter2.pdf (115 pages, file dated 2017-03-07). Page numbers below are the replica's "Page \| N" footers. Search the PDF for the quoted words.
- **County GIS and FEMA.** The URLs below are ArcGIS REST queries. Each returns JSON in a browser.
- **Archived builder pages.** web.archive.org links. The archive reset some connections on 2026-10-05; a retry worked.
- **Distances.** Driving miles come from the public OSRM router (OpenStreetMap roads). The response gives `"distance"` in meters; miles = meters / 1609.344. Straight-line miles are haversine distances (Earth radius 3,958.76 miles) between the same two points.
- **Origin point for distances:** the county address point for 130 Grand Cypress Way (lon -79.07094, lat 33.586369), on the amenity parcel (row 15).
