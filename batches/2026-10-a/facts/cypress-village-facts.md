# Fact ledger: Cypress Village, Little River (55+ community page)

Batch 2026-10-a. The page URL is not assigned yet. The researcher opened every source below on 2026-10-05.

One row per claim. **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** The researcher filled columns 1 to 7. The verifier fills 8 and 9.

## Read this first

**No age restriction was found for Cypress Village.** The recorded declaration (2015), its first amendment (2016) and the association's bylaws contain no age rule (rows 1 to 3). In 2020 the builder described Cypress Village as "a unique place to live for people of all ages" (row 4). The association's recorded pool rules call the pool area a "family recreational area" (row 5). Two later rule sets were recorded, in 2019 (21 pages) and on 2026-09-24 (59 pages), but the county does not show document images online, so they were not read (row 6). Until someone reads them or the association confirms an age rule in writing, this page should not call Cypress Village age-restricted, and the hub's 55+ listing is unsupported. The owner decides whether the page goes ahead (Questions, item 1).

## Method notes for the verifier

- **Reference point.** The Census geocode of 100 Zostera Drive (lon -78.625036, lat 33.874983), the address Coastal Association Management lists for Cypress Village (row 9). The county shows 100 Zostera Drive as Phase 1, Lot 29, a residential parcel (row 10). The association's amenity parcel is PIN 31205010350 (row 36).
- **Address points.** From the county Addresses layer (HorryCountyGISApp layer 22), for every parcel whose county legal description contains "CYPRESS VILLAGE". That gives 427 address points.
- **County GIS.** HorryCountyGISApp MapServer: https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 17 Highways, 19 Flood Zones, 22 Addresses, 24 Parcels, 30 Zoning). Municipal limits: https://gisportal.horrycounty.org/server/rest/services/OpenData/Municipalities/MapServer/0
- **Deed index.** Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb: https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/ . Accept the disclaimer. Book/Page search, or Name search ("CYPRESS VILLAGE HOMEOWNERS", "CYPRESS VILLAGE DEV"). Index rows are quoted field by field.
- **FEMA.** hazards.fema.gov refused curl from this environment (TLS error). The FEMA rows were opened with WebFetch. Each URL is a point query that opens in a browser.
- **Distances.** Driving miles come from the public OSRM router (router.project-osrm.org), shortest route on its road model, no traffic. Straight-line miles use the haversine formula. Both start at the reference point.
- **Owner names.** The county parcel layer shows owner names. This ledger names organizations only, never private owners.
- **Scanned PDFs.** The declaration, amendment, bylaws and pool rules are scans. Quotes were read from the page image; the PDF page is named.
- **Builder pages.** The two mungo.com pages are about 42 MB each and exceed WebFetch's limit. Open them with curl (compressed) and search the text for "Cypress Village".
- **Name confusion.** cypressvillage.org is a different community in Cypress, California (Not verified, item 7). Search results for "Cypress Village 55" are mostly other communities (Search check).
