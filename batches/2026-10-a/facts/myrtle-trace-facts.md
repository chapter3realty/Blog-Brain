## Method notes for the verifier

- **Reference point.** The Census geocode of the HOA's address, 101 Myrtle Trace Drive: lon -78.996618, lat 33.778502. It falls on the county parcel for the community's streets (PIN 40000000023), beside the HOA's recreational parcel (PIN 40003010085).
- **Address points.** From the county Addresses layer (HorryCountyGISApp layer 22), for every parcel whose county legal description contains "MYRTLE TRACE" and not "SOUTH" or "GRAND". That gives 519 address points.
- **County GIS.** HorryCountyGISApp MapServer: https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 17 Highways, 19 Flood Zones, 22 Addresses, 24 Parcels, 30 Zoning). Municipal limits: https://gisportal.horrycounty.org/server/rest/services/OpenData/Municipalities/MapServer/0
- **FEMA.** hazards.fema.gov refused curl from this environment (TLS error). The FEMA rows were opened with WebFetch. Each URL is a point query that opens in a browser.
- **Distances.** Driving miles come from the public OSRM router (router.project-osrm.org), shortest route on its road model, no traffic. Straight-line miles use the haversine formula. Both start at the reference point.
- **Owner names.** The county parcel layer shows owner names. This ledger names organizations only, never private owners.
- **Covenants.** The HOA posts an OCR copy of its covenants with a disclaimer (row 2). The Register of Deeds index is online, but its document images are not (Not verified, item 1). The recorded originals were not read.
- **Scanned PDFs.** Quotes from scanned pages were read from the page image. The page is named.
- **Name confusion.** Three associations carry the name: Myrtle Trace (this page), Myrtle Trace South and Myrtle Trace Grande (rows 68 and 69). The agent site myrtletrace.com is not the HOA. The HOA's site is myrtletracesc.org.

## Not verified

1. **Recorded originals.** The deed index is online, but the images are not. Trying https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/Image/DocumentPdfAllPages/3958708 (the 1983 declaration) returns: "Horry County does not provide Images of Documents to be viewed via the Internet at this time. You must visit the public viewing area located at 1301 Second Avenue, Conway, SC to view and print documents." So the age rule rests on the HOA's OCR copy, not the recorded text.
2. **The 1993 amendment** (Book 1651, Page 540): contents not read, for the same reason. Whether it is the 50-to-55 change is unconfirmed.
3. **Golf carts.** No rule found in the covenants, Board/General Policies, ARC Guidelines, Property Committee Guidelines, pool rules, clubhouse rules, or the 2025 and 2026 board minutes.
4. **Guest stays.** No limit on how long a guest under 55 may stay was found in the same documents. The pool rules say children under 16 must be with "the resident they are staying with or a responsible adult"; that is a pool rule, not a stay limit.
5. **"Average near $305,000."** No primary source. Waits for MLS.
6. **"2005" as the last year built.** Found only on 55places. The county's public GIS layers have no year-built field. The county Land Records app, which has assessor detail, requires a login: https://www.horrycountysc.gov/apps/LandRecords/ (redirects to Account/Login).
7. **Lawn care in the dues.** An agent page (search result 5) says dues include "lawn mowing, and landscaping". The covenant makes each owner responsible for the lot and grass (row 59), and the HOA's list of what dues cover has no lawn care (row 34). Do not repeat the agent claim.
