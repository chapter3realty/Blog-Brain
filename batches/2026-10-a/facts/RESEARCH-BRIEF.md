# Research brief: one fact ledger per 55+ community

Batch 2026-10-a. Six pages, one per major age-restricted community on the Grand Strand. Each page answers the questions a buyer types about that one community. The researcher fills a ledger; a different agent verifies it; the writer uses verified rows only.

## Rules that shape what may be researched and written

- **Observable, dated facts only about a named community, HOA or builder.** Never a conclusion ("well run", "the best", "poorly built"). Website CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 5 and PLAYBOOK A20: defamation and trade-libel exposure.
- **Fair housing.** Describe property, rules and geography. Never who lives there, who it suits, or what kind of people buy. The age rule itself is a recorded property rule and may be described exactly (federal Housing for Older Persons Act, 42 USC 3607(b); HUD rules at 24 CFR 100.304 to 100.307).
- **Primary sources only.** For a community's own product facts, the primary source is its builder's or HOA's own site, attributed ("Pulte lists...", "the association's site says..."). The age rule's primary source is the recorded declaration (Horry County Register of Deeds) or the HOA's posted governing documents. Taxes, flood zones and parcels come from the county, the auditor and FEMA. Listing sites, 55places, realtor blogs and search summaries only help find a primary source; they are never cited.
- **Verbatim quotes.** A calculation shows its inputs and arithmetic in the quote column. A distance says how it was measured.

## Questions each ledger answers

1. Is it age-restricted, and exactly what does the rule say: who must be 55 or older, the minimum age of other residents, how long a younger guest may stay.
2. Where it is: main entrance address, town, inside city limits or unincorporated Horry County, tax district.
3. Size and history: builder or builders, years built, homes planned and built, acreage.
4. Homes: types (single-family, villa, attached), square footage range, bedrooms; current new-home base prices if the builder still sells, dated.
5. HOA: dues and how often, what the dues cover, one-time fees at purchase (capital contribution, initiation, transfer), management company.
6. Amenities: clubhouse, pools, courts, fitness, trails, golf, staff.
7. Rules a buyer asks about: renting the home (minimum lease, short-term rentals), golf carts, fences, pets.
8. Flood zone or zones (FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer), and whether the community sits east or west of US 17 or Bypass 17 (the wind pool line in Horry County, SC Code 38-75-310).
9. Distances to the nearest beach access, the nearest hospital, and Myrtle Beach International Airport, with the method.
10. Anything else dated and observable a buyer would ask (homes left to build, a phase opening, a change to dues).

## Also in each ledger

- **Hub check:** each claim the live /buyers/55-plus-communities/ page makes about this community, marked confirmed, different (with the source's figure) or unverifiable.
- **Waiting for MLS:** resale price range, median price, days on market, price per square foot. The owner's MLS access starts in about a week.
- **Search check:** the top five results for the community's name plus "55", what each answers, and what a buyer asks that none of them answers.
- **Not verified:** anything found only on a listing site or blog, with what was tried.
