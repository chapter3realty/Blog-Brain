## Del Webb North Myrtle Beach

Ledger: `facts/del-webb-north-myrtle-beach-facts.md`.

Pros:

1. The beach is about a mile away, about 4 minutes by car. Rows 41, 62.
2. The homes are new, built from 2022 on, and Pulte says its 10-year structural warranty passes to the next owner. Row 73 (listings), row 36 (say "Pulte says").
3. The clubhouse has an indoor lap pool, an outdoor pool, a fitness center and a craft room, and there is a full-time lifestyle director. Rows 32, 69, 76 (say "Pulte lists").
4. The homes are not in a flood zone, so a lender will not require flood insurance. Rows 37, 68.
5. Extra: a city community center with indoor pickleball is about a quarter mile from the HOA office. Rows 79, 80.

Cons:

1. Building is still going on. Pulte sold about 120 new homes here in the last 12 months. Row 59 (count), row 26 (homes still for sale).
2. The lots are narrow, 42 to 66 feet wide. Row 18.
3. The HOA fee on listings has gone up each year: mostly $267 a month on listings from 2022 to 2024, $285 in 2025 and $315 in 2026. Row 71 (listings). Do not say the HOA raised its dues; say what listings show.
4. A golf cart may cross US 17 but may not drive along it, and US 17 lies between the homes and the beach. Rows 40, 93, 94. Care: Pulte says "Ride your golf cart to the beach" (row 47). No cart route that only crosses US 17 has been checked (row 94 note), so do not say carts cannot reach the beach.
5. Extra: the airport is about 35 minutes away. Row 65.

## Del Webb at Grande Dunes

Ledger: `facts/del-webb-grande-dunes-facts.md`.

Pros:

1. Owners get a household membership to the Grande Dunes Ocean Club, a private beach club with a large pool and beach access. It is not free: every owner pays a mandatory beach club fee. Rows 91, 32. Row 32 says "Beach Club" and row 91 "Ocean Club"; no source says they are the same, so keep the fee sentence general.
2. Daily needs are close: a grocery store and the beach are about 4 minutes away, and Grand Strand Medical Center is about 6 minutes. Rows 84, 85, 86.
3. The amenity center is on the Intracoastal Waterway and has a day dock for boats. Rows 41, 45.
4. The homes are not in a flood zone, so a lender will not require flood insurance. Rows 60, 90.
5. Extra: Pulte has finished selling new homes here. Row 99. Do not say nothing more will be built (Pulte still owns one parcel marked future development).

Cons:

1. The HOA fee is the highest of the four: listings show about $400 a month for a house and about $445 for a villa. At Seasons listings show about $340, at Del Webb North Myrtle Beach about $315, and at Myrtle Trace the HOA charges $95. Rows 95 (headline fees stand, per its note) and 96 (listings); Del Webb North Myrtle Beach row 71; Seasons row 87; Myrtle Trace row 30.
2. Renters must sign for at least 12 months, and a home can be rented out only once a year. Row 50 (use its reworded form).
3. Mailboxes are at the amenity center, not at the house. Row 54.
4. Yard fences must be 4-foot black aluminum and approved first, and sheds and dog runs are not allowed. Rows 49, 56.
5. Extra: the beach club is a drive, about 8 minutes. Row 106.
6. Extra: guests under 19 may stay no more than 90 nights in any 12 months. Row 2.

## Myrtle Trace

Ledger: `facts/myrtle-trace-facts.md`.

Pros:

1. The HOA fee is low, $95 a month, and pays for the pool, the clubhouse, the gate, the roads and the ponds. Rows 30, 34.
2. The hospital is about 3 minutes away by car, and so is a Walmart Supercenter. Rows 87, 86.
3. There is a lot to do: the HOA's calendar lists about 70 activities a month, such as game nights, bingo, a Labor Day picnic and a craft fair. Rows 78, 79, 80.
4. Homes cost less than in the newer 55+ neighborhoods: the middle sale in the last 12 months was about $300,000. Row 81. Price goes after the home and the area (RULES PLAIN-7).
5. Extra: the homes are not in a flood zone. Rows 60, 91.

Cons:

1. The beach is a drive, about 15 minutes. Row 85.
2. The homes are older; most were built from 1984 to 1998. Row 94 (new, listings). It re-files row 92 with the wording its verifier gave.
3. Only the back entrance has gates; the front entrance is open at all times. Row 16.
4. Fences between yards and along the front are not allowed, and neither are sheds or private pools. Rows 54, 56.
5. Owners mow their own grass and keep up the outside of the house. Row 59.
6. Extra: there are no sidewalks. Row 29.
7. Extra: renters must sign for at least a year, and at least one tenant must be 55 or older. Rows 51, 8.

## Seasons at Prince Creek West

Ledger: `facts/seasons-at-prince-creek-west-facts.md`.

Pros:

1. The main entrance has a gate with a call box. Rows 50 and 52 (builder 2013, charter), rows 92 and 93 (new, listings). Today's gate rests on listings only; say "listings show" or let the owner confirm (owner question 3). Do not say the gate is staffed (see below).
2. The clubhouse has an indoor pool and an outdoor pool, a fitness center, tennis and bocce. Row 56.
3. Shops and care are close: Publix is about 5 minutes away and the hospital about 8. Rows 85, 78.
4. Most homes are on one level. Row 89 (listings; word it as its note says), row 83.
5. Extra: some listings say the fee includes internet, weekly trash pickup and lawn care. Row 88 (listings; 4 of 8).
6. Extra: the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk is about 10 minutes away. Row 81.

Cons:

1. The beach is a drive, about 10 minutes. Row 76.
2. The homes were built from 2006 to 2016, and the builder sold its last lot in 2016, so every home is a resale. Row 89 (listings), row 23.
3. Some lots touch the flood zone: 45 of the 444 lots have a corner in a high-risk area. The elevation certificate for the house decides. Row 59.
4. Renters must sign a lease of at least one year. Row 42.
5. Visitors must use the main gate on TPC Boulevard; the back entrance off Wilderness Lane is for residents only. Row 90 (new, county roads), rows 93 and 95 (new, listings).
6. Extra: golf carts must be kept in the garage, not on the street. Row 47.
7. Extra: a home may keep two pets at most. Row 45.
8. Extra: fences need approval and must match the community's style. Row 46 (say "must be uniform", per its note).
9. Extra: a buyer pays the association two months of dues at closing, and the road and park association charges a transfer fee of a quarter of one percent of the price. Rows 39, 40. Do not give the $480 cap.

## The other questions in this pass

**Seasons gate.** No primary source says whether the gate is staffed, when, or how guests and deliveries get in. What exists:

- Primary: the builder called Seasons gated in 2013 (row 50). The charter lets the association run entry gates and says a gate is no security guarantee (row 52).
- County (new): two road links, the main one at TPC Boulevard and a second from Bay Hill Court to Wilderness Lane (row 90). A small unnamed building of about 600 square feet stands in the entry road (row 91, context only).
- Listings (new): 22 of 23 listings from 2025 and 2026 mark it gated (row 92). Directions send visitors to the main gate's call box (row 93). Two 2025 listings give guard hours, and they differ: weekdays 8 to 4, or 9 to 5 (row 94). The Wilderness Lane entrance is residents only (row 95).
- Suggested wording until the owner confirms: "Listings show a gate at the main entrance on TPC Boulevard, with a call box for visitors." Leave out "staffed" and any hours.
- Not reachable: the association's site disallows our agent; web.archive.org and Common Crawl reset every connection (Seasons Not verified, items 28 to 30).

- Del Webb North Myrtle Beach: Pulte says "amenity center clubhouse", "Owner's Clubhouse" and "Amenity Center" (row 106, new).
- Del Webb at Grande Dunes: the association's rules and hurricane guide say "the Amenity Center", and its budget lines say "Clubhouse" (row 108, new).
- The page can say "the amenity center (the clubhouse)".

- Myrtle Trace: the HOA's own 2025 gate policy says the back entrance on Myrtle Ridge Road has gates and the front entrance on Burning Ridge Road "is not gated and is always available" (row 16). Any listing that calls Myrtle Trace a gated community is only half right.
