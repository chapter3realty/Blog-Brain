## Del Webb North Myrtle Beach

Ledger: `facts/del-webb-north-myrtle-beach-facts.md`.

Pros:

1. The beach is about a mile away, about 4 minutes by car. Rows 41, 62.
2. The homes are new, built from 2022 on, and Pulte says its 10-year structural warranty passes to the next owner. Row 73 (listings), row 36 (say "Pulte says").
3. The clubhouse has an indoor lap pool, an outdoor pool, a fitness center and a craft room, and there is a full-time lifestyle director. Rows 32, 69, 76 (say "Pulte lists").
4. The homes are not in a flood zone, so a lender will not require flood insurance. Rows 37, 68.
5. Extra: a city community center with indoor pickleball is about a quarter mile from the HOA office. Rows 79, 80.

Cons:

1. Building is still going on. Pulte sold about 120 new homes here in the last 12 months. Row 59 (count), row 26 (homes still for sale).
2. The lots are narrow, 42 to 66 feet wide. Row 18.
3. The HOA fee on listings has gone up each year: mostly $267 a month on listings from 2022 to 2024, $285 in 2025 and $315 in 2026. Row 71 (listings). Do not say the HOA raised its dues; say what listings show.
4. A golf cart may cross US 17 but may not drive along it, and US 17 lies between the homes and the beach. Rows 40, 93, 94. Care: Pulte says "Ride your golf cart to the beach" (row 47). No cart route that only crosses US 17 has been checked (row 94 note), so do not say carts cannot reach the beach.
5. Extra: the airport is about 35 minutes away. Row 65.
