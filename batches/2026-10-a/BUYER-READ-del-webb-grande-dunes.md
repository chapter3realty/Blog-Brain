## Answers

### What did you want to know first? Where did I find it?

First: what does it cost to live here each month, and what do the houses sell for? Dues plus the beach club fee, plus the price range.

- The home price: never. The page uses $500,000 as an example but never says what homes there actually sell for.
- The monthly dues: never. The page says "ask the management company" three times.
- The beach club fee: never.
- Property tax: found in the middle, and it is a clear number.
- The 55+ rule and whether I can rent: first screen. Good.

Second: is it nice, what do I do all day? Pools and clubhouse are on the first screen. Activities, clubs, golf, fitness: never.

Third: is it safe from hurricanes and floods? Flood: found near the bottom. Hurricanes and wind insurance: partly, never a price.

### Three things I liked

1. The Carol and Jim example. It showed what two people my age would pay up front and each year, in order.
2. The page tells me the bad news, like no lifeguards, no boat ramp, no short rentals, and $7,638 tax on a second home. I trust it more for that.
3. The first screen told me the beach club, pools and dock, plus that lawn care is in the dues and a hospital is close.

### Questions I still have, ranked

1. What are the monthly dues and the beach club fee, added together, for a house and for a villa?
2. What do homes sell for there right now, and what does a villa cost against a house?
3. What does homeowners and wind insurance cost, not just flood?
4. What is a villa? Is it attached to another home?
5. Which of the 2017 plans (tennis, bocce, pickleball) were built?
6. What is there to do? Clubs, events, fitness, golf, restaurants, shopping.
7. How far is the beach, and how far is the airport?
8. How much is the fine if I break a rule, and who is on the board?
9. Do I lose any Ohio benefit by moving, and how does South Carolina tax retirement income?
10. What is in the January 2025 rules that I cannot read?
11. Is the community finished, or still building? Are new homes for sale from the builder?

### Would you call this company?

Maybe, because the page told me honestly what I cannot get (short rentals, lifeguards, boat ramp), but I would first want the monthly cost, and the page makes me call to get it.
