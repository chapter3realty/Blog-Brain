  1. Three research agents pulled 266 facts from the builders' and associations' own pages, county records, tax bills and FEMA's flood maps.
  2. Separate agents re-opened every source. They caught 21 wrong facts and set aside 18 they could not re-open. None of those is on a page.
  3. A writer drafted the four pages from the checked facts, with your 69 rules from past edits.
  4. A reviewer who did not write them read every sentence against those rules and listed 75 fixes. All were applied.

Two communities were held out. Cypress Village's recorded declaration has no age rule, and Cresswind's could not be read. See question 1.

Questions only you can answer

  1. Fair housing. Your live 55+ hub lists Cypress Village and Cresswind as 55+, but no recorded age rule has been found for either. Listing an all-ages community as 55+ can read as an age preference in an ad. Take both off the 55+ table until a closing attorney or title company pulls the declarations (Cresswind: Book 3678, Page 31. Cypress Village: Book 5130, Page 293)?
  2. May the hub's old figures be replaced with the checked ones? Myrtle Trace dues $90 become $95. Seasons' 460 homes become 444 lots. Del Webb North Myrtle Beach prices become $585,990 to $704,590. "Premium end" and "value option" come off. The change is ready as one file.
  3. The hub says "Clients of ours made exactly this move": a couple sold in Blackmoor and bought in Seasons. Did it happen? If yes, it goes on the Seasons page. If no, it comes off the hub.
  4. The pages offer "Have us read the HOA documents with you" to every buyer. Do our agents do that for every buyer, or only for investors?
  5. Does the Seasons gate operate today? The charter lets the association run one; nothing current says it does.
  6. Can Tim give one sentence per community, something he has seen or does there? Optional. The site's own check asks for it.
  7. The examples use budgets of $300,000 in Myrtle Trace, $450,000 in Seasons and $500,000 in Grande Dunes. Are those realistic for a resale today? If not, they switch to the MLS medians next week.
