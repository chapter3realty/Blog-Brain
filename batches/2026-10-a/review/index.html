  4. Four new reader agents each read one page as a 62-year-old buyer from Ohio and wrote down every word they did not know and every question they still had. A reviewer checked every fact and rule at the same time and found 63 fixes. All serious and medium ones were applied.
  5. Without MLS or Zillow listings, a researcher pulled every recorded home sale in each community from October 2025 to September 2026 out of the county's deed index. A separate agent re-ran the search and opened 136 deed records; every price matched. The examples now use each community's typical price.
  6. Every check passes again: score 100 on all four, no banned claims, no known-wrong facts, no sentence repeated across the four pages.

What I decided, so you do not have to

  • Monthly dues. No public source gives the amount per home for the two Del Webb communities or Seasons. Each page says what the dues include and that we get the current amount for the buyer. The number goes in when your MLS access arrives.
  • Seasons events. No 2026 source we may read gives a count. The page lists the amenities and makes no claim about events.
  • Zillow. Its rules forbid automated reading of listing pages, so prices come from the county's deed records instead: what homes actually sold for, not asking prices.
  • One Grande Dunes sale left out. A $900,000 sale was recorded under a lot number that does not match its deed. Without proof it is in the community, it is not counted.
  • "In Chapter3's experience" sentences. Kept. Each one is your own words from an earlier answer in the story bank.
