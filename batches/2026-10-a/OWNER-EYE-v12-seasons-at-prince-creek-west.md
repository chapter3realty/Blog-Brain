### 2. The part-time answer is in the wrong order

- "Yes, if you live in Seasons at least six months a year. Under the homeowners association (HOA) rules, one person in the home must be 55 or older."
- Read fast, it sounds like anyone who stays six months is fine, and the 55 rule is a second, separate thing. The short answer has it right: the person who is 55 or older lives there six months or more.
- Want: "Yes, as long as the person who is 55 or older lives in Seasons at least six months a year." Keep the rent line after it.
