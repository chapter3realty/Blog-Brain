## What I would send back

1. **The rental question is still not answered, and the answer now says more than we know.**
   "Under the plan the city approved for the neighborhood in 2020, you can rent your home out for 30 days or more at a time. That is the plan, not the HOA's recorded rule, so ask to see the HOA's recorded rules before you sign."
   - The sentence leads with where the fact came from. That is the "records show" habit again.
   - "you can rent your home out" is a promise. The city's plan only gives a minimum stay. Row 75's limit says not to state that the 30 days is in force today.
   - "ask to see the HOA's recorded rules" means "go find out". I said a page never does that.
   - The fix is the one from last time. Someone gets the recorded rules from the Register of Deeds in Conway (Book 4442, Page 1382, and the 2026 rules at Book 5032, Page 1405). Then the first sentence answers in plain words, for example "Yes, if each renter stays at least 30 days." Until we have them, use row 75's own wording, with no "you can" and no date: "The city's plan for the neighborhood says renters must stay at least 30 days." Leave the offer to check the rules in the CTA, not in the answer.

It does not touch the buyer at all. Row 75 asks us to keep it whenever the 30 days is quoted, so say plainly who it is for: "Only the builder may let people who are thinking of buying stay 2 to 10 nights."
