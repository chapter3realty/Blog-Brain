# Cold retell, version 13 stories (STORY-SIMPLE test 12), 2026-10-11

A fresh reader, given only the four stories and no page, read each once and told it back. The reader stood in for a 66-year-old retired school secretary from Ohio with no real estate background.

| Story | Retold want, problem, fix? | Rating | Where the reader stopped |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grande Dunes | Yes | a little hard | "their plan is about two weeks too long": "Too long for what?" Counted nights in their head. |
| North Myrtle Beach | Yes | easy | "the city's renting rules for the neighborhood": city or neighborhood? |
| Myrtle Trace | Yes | a little hard | Did not know what a dog run is, or why golf course houses cannot have a fence. |
| Seasons | Yes | easy | None that matter. |

The reader also did not know "Chapter3", "Del Webb" or "HOA". On the page, the byline, the short answer and the first use of "homeowners association (HOA)" explain these before the story. A story read alone does not, which is expected.

## Fixes made

- Grande Dunes: the limit in its own sentence, then the comparison. "A grandchild can stay up to 90 nights a year. Their plan is about two weeks more than that."
- Myrtle Trace: "a dog run, a small pen beside the house, is allowed once the HOA approves it."
- Not changed: why the fence rule exists. No verified row gives the reason, so the story does not guess.

## Lesson (added to STORY-SIMPLE)

A comparison needs both sides in plain view. "Too long" with the limit in the same clause made the reader do the subtraction. Name the limit, then say how far over the plan is.
