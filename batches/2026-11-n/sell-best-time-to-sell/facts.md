**Deciding fact (checked first).** Do Horry County's own deed records show a month-to-month pattern that is large and steady enough to build a "best month" page on? Yes, with a limit. In each of five full years (2021 to 2025), March to June held 35 to 42 percent of the year's home resales (an even spread would be 33 percent), and January and February held 12 to 16 percent (even: 17 percent). January or February was the slowest month in four of the five years (row 1). The price pattern is smaller: homes that closed in February sold for a middle price about 4 percent below the months around them, below in all five years; May and June closings were about 2 percent above, in four of five years (row 3). The pattern was weakest in 2025, when October was the busiest month. The page can be written, and its honest answer is "spring and early summer, by a few percent, and the price you ask matters more".

**Method limit the verifier should test first.** The deed index carries a parcel number on nearly every deed only from July 2020 (row 2). Before that the county's index left most parcel numbers blank, so 2019 and early 2020 cannot be matched to homes. The page says "five years" (2021 to 2025), never "since 2019".

**Corrections to the live site found here.**
- The /sell/ hub FAQ says "February through July is historically the strongest selling window ... Peak buyer activity runs March through June ... families want to close before the school year starts." No source. Our data agrees on the months (contracts peak March to May, closings March to June) and disagrees on nothing, but the school-year reason has no source. NEEDS.md asks the lead to point the hub answer at this page and use its numbers.
