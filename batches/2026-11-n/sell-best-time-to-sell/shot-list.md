# Shot list: /sell/best-time-to-sell/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Our own photos once he confirms in writing. No person as the subject, and nothing readable: no phone number, house number, brokerage name or car plate.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Where it goes on the page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The beach in front of the oceanfront towers in Myrtle Beach, any weekday in January or February | A wide view down the beach with the towers on one side and nearly empty sand. Landscape. | Shows the off season. Pairs with a licensed photo of the same beach crowded in late June. | Section "Do tourist season and the school year change the best month?" |
| 2 | An ordinary street in Carolina Forest, Socastee or Conway, in winter | A house with a for-sale sign in the yard. The sign's brokerage name and phone number out of frame or unreadable. Landscape, plus a square crop. | Section 5 answers "Is winter a bad time to sell?" and has no picture of a winter listing. | Section "Is winter a bad time to sell a house here?" (no stand-in now) |
| 3 (optional) | The same kind of street in April or May | The front of a house in spring, trees in leaf and flowers out. No house number. | The share image and the picture beside the short answer ("list in March to close in May or June"). | Short answer and share image (no stand-in now) |
