Short names for sources:
- **CRM**: the site's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?<params>, active MLS listings. Every call has `type=house` and `has=county_horry` unless the row says otherwise. A condo's `pool` flag means the building's pool, so condos are left out (537 Horry condos carry it). `not=pool` = houses whose listing does not say private pool. `not=hoa` and `hoaMax=0` both return the houses listed with no HOA fee.
