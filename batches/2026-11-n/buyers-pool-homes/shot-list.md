# Shot list: /buyers/pool-homes/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Five photos. The page works without them: today it has one public-domain USGS aerial from 2011 (four oceanfront houses with pools in Surfside Beach), our fence drawing, a chart and a table.

For every photo: no person as the subject, no readable house number, plate or business sign, no listing photo. Shoot from a public place, or with the owner's or the HOA's written permission. Send the street or neighborhood with each photo, and confirm in writing that we may use it as our own. Landscape, daylight, the widest shot first.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Where it goes on the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A house with a backyard pool on the ocean side of US 17 in Surfside Beach, Garden City or North Myrtle Beach | The pool and its fence from the yard, the house wall in the back | "Where are the homes for sale with a pool", in place of the 2011 aerial |
| 2 | The gate of a backyard pool fence | Close up: the latch on the pool side near the top of the gate, and the gate swinging out, away from the pool | "Do you need a fence around a pool", beside the drawing (items 3 and 4) |
| 3 | A pool pump, filter and heater next to a pool fence | Wide enough to show the space between the equipment and the outside of the fence | The same section (item 6, the 36-inch clear space) |
| 4 | A neighborhood pool at an amenity center in Carolina Forest, Market Common or a newer Conway neighborhood | The pool, its fence and gate, empty; the posted state permit if it is on the gate | "Should you buy a house with a pool or use a community pool", the shared-pool card |
| 5 | A screened pool enclosure (the screen cage over a backyard pool) | From the yard, the whole cage in frame | The same section, the own-pool card |
