# Photo shot list: /sell/cash-offer/

For the photo partner. One drive, about an hour. Hold the phone sideways for every shot and take each one three times. Shoot only from public roads and sidewalks. Rules: `rules/images.md`.

The page now uses a 2018 flood photo from the National Guard as a stand-in. Any of shots 1 to 3 replaces it.

| # | Shot | Where it goes on the page | Done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A "We buy houses" or "Cash for houses" sign by a road in Horry County. The sign is the subject. No phone number, web address or company name readable: shoot at an angle or from behind the lettering. Also a square crop. | "Who makes cash offers on houses in Myrtle Beach?" | [ ] |
| 2 | An older house in Horry County that plainly needs work: a worn roof, a blue tarp, peeling paint or an overgrown yard. The whole house from the street. No house number, no people, no car plates. If someone is home, ask first. | "When is a cash offer the better choice?" (replaces the flood photo) | [ ] |
| 3 | A renovated house of the same age on a similar street: fresh paint, a new roof. Not the same house as shot 2 unless its owner agrees in writing. | "How much less is a cash offer?" beside the bought-and-resold chart | [ ] |
| 4 | A "For Sale" sign with an "As Is" or "Cash Only" rider, if you see one. No firm name, phone number or house number readable. | "Can you sell a house as is in South Carolina?" | [ ] |
