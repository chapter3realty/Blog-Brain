## Not verified

1. **What share South Carolina courts usually give.** Law-firm pages say SC courts often start near 50/50 for long marriages. That is case law and practice, not in the statute. Not used; the page says the judge weighs 15 factors (row 3) and to ask a family law attorney.
2. **Mixing ("transmutation").** Whether an inherited or premarital house becomes marital when it is retitled to both or paid with joint money is case law. Not researched to a primary source; the page sends this case to an attorney.
3. **Taking selling costs off a buyout price.** Some agreements reduce the buyout by an estimated cost of selling. Practice only; no primary source. Not used.
4. **Who pays the mortgage while the case is open.** The Family Court can address it in a temporary order; we found no statute that says so in plain words (20-3-120 covers alimony and suit money pendente lite only). Not used beyond "both of you still owe the lender" (row 18).
5. **Whether a spouse whose name is not on the deed must sign a sale before a case is filed.** South Carolina abolished dower (we could not open the current Title 21 Chapter 5 page; the Statehouse returned "That chapter could not be found"), and row 7 says a spouse's claim does not bind a buyer until the notice is filed. The page must not say "your spouse can sell without you" or "cannot"; it says what row 7 says and to ask an attorney.
6. **Horry County divorce counts.** SC DPH's Vital and Morbidity Statistics PDFs for 2018 to 2024 returned 404 at the old addresses; the court's annual reports page had no caseload file. Not used.
7. **Court-officer deeds in Horry County.** A pull of COURT ORDER DEED (125) records for October 2025 to September 2026 (347 rows, scratch only) found 332 signed by the Master in Equity (foreclosures) and 2 by the Clerk of Court, one of them a 2026 "FINAL JUDGMENT AND DECREE OF DIVORCE" from another state's court. The reason for each deed is not in the index, so no count of divorce deeds is possible. Not used. Row 6's rule stands without it.
8. **Photos.** Two CC BY-SA Commons photos of the Horry County courthouse were found (PLAN.md); both downloads returned Wikimedia's "Too many requests" page, so neither has been viewed at full size. Not used until viewed.
