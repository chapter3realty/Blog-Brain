1. **A real seller story.** Have you had a seller who fixed something before listing (a roof, the heating and air, a water heater, a crawlspace) and it made the sale go through, or one who did not and a buyer's inspector, insurer or termite report stopped or changed the deal? 2. **Roofs and insurance here, now.** At what roof age do the insurance agents you work with start asking for more papers, a four-point inspection or a roof certification, or refuse to write a policy? Do they ask about any electrical panel or pipe type by name?
3. **A quote before listing.** The story bank says Chapter3 can get a buyer an insurance quote before an offer. Can the page say we can get a seller a quote on their own house before listing, so they know whether the roof is a problem?
4. **What our agents do before listing.** Which of these do we do, so the page can say "we" and not tell the reader to do it: look up the roof's permit date, order a CL-100 before listing, suggest a pre-listing inspection, walk the house with the seller and name what to fix.
5. **The CL-100 in our deals.** Who usually orders and pays for it here, the buyer or the seller? How close to closing do lenders want it dated?
6. **Repair requests.** After a buyer's inspection, what do sellers here most often do: fix it, give a credit, lower the price, or say no? Anything you would always fix, or never fix?
