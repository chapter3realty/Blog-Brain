## The deciding fact (checked first)

The page plans to tell a seller that a buyer's lender and insurer, not the seller's taste, decide which repairs matter. That needed a written rule from at least one lender program and a written inspection form an insurer uses. Both exist. FHA's handbook (4000.1, revised 8/12/2026) says the appraiser must report a roof with "less than two years of remaining life" and condition the loan on a roofer's inspection (row 7). The four-point form insurers use asks for the roof's age, its remaining useful life, the date of the last roofing permit, the electrical panel's brand and age, the age of the heating and air system and water heater, and the pipe type (row 12). And the homes here are at the age those forms look at: half of Horry County's homes were built in 2003 or earlier, and about 1 in 5 were built from 2000 to 2009 (rows 1, 2).
