# Photo shot list: /sell/repairs-before-selling/

For the photo partner. Most of these are close-ups of parts of a house, so they need a homeowner who says yes: the owner's own house, a Chapter3 listing with the seller's written OK, or a friend's house. Never a stranger's yard. Hold the phone sideways for every shot and take each one three times. Rules: `rules/images.md` and `templates/photo-request-email.md` (no faces, no house numbers, no readable plates, no brand name or logo as the subject).

Most wanted first.
