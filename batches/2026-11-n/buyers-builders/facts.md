Use the corrected wording and the numbers in `findings-verified.json`.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the script that computes every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-n/buyers-builders/` (see its `README.md`):
- `pull_deeds.py` (Register of Deeds index, every DEED (001) recorded 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31, 30,333 deeds; writes `raw/deeds-co.json`; a person's name is blanked). Pulled 2026-11-08 07:2x to 07:32Z. Same search as the verified batch 2026-10-d pull, one month later; that older file (`data/2026-10-d/buyers-new-construction/raw/deeds-slim.json`, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026) was not reused because its slim step blanked company names it did not know, which hid Clayton Properties Group's other names and the Chesapeake community companies.
- `pull_parcels.py` (county GIS layer 24 parcels for 18,165 priced parcels, layer 22 postal city for 6,575 parcels a company sold; writes `raw/parcels.json`, `raw/postal-city.json`).
- `pull_listings.py` (CRM map API; writes `raw/listings-calls.json` with all 95 URLs called and the totals the API returned; the pin file with listing firms stays in the scratch folder). Pulled 2026-11-08 about 07:28Z to 07:36Z.
- `analyze.py` (writes `findings.json`). Build every chart and table from `findings.json`, never type a number.
- `check_chesapeake.py` (prints the row 15 cross-check).
- `raw/sec/*.txt`: text of each 10-K (and one 8-K) quoted below, converted from the EDGAR HTML.

**Never on the page, from any row:** an interest rate, a payment, any address, MLS number, listing firm or photo of a listing, a value for any one home (no AVM), a builder's photo, logo or rendering, or a verdict on a builder (quality, reviews, "best"). No community is described by who lives there. Rankings are by homes sold, and the page says so.

Short names for sources:
- **DEEDS**: Horry County Register of Deeds, public index (AcclaimWeb), https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/search/SearchTypeDocType, document type DEED (001), recorded 2025-11-01 to 2026-10-31. Counted by `analyze.py` with the verified filter of batch 2026-10-d (`data/2026-10-d/buyers-new-construction/verify_build_deeds.py`): price over $10,000; one 11-digit parcel; no tax-sale or court party; no corrective, re-recorded or multi-lot memo; parcel in the county layer with a residential land use code. **A builder sale** = the seller is one of the builder companies in `analyze.py` BUILDERS and the buyer is not a builder, developer, land company or owners' association. Buyers that are other companies (mostly rental companies) are kept and counted in row 10. "Home" here means a house, townhouse or condo unit: the deed does not say which (Toll Brothers' Hill Street Park Villas and Ashton Woods' Blue Heron Retreat are recorded as condo regimes). Price = the consideration on the deed.
- **PARCEL**: Horry County GIS, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24 (parcels) and /22 (address points, POSTAL_CITY). Town = the postal city of the parcel's address point (the mailing town, not city limits). 335 builder parcels had no address point yet: 139 took the postal city most common among builder parcels in the same subdivision, 195 that of the nearest builder parcel (`findings.json` town_assigned_by).
- **PLAT**: the subdivision name in the deed memo ("LOT 12 SUB AUBERON WOODS PH 2"), with phase, section and lot cut off; the same parse as batch 2026-10-d `build_communities.py`. This is the county's plat name. A builder may sell the community under another name (Pulte's "BELL TRACT" in Little River is a plat name, not a sales name). The writer names a community only by a name the reader will see on a sign, after the verifier checks it on the builder's own page; otherwise the town only.
- **CRM**: the site's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?<params>, active MLS listings in Horry County flagged new construction (`has=new_construction,county_horry`, types house, townhouse, condo), 1,086 pins. A listing's builder is the builder's own sales company when the listing firm is one (778 listings), else the CRM's "Built by" filter when exactly one matches (14), else none (294). Asking prices, counted in aggregate. MLS source line, from the API's `foot`: "Source: Coastal Carolinas Association of REALTORS® Multiple Listing Service (CCMLS)."
