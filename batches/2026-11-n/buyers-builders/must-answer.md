## Who searched this, and why

A buyer, often moving from another state, who has seen model-home signs along US 501, SC 31 and SC 90 and wants to know which names matter. Many have heard of D.R. Horton or Lennar at home; few have heard of Mungo, Beverly, Chesapeake or Great Southern. They are worried about build quality (they search "reviews", "complaints", "vs") and about being steered into the builder's lender.

Why they typed it (CRAFT 1):
- "best home builders myrtle beach" / "reviews": they want a quality verdict. Fair dealing and our rules rule out a verdict on a named company. The honest answer is what can be counted (homes sold, where, at what price) and what each builder states in writing (warranty, lender), plus the offer to walk a buyer through any one builder's contract.
- "dr horton vs lennar": the two largest here, side by side. The page gives the comparison in numbers: count, middle price, towns, warranty, lender share.
- "new construction under 300k": a budget buyer. The asking-price ranges by builder answer which builders list homes at that price now.
- "ryan homes myrtle beach": a buyer who cannot find Ryan Homes in the listings. Ryan sold the third most homes but almost none show in the MLS feed (facts row 8).
- "custom home builders myrtle beach": a different reader (own lot, own plans). One sentence says the builders here are production builders, and the small local builders who sell a few homes a year on single lots are counted together.

## The deciding fact (checked first)

**Can the builders be counted from public records, by name, for the latest 12 months?** Yes. Horry County's Register of Deeds index names the seller of every deed. In the 12 months from November 1, 2025 to October 31, 2026, 5,280 of 17,778 recorded home sales were a builder's sale to a first owner, and 17 builders recorded 50 or more (facts rows 1, 2). The two largest recorded about 45 percent and the five largest about 67 percent. Without this count the page would be a list of names any search returns; with it, the page has the ranking nobody publishes.
