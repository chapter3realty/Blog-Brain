# Shot list: /buyers/new-construction/builders/

For the photo partner, sent with `templates/photo-request-email.md` (its safety and privacy rules go in the email). The page works without these. They would replace a 2023 government aerial with today's ground, and show what a builder's community looks like from the street.

Never a builder's sign, flag, banner, logo, model-home sign or sales trailer as the subject, and none readable if it can be avoided. No people, plates or house numbers. The captions will name the community, never praise or fault a builder.

## Most wanted

1. **Auberon Woods, Conway** (Google Maps: "Auberon Woods, Conway, SC", off Highway 90). The page shows this land as cleared ground in 2023; the county records 217 new homes sold here in the last year. One shot down a street of finished houses from the sidewalk, and one wide shot from the entrance road showing how many houses stand there now. For: the "who builds the most" section, as the "after" to the aerial.
2. **A street still being built, near Longs** (Google Maps: "Eden Springs, Longs, SC", or any large new community along Highway 90). Houses in open wood framing beside finished houses on the same street, from a public road. For: showing a community while the builder is still selling.

## If there is time

3. **Arcadia, Myrtle Beach** (Google Maps: "Arcadia, Myrtle Beach, SC", near the Carolina Bays Parkway). Three builders sold homes here in the same year. One street shot where houses of different designs stand side by side. For: the "compare builders in one visit" paragraph. The caption will not say which builder built which house.

Three of each, phone sideways, morning light.
