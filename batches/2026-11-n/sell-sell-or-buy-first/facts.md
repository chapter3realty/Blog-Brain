**Deciding fact (checked first).** Can county records show what owners here actually do, sell first or buy first, and how long each takes? Yes. The deed index names the first seller and the first buyer on every deed. Matching a seller's name, letter for letter, to the buyer on another Horry County home deed within a year found 3,233 moves from 33,089 home sales by one person (July 2021 to October 2025). A chance check says the matches are real: of sales with two years of deeds on each side, 1,595 had a purchase within a year and only 290 had one only 1 to 2 years away (row 2). Keeping only buyers who still own the new home and get the 4 percent rate for a home you live in leaves 1,749 households who moved from one Horry County home to another. They split almost exactly in thirds (row 1). The page can be written as planned, with "what owners here did" as its spine.
