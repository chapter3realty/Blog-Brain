# Owner questions: /sell/sell-or-buy-first/

Asked 2026-11-08. The page ships without Q1. Q2 and Q3 each have a proposed answer the page uses until the owner replies.

**Q1. A real story of a client who sold one home here and bought another.** Which came first (sold first, bought first, or both the same week)? How long between the two closings? Where did they stay, if they sold first (a rental, family, a rent-back)? Did their offer depend on their sale (the SCR504 addendum), and did the seller ever send the notice that a backup offer had come in? Tell it as it happened; we add no detail. Proposed answer if none: no story; the county numbers carry the page.

**Q2. The contract forms our agents use today.** Do our agents use the South Carolina REALTORS forms (SCR310 with the SCR504 sale contingency and the SCR505 notice) for homes here? In the 2026 versions, does SCR504 still set a deadline for the buyer's house to close, let the seller keep marketing, and give the buyer a set number of days after the SCR505 notice to show a contract, drop the condition, step back, or end the deal? Proposed answer: yes to both, which is what the 2020 forms and SCR's legal FAQ say; the page names the forms by number and describes them in its own words.

**Q3. How we put a rent-back in writing.** When a seller stays in the house after closing, do we write it on an addendum to the contract, or does the closing attorney draft a separate agreement? What do we usually cover (move-out day, daily rent, a deposit, utilities, insurance)? Proposed answer: "We ask for the rent-back in your contract, and it is in writing before closing day", with the list of what goes in it, and no claim about who drafts it.
