**Deciding fact (checked first).** The page promises a place a buyer can walk to the sand from a house, and what it costs against inland. Both hold. (a) The county record has about 10,400 houses within half a mile of the ocean in Horry County alone (row 9), and 420 houses for sale within half a mile of it today (row 6); a public path to the sand is never far, with about 13 to 21 public beach accesses per mile in the beach towns (row 11). (b) The price difference is real and has a shape no one has published: house sales jump in the first quarter mile, then hold about level out to 2 miles, then step down (rows 2, 3, 6). The page can be written as planned.

**New pulls (2026-11-08).**
- SHORE = the ocean shoreline from the US Census TIGER/Line 2024 coastline (public domain), https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2024/COASTLINE/tl_2024_us_coastline.zip , clipped to lat 33.30 to 33.87 (`build_coast.py`, `coast.geojson`). It is one line from south of Pawleys Island to Hog Inlet at the state line; it crosses the inlets and does not follow Murrells Inlet's creeks or the marsh. Checked on known places: Kings Highway at 29th Avenue North is 0.31 mile from it, the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk 1.37 miles (to the Garden City beach), Conway 14 miles.
- DIST = straight-line distance from a point to SHORE, in miles, on a flat local projection at 33.65 N (error well under 1 percent at this size). A distance word on the page is this measure unless the row says "walk".
- STRETCH = four stretches of shore, cut at measured points (`findings.json` "stretch_cuts_miles_from_south_end"): North Myrtle Beach and Cherry Grove (north of Briarcliffe Acres); Myrtle Beach (Briarcliffe Acres south to the Surfside Beach town line; it holds Arcadian Shores, the city and Myrtle Beach State Park); Surfside Beach, Garden City and Murrells Inlet (the Surfside line south to the Murrells Inlet jetty; a Murrells Inlet house's nearest ocean is the Garden City beach); Litchfield and Pawleys Island (Georgetown County, south of the jetty). A point belongs to the stretch of its nearest shore point.
- COUNTY = Horry County GIS parcel app, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer : layer 24 house parcels with a building (MarketImprv > 0) in a strip about 3.5 miles wide along SHORE (53,406 parcels), layer 19 FEMA flood zones in the strip (980 polygons), layer 31 town limits (`fetch_county.py`). Countywide house parcel count: same layer and rule, `returnCountOnly` = 130,922 (`RAW/county-house-count.json`). A parcel's point is its centroid. Parcel rows stay in scratch (RAW); outputs carry counts only.
- LISTINGS = the CRM's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?type=house and the same with `has=` each beach and flood key and `not=hoa`, pulled 2026-11-08 07:17 to 07:25 UTC, MLS data refreshed 2026-11-08 07:10 UTC (`pull_listings.py`; 394 calls). The API returns at most 144 pins a call, so each search was cut into price slices until every slice returned all of its homes: 3,673 of 3,677 houses pulled (4 sit in price slices of under $1,000 that could not be split). Pins outside South Carolina dropped; 3,483 SC houses with a map point remain. Counted in aggregate only; no address, MLS number, firm or photo is kept or published. Source line for the page: Coastal Carolinas Association of REALTORS MLS (CCMLS), through our site's search, on the date of the pull.
- ACCESS = SC Department of Environmental Services (SCDES), Beach Access map, https://gis.des.sc.gov/beachaccess/ (service https://gis.des.sc.gov/gisserver/rest/services/BeachAccess/MapServer/0 , ACCESS_PTS), every point in Horry and Georgetown counties: 451 points (`pull_access.py`, `raw/scdes-access-points.json`). The points were last edited between 2013 and 2022 (166 in 2013, 199 in 2014, 76 in 2019, 10 in 2022). Their parking fields are that old too, so the page uses the points' places only, never their parking.
- WALK = the OpenStreetMap foot router, https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/ (OSRM table service), from each house for sale within 1.5 miles of SHORE to the nearest of its 6 nearest ACCESS points, plus the straight step from the house's map point to the nearest mapped path (`pull_walks.py`, `analyze_walks.py`, `walk-findings.json`). Walking speed in that router's profile is 5 km/h, about 3.1 miles an hour (row 15).
