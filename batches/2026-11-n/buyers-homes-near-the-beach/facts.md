**New pulls (2026-11-08).**
- SHORE = the ocean shoreline from the US Census TIGER/Line 2024 coastline (public domain), https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2024/COASTLINE/tl_2024_us_coastline.zip , clipped to lat 33.30 to 33.87 (`build_coast.py`, `coast.geojson`). A point belongs to the stretch of its nearest shore point.
- COUNTY = Horry County GIS parcel app, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer : layer 24 house parcels with a building (MarketImprv > 0) in a strip about 3.5 miles wide along SHORE (53,406 parcels), layer 19 FEMA flood zones in the strip (980 polygons), layer 31 town limits (`fetch_county.py`). Parcel rows stay in scratch (RAW); outputs carry counts only.
- LISTINGS = the CRM's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?type=house and the same with `has=` each beach and flood key and `not=hoa`, pulled 2026-11-08 07:17 to 07:25 UTC, MLS data refreshed 2026-11-08 07:10 UTC (`pull_listings.py`; 394 calls). Pins outside South Carolina dropped; 3,483 SC houses with a map point remain. Counted in aggregate only; no address, MLS number, firm or photo is kept or published. Source line for the page: Coastal Carolinas Association of REALTORS MLS (CCMLS), through our site's search, on the date of the pull.
- ACCESS = SC Department of Environmental Services (SCDES), Beach Access map, https://gis.des.sc.gov/beachaccess/ (service https://gis.des.sc.gov/gisserver/rest/services/BeachAccess/MapServer/0 , ACCESS_PTS), every point in Horry and Georgetown counties: 451 points (`pull_access.py`, `raw/scdes-access-points.json`). The points were last edited between 2013 and 2022 (166 in 2013, 199 in 2014, 76 in 2019, 10 in 2022). Their parking fields are that old too, so the page uses the points' places only, never their parking.
- WALK = the OpenStreetMap foot router, https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/ (OSRM table service), from each house for sale within 1.5 miles of SHORE to the nearest of its 6 nearest ACCESS points, plus the straight step from the house's map point to the nearest mapped path (`pull_walks.py`, `analyze_walks.py`, `walk-findings.json`). Walking speed in that router's profile is 5 km/h, about 3.1 miles an hour (row 15).
