# Photo shot list: /buyers/homes-near-the-beach/

For the photo partner. One drive along the beach towns, about two hours. Hold the phone sideways and take each shot three times. Shoot only from public streets, sidewalks and public beach accesses. No faces, no house numbers, no car plates. Rules: `rules/images.md`.

The page is about houses a few streets back from the ocean that you can walk to the beach from. Today it has aerial photos and one photo from the sand. It has no photo from the ground of the walk itself.
