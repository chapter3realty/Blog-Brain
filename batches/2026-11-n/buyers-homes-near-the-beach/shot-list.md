# Photo shot list: /buyers/homes-near-the-beach/

For the photo partner. One drive along the beach towns, about two hours. Hold the phone sideways for every shot and take each one three times. Rules: `rules/images.md`. Shoot only from public streets, sidewalks and public beach accesses. No faces, no house numbers, no car plates.

The page is about houses you can walk to the beach from, a few streets back from the ocean. We have aerial photos; we have no photo from the ground of what that walk looks like.
