# Owner questions: /sell/seller-disclosure/

Asked 2026-11-08. The page can ship without Q1. Q2 decides the offer words; the plan has a fallback.

**Q1. A real disclosure story.** Has a Chapter3 sale had a disclosure question that mattered: a flood claim, a roof leak, a beachfront setback statement, an HOA special assessment, a lead form on an older house? If yes, tell it in your words: where (town, kind of home), what was on the form or left off, and how it ended. No story is in `stories/stories.json` about a disclosure, so the page has none until you send one. We will not add a detail.

**Q2. The record look-up offer.** Will a Chapter3 agent, before a seller fills in the form, look up the home's FEMA flood zone, whether it sits past the state's beach setback line, and its year built, and send them to the seller? The plan uses "Have us pull your home's records" as the offer under the short answer and in two offer boxes. If not, the page uses the pricing offer instead (Have us price your home).

**Q3. Who gives sellers the form.** When Chapter3 lists a home, does the agent give the seller the commission's form and the written notice of the seller's duties that SC Code 27-50-70 requires at the listing? A yes lets the page say "When you list with us, your agent gives you the form and explains each question" in the end band. A no, or no answer, leaves that sentence out.
