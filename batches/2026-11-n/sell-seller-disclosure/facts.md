**Deciding fact (checked first).** Does South Carolina make every seller of a home fill in a state form, and is the duty about what the seller knows? Yes. An owner of a home with one to four units must give the buyer the commission's written disclosure statement, unless the sale is one of 15 exempt kinds (rows 1, 5). The form asks what the owner has "actual knowledge" of, and a seller is liable only for what they knowingly leave off or misstate (rows 7, 26). The page can be written as planned.
