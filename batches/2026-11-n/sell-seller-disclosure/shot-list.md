# Shot list: /sell/seller-disclosure/

For the photo partner (template: `templates/photo-request-email.md`; the owner sends it from his Gmail). Batch 2026-11-n, 2026-11-08. The page now uses one USGS aerial from 2011. Ground photos from today would replace or join it.

Rules for every shot: no faces, no readable house numbers, plates or business signs, no building name in view. Landscape, at least 2,400 px wide. Tell us the street or beach access and the date.

1. **Dune line behind oceanfront houses** (section "What extra disclosure does a beachfront home need?"). From the beach at low tide in North Myrtle Beach or Garden City: the dune, a dune walkover and the first row of houses behind it, wide enough to show all three.
2. **Dune line in front of an oceanfront condo building** (same section, the condo case). From the beach in Myrtle Beach or Arcadian Shores: the dune or the seawall at the foot of a condo tower, the beach in front. No building name readable.
3. **A house raised on pilings on a street away from the beach** (section "Do you have to disclose flooding?"). Socastee, or Conway near the Waccamaw River. The whole house and the open space under it, from the street.
4. **An older brick ranch house** (section "Do you need a lead paint disclosure?"). Conway, Surfside Beach or Myrtle Beach, a house of the 1960s or 1970s, from the street.
5. Optional: **a septic tank lid or a well head in a yard**, close up, nothing that identifies the house (the checklist, Part I).

His photos are our own photos once he confirms in writing.
