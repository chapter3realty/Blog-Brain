## Other findings

- **"Not found" note is partly wrong.** It says CCAR reuses one link per month and older reports are gone. Some 2026 files are still up under other names: `Coastal-Carolinas_MMI_2026-06.pdf` (200) and `July_2026_All_Cities.pdf` (200). `Coastal-Carolinas_MMI_2026-08.pdf` and `-09.pdf` return 404. This does not change any row.
- **"The Grand Strand" for two-county figures.** The checks say to call CCAR's figures "the Myrtle Beach area" or "the Grand Strand". CCAR's group is all of Horry and Georgetown, including Conway, Aynor, Loris and Andrews, which are inland. Name "Horry and Georgetown Counties" once at the top and in each table caption, so "the Grand Strand" is not read as the coast alone.
- **Wow lines that pass.** For the selling places on this page, these are true and sourced: condo offers accepted January to September 2026 were more than in either of the last two years while the condo median was the lowest of the three (rows 7, 10); the middle condo sold for less than a year before in 10 of the last 12 months (row 8, corrected); houses sold at 97.3 percent of their last list price (row 4).
