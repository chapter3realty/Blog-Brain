## Checks on the four stretches

- **Group.** Rows 1 to 12 are CCAR MLS sales in Horry and Georgetown Counties. Row 14 is Horry County deeds. Never one ratio across them. "House" here is CCAR's "Single-Family"; "condo" is CCAR's "Condo". Say "the Myrtle Beach area" or "the Grand Strand", never "the City of Myrtle Beach".
- **Time.** Every CCAR figure is September 2026, or January to September of 2024, 2025 and 2026. Say "September 2026" once at the top and in each table caption. The deed row is October 2025 to September 2026.
- **Completeness.** Row 10: "more than in either of the last two years", not "the most". Row 9: September only.
- **Firmness.** Days stop at the accepted offer (row 3). Row 9's "has stopped" is the past two years, not a forecast. Row 16 promises no date.
