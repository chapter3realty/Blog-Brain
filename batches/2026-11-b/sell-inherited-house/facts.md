## Conflict noted (not a page claim)

- Horry County's Estate Administration page (row 3's URL) still says "A Small Estate Administration occurs when the decedent's personal property less liens and encumbrances does not exceed twenty-five thousand dollars" and "less than twenty-five thousand dollars". That is the limit before 2025 Act No. 26. The county's own fee schedule (https://www.horrycountysc.gov/departments/probate-court/fees/) already uses the new figure: "Affidavit for Collection of Personal Property: ... Property Value $20,000.00 and up to $45,000.00 $67.50". The page states the statute ($45,000) and links the statute, not the county estate page, for the limit.
