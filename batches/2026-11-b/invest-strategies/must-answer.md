## The reader

Someone with money to put into Grand Strand real estate who has not picked a way to do it. Often from another state (about 3 in 10 homes sold here go to buyers whose tax bill goes to another state, row 25). Some already own a rental elsewhere and have a gain to move. Some have cash and time. Some want income with no calls at night. They have heard the words (BRRRR, DSCR, 1031, Airbnb) from podcasts. They do not know what each costs to start here, how often it actually happens here, or which one fits their money and their week.

**Their decision:** which of the eight ways fits my money, my time and my goal, here, and which page do I read next?

5. **Can I invest without being a landlord?** A DST (rows 27 to 30), or any rental with a property manager. One line each.
6. **How do the strategies fit together?** DSCR is a loan, not a property: it finances a long-term rental, a nightly rental or the BRRRR refinance. 7. **The pairs people search:** BRRRR or flip, flip or rent, long-term or short-term, 1031 or DST. One plain sentence each, then the link.
8. **What can go wrong with each?** One honest line per strategy, from its page (rows 9, 10, 18, 22, 24, 28, 29).
9. **How do we help?** We read the HOA papers for rental rules before you offer (row 5), we run the rent and the costs on any address, and some deals come before they list (rows 2, 3). Investors most often ask us for properties with the numbers run (row 4).
