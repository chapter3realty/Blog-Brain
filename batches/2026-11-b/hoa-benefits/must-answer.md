## The reader, and why they typed it

A buyer who has found a home with a monthly fee, and wonders if the fee is money well spent. Three common cases here:
1. A house buyer in a newer neighborhood who sees $95 to $150 a month and asks what it buys.
2. A buyer weighing a community with a big amenity, such as a beach club, a resort pool or a staffed gate, and its higher fee of $300 to $400.
3. A buyer with a boat, a truck or a plan to rent, who suspects the fee comes with rules that cost them more than money.

Why they typed "worth it" or "waste of money": a fee looks like rent on top of the mortgage. They want it priced against what they would pay to get the same things alone. What they would pay an expert to tell them: what this fee buys in dollars, what the same thing costs alone, whether it raises the home's price when they sell, and what they give up.
