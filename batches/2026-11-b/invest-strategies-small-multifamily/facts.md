`DATA` = website working copy `data/2026-11-b/invest-strategies-small-multifamily/`. Numbers built from data are in `DATA/findings.json` (made by `DATA/analyze.py`), `DATA/pums-2to4.json` (`DATA/analyze_pums.py`) and `DATA/code-check.json` (`DATA/check_codes.py`). Sources (short names used in the rows):
- **LIST**: the site's own public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?type=multi (the MLS type the API labels "2 to 4 units"), pulled 2026-11-10, feed refreshed 2026-11-10T06:00 UTC. Source line: "Coastal Carolinas Association of REALTORS® Multiple Listing Service (CCMLS)". Counted in aggregate; no address, firm or photo kept. `DATA/pull_listings.py`; `DATA/raw/listings-prices.json` (price, mailing city, parcel code, in-county flag). County line: Census TIGERweb (`data/2026-10-d/sell-home-value/raw/horry-county.json`). Town = the listing's mailing city, so "Myrtle Beach" includes Carolina Forest and Socastee addresses.
- **PARCELS**: Horry County GIS, Parcels layer 24 and Addresses layer 22, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24 and /22, queried 2026-11-10: every parcel with `LandUseCode` 102, 103 or 104 and `MarketImprv > 0`. `DATA/fetch_county.py`. County-wide counts by code: `DATA/raw/stock-by-landuse.json`.
- **DEEDS**: Horry County deeds recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, 13,194 home sales after the filters in `batches/2026-10-b/facts/affordable-facts.md` row 18 (verified), file `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json`. This page keeps the sales whose PIN is in PARCELS.
- **OCEAN**: straight-line distance from each parcel's first county address point (or the listing's map point) to the edge of the Census TIGERweb polygon named Atlantic Ocean (`data/2026-10-d/sell-home-value/raw/ocean.json`), the method of /sell/home-value/. Straight line is the point here: how close to the ocean a building stands (CRAFT 2, distance words).
- **PUMS**: Census ACS PUMS 2020-2024 5-year, South Carolina housing file, https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/pums/2024/5-Year/csv_hsc.zip , PUMAs 02701 and 02702 (wholly in Horry County), 2024 dollars, 90 percent margins from the 80 replicate weights. Same method as `data/2026-11-a/invest-rent-prices/analyze_pums.py` (that page's detached-house figure, 275 records and $1,400, reproduces exactly). BLD 04 = a building with 2 apartments, BLD 05 = 3 or 4 apartments (PUMS data dictionary).
- **ACS**: ACS 2020-2024 5-year B25024 and B25032, Horry County, https://data.census.gov/api/access/data/table?id=ACSDT5Y2024.B25024&g=050XX00US45051 (and B25032). `DATA/raw/acs5-2024-B25024-horry.json`, `-B25032-horry.json`.
- **HUD**: HUD Handbook 4000.1, Update 18, issued August 12, 2026 ("Last Revised: 8/12/2026"), https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/Housing/documents/40001-hsgh-Update-18.pdf . The lines used: `DATA/raw/hud-4000-1-update18-excerpts.txt`.
- **92561**: HUD form 92561, https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/92561.pdf , copy `DATA/raw/hud-92561.txt` (from the condotel batch).
- **FHLMC**: Freddie Mac, "Maximum LTV/TLTV/HTLTV Ratio Requirements", https://sf.freddiemac.com/general/maximum-ltv-tltv-htltv-ratio-requirements-for-conforming-and-super-conforming-mortgages , fetched 2026-11-10, `DATA/raw/fhlmc-ltv.html`, `.txt`.
