- HomeLight "best time to sell" and "sell house fast" pages; Opendoor's and Sundae's cash-offer pages. National templates. Opendoor's Myrtle Beach page prints "43.7 months of supply", an impossible number.
- None gives CCAR's latest numbers by area, the months when buyers sign the most contracts here, or what an investor really pays here.
- Beat them with: CCAR's September 2026 days to an accepted offer and share of list price for houses and condos and for 13 areas; the months with the most accepted offers; our own count from Horry County deeds of what companies paid for houses and what they resold them for; South Carolina's rule on wholesaling; one line on cost with the net-proceeds link.

## The reader

A Grand Strand owner who is thinking about selling in the next few months. Often a second home or a rental, often an owner who lives in another state. Some need speed (a move, an estate, a divorce, a house that needs work). They know their address and roughly what they owe. They do not know how long a sale takes here, how close to asking homes sell, what a cash buyer really pays, or what it costs.

Why they type it:
- "sell my house myrtle beach" / "sell my home myrtle beach sc": they are choosing how to sell and who to call. They want the options with numbers, and a reason to call us.
- "sell my house fast myrtle beach" / "we buy houses myrtle beach": they want speed or no repairs, and they wonder how much less a cash buyer pays.
- "is now a good time to sell": they want today's numbers for sellers (how fast, how close to asking), not a forecast.
- "best time to sell a house in south carolina": they want the month to list.
- "how do you decide how much to sell your house for": a pricing question; /sell/home-value/ owns the value. This page says one thing about pricing (most homes for sale have cut their price; sale-to-list is measured from the last price) and links home-value.
- "sell home and buy another": they need to know whether they can buy first, sell first, or stay after closing.
