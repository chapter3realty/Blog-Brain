## Group, time and firmness notes for the writer and verifier

- The values are the county's market values as the GIS layer serves them on 2026-11-09, not necessarily the values "for the year you bought" that the page tells readers to use. The page should say "the county record" for the example, and keep its instruction to readers to use the year of purchase.
- The three records are single real cases, chosen at the median price, not typical ratios. The page already says "There is no typical ratio"; rows 1 and 2 at the same price show why (27 against 62 percent).
- Bedrooms and size are not in the layer; the page should not describe the houses beyond the place.
