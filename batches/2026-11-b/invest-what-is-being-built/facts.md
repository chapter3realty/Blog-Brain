The deciding facts, reported first:

1. **The metro is Horry County alone.** The page says the permit count "covers the Myrtle Beach metro, which is Horry County plus Brunswick County in North Carolina." Since the July 2023 delineation, Brunswick County is in the Wilmington metro. The Census metro file for 2025 matches the Horry County file exactly (rows 1, 5). The sentence is wrong and must change.
2. **Which permit count.** The page sums FRED's monthly figures, which are the Census's first count for each month. The Census's own year-to-date and annual files add late reports and corrections, and they differ a lot: 2025 is 6,511 in the annual file against 6,898 summed by month, and January to September 2026 is 3.7 percent below 2025 on the year-to-date files against 12.3 percent below on the monthly sums (rows 2 to 7). The page should print the Census year-to-date and annual totals. The monthly bars can stay as first counts.
