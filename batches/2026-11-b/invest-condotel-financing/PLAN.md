## The reader and the decision

An out-of-town buyer who has stayed in a Myrtle Beach oceanfront resort, found a unit for sale, and heard "no" from a bank or saw "rental program" on the listing. Many will rent it nightly through the resort's desk and use it a few weeks a year. They decide: **can this unit get a loan, which loan, and if not, which unit or building should I look at instead?** They fear being stuck with a unit nobody can finance when they sell.

## The answer in two sentences

Fannie Mae and FHA loans do not work in a Myrtle Beach building run like a hotel, and about 8 in 10 oceanfront condos for sale say they are in the resort's rental program (885 of 1,093). Condotel loans from non-agency lenders do, when the unit has a separate bedroom, a full kitchen and about 500 square feet; about 1 in 5 rental-program units for sale is smaller than that.
