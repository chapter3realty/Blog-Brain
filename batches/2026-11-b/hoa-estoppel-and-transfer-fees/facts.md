## What I tried for row 1

- Web searches (2026-11-09) for Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet and Grand Strand HOA or management company estoppel, resale certificate, resale package, closing letter and statement-of-account fees, with and without dollar amounts, and with HomeWiseDocs and CondoCerts (the ordering portals many managers use). Every hit with a number was Florida, Texas, Maryland, Virginia, Washington or Connecticut law, or a marketing blog.
- The one local number found is a real estate blog on Market Common (lifeinmb.com) saying "$100 to $400". A blog is not a source (researcher rule 2). It is the same range the /sell/ page prints, and it does not say where it came from.
- Community Management Solutions (Myrtle Beach), its FAQ page https://commgtsol.net/Community-Management-FAQS.asp: no fee schedule. Searches for Hinson Management and IMC Resort Services fee pages: nothing.
- The ordering portals show a fee only after you pick a specific association and start an order. A range across the Grand Strand would need many associations sampled one by one, with each fee recorded. That is a research job, not a single source.
- The website repo's own data: no Grand Strand fee in data/ or research/. data/2026-11-a/hoa-documents/ holds other states' resale-certificate statutes and fee caps (Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida), none for South Carolina, because South Carolina has none.
