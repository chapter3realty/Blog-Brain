## The reader

## The deciding fact

Who pays which closing cost by default. The state REALTORS' standard contract, Form 310 (03/2026), answers it: the seller pays the deed preparation, deed stamps, recording, the payoff and its release, their share of the year's taxes, their broker and their own attorney; the buyer pays both title insurance policies; the HOA's sale charges go to whichever side is ticked, and the seller if no box is ticked. Verified in 2026-11-a (rows 1 to 3, corrected to the 03/2026 form). The page can be written as planned.
