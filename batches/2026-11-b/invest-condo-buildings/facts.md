Short names:
- **API**: Chapter3's public map API over the CRM feed of Coastal Carolinas MLS active listings, counted in aggregate, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?type=condo&subdivision=<words>. `subdivision` matches the MLS subdivision name by its words (a substring). `hoaMax=n` counts listings whose association fee per month is n or less; a blank fee counts as 0. The middle fee and middle half are found by bisection on hoaMax, to $5 (`pull_building_fees.py`). Pulled 2026-11-09 05:22 to 06:17 UTC; every URL called is in `building-fees.json` `calls` (the first two buildings, Myrtle Beach Resort and Landmark, were pulled by an earlier run of the same script whose call list was not saved; their base URL is in their `url`).
- **Group note for every API row:** units for sale now, as each listing agent entered the fee and ticked the flags. Not sales, not the association's budget. A unit's fee depends on its size, so a building's middle fee is the middle of the units on the market this week.
- **Parcels**: Horry County GIS parcel layer 24, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24/query, every parcel whose legal description starts with the building's regime names (`regimes.json`). A unit is a parcel with a building value. Read 2026-11-09 (`pull_regimes.py`).
- **Deeds**: the batch 2026-10-d deed file above: one-parcel residential sales of $10,000 or more recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, matched on the same regime names. A regime the county codes as other than residential (land use 1xx) would have units and no sales; `regimes-out.json` gives each building's residential share (all 0.96 or more).

**Never on the page, from any row:** an interest rate or a payment amount; a value for one unit or address; a single listing's price; anything about who lives in a building; a grade of a building or a company.
