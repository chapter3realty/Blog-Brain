## Notes on the data method (rows 12 to 18)

- NfipPolicies is "policy transactions". In the window, every record is a one-year term (policyTermIndicator 1). Repeats with the same complex, construction date and coverage have their own original start dates and terms, so they are separate buildings in one complex, not endorsements. 41 records match another record in every field. They may be twin buildings or duplicates. They change no "most" or "about" statement. Call them "policies", never "buildings counted".
- Condo codes H and L have no policies in the window: checked by count query (0 and 0).
