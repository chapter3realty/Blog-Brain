## The deciding fact

Whether a public source states what the reader must buy, in numbers. It does: Fannie Mae's Selling Guide B7-3-04 (08/05/2026) sets the HO-6's minimum (enough to restore whatever the master policy leaves out, or the per-unit deductible, whichever is more) and its maximum deductible (5 percent or $2,500, whichever is more). B7-3-03 caps the master policy's deductible at 5 percent of its coverage, including a separate wind deductible. With FEMA's own estimate of what Horry condo buildings cost to rebuild per unit, those rules give a dollar example for one owner. Checked first; the page can be written as planned.
