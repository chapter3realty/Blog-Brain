## 2. The reader and their decision

A buyer of a Grand Strand condo, often an oceanfront unit they may rent out, between offer and closing. Many are first-time condo buyers from out of state. Their decision: **how big an HO-6 to buy** (the inside of the unit, the loss assessment limit, wind, and whether to add a flood policy), and what to ask the association before they close. They worry about a storm bill they did not see coming. They will ask next what the policy costs and what the lender checks.

## 3. The answer in two sentences

The building's master policy, paid through your condo fee, insures the structure; your HO-6 insures your belongings, your liability, and whatever the master policy leaves out inside your unit. On this coast, check the building's storm deductible: at 5 percent, an owner's share could be about $6,500, and a standard HO-6's loss assessment cover is $1,000.

(Rows 1, 7, 8, 9, 20. The second sentence is a labeled example in the body: "if the bill is split evenly". The hero may shorten it.)
