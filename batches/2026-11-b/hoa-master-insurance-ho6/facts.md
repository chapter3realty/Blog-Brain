Short names:
- **RCBAP**: the federal flood policy a condo association buys on its building (Residential Condominium Building Association Policy). OpenFEMA code `condominiumCoverageTypeCode = A`.
- **Unit flood policy**: a federal flood policy on one condo unit (OpenFEMA code `U`, "Individual Condominium Unit insured by a unit owner or by an association"). It is written on the Dwelling Form.
- **Window**: flood policies that started 2025-09-01 to 2026-08-31, OpenFEMA asOfDate 2026-09-08 (the same window as the hub's row 9).
