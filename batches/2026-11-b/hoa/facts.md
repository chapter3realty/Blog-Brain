Short names:
- **API**: Chapter3's public map API over the CRM feed of Coastal Carolinas MLS active listings (homes for sale, the whole feed: Horry, Georgetown, nearby counties and a few NC ZIPs), counted in aggregate, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?<params>. `hoaMax=n` counts listings whose association fee per month is n or less (the site's filter "HOA up to $n"; a blank fee counts as 0). `has` and `not` are the listing agent's feature flags (labels in `raw/filters.json`: "Oceanfront building", "High-rise" (Outside group), "Pool or clubhouse", "Gated", "Golf community", "Amenities in the community"; the fee-includes flags are in the "Community and HOA" group). Quartiles are found by bisection on hoaMax, to $5, among listings with a fee. `pull_fees_by_kind.py`, pulled 2026-11-09 03:07 to 03:46 UTC (all condos at 03:47).
- **Group note for every API row:** homes for sale now, as each listing agent entered the fee and ticked the flags; not sales, not a survey of associations. A flag such as "Gated" or "Golf community" is the agent's.

**Never on the page, from any row:** an interest rate or a payment amount; a value for one address; fees of a named oceanfront building (/invest/condo-buildings/ owns them); anything about who lives anywhere.
