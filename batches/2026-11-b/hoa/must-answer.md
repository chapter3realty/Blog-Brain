## The reader

Someone who has seen a Grand Strand listing with a monthly fee, often from out of state, and wants to know if that number is normal for this kind of home and what it pays for. Two common versions: a condo shopper who sees $900 a month on an oceanfront unit and wonders why, and a house shopper who sees $95 or $350 and wants to know what that buys. They know nothing about "regime", "master association" or "reserves". They are worried about paying for nothing, and about a fee that hides a bigger bill. Next they ask: can it go up, what if I do not pay, and is there a home with a lower fee or none.

Why they typed it (CRAFT 1): they are pricing a home they like ("is $640 normal?"), comparing kinds of home ("condo or house?"), or budgeting a move ("what will I pay each month besides the mortgage?"). Some arrive from a named place ("del webb myrtle beach hoa fees", "waterbridge myrtle beach hoa fees").
