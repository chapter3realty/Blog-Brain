# Fact ledger: /invest/accommodations-tax/

Researcher pass, 2026-11-09. Only the facts the triage fix needs: each city's own tax on rent, and the total on a $1,000 booking. The verifier fills the last two columns.

The deciding fact, found while checking: the state revenue department collects more than 7 percent on a stay in Horry County. Its own rate chart (ST-575) puts the accommodations total it collects at 9 percent in Horry County, North Myrtle Beach and Surfside Beach, 10 percent in Myrtle Beach, and 8 percent in Georgetown County (rows 1 to 3). The extra is local sales tax the department collects for the county. The page's "South Carolina charges 7 percent. Your county or city adds 1 to 3 percent on top" leaves it out, so the totals in rows 10 to 12 cannot be built on the page's current table alone.

## Not verified

- Whether North Myrtle Beach has a separate local accommodations tax on top of the 1.5 percent. Its FY2024 budget ordinance (https://www.nmb.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Item/887?fileID=2601) speaks of "The 2 1/2% Hospitality Fee and the 3% Local Accommodations Fee", which reads as the city's and the county's charges added together (1% + 1.5% on food; 1.5% + 1.5% on rooms), not a third city tax. The city's FAQ (row 7) lists only the 1.5 percent on accommodations, and the S&P report names only "A 1.5% charge" plus "a portion of a county fee". Row 11 uses the FAQ. A verifier who wants certainty can call the city revenue office, 843-280-5555.
- The Myrtle Beach 2021 news notice prints the local accommodations tax as ".05 percent" in one copy (https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/news_detail_T6_R1354.php). The FAQ (".5%") and the other notice ("0.5%") agree, and 13 percent only adds up with 0.5. Treat ".05" as a typo.
- Conway's own 1 percent fee is already on the page; it was not re-checked here, so no Conway total is given.
- Airbnb's collection list was not re-checked (not part of this fix).
