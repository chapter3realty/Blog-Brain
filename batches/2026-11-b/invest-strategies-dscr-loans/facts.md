## Our arithmetic: how much of the rent the tax, insurance and HOA use before the loan

Method for rows 16 to 22: `DATA/build_findings.py`. Tax = price x 0.06 x mills / 1000 / 12 (rows 11, 12). Insurance = row 15 / 12. Dues = row 14. The share is (tax + insurance + dues) / the monthly rent counted. At a 1.25 ratio, the whole PITIA can be at most 80 percent of the counted rent, so "left for the loan" = 80 percent minus that share. No rate or payment is used. Flood insurance is left out (none of the middle houses here need say they carry it).
