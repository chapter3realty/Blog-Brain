# Shot list: /invest/condos/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). The page works without these; each one replaces a free-licence stand-in or adds a photo of the subject itself. No person as the subject, no readable unit numbers, plates or business signs. With each photo, send the street and the building or complex name, and confirm in writing that we may use it as our own.
