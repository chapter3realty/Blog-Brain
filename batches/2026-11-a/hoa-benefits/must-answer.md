Someone who found a home they like, and it has a fee. Often a buyer from up north who has never paid a condo fee of $650 a month, or a house buyer who sees "$95 a month" and wonders what it buys. Some are boat or work-truck owners who suspect an HOA will not let them park it. They want a yes or no for their kind of home, in dollars, and what could go wrong. They do not know SC law, the word "regime", or what a reserve study is.
