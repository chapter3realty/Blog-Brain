## Short names for sources

- **Places.** Thirteen places, each one outline, north to south (`analyze.py` header). The 11 of /invest/where-to-buy/ (its ledger, "Areas": city or town limits for Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach and Conway; the Census designated place for Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Carolina Forest, Socastee and Little River; Longs = ZIP code area 29568 outside North Myrtle Beach; Pawleys Island and Litchfield = ZIP 29585), plus **Loris = ZIP code area 29569** (the City of Loris and the county around it) and **Aynor = ZIP code area 29511** (the Town of Aynor and the county around it). A house is in a place when its parcel's centre point is inside. Murrells Inlet's Census place is on the Georgetown County side only (where-to-buy VERIFY finding A); the Horry side of Murrells Inlet is not in it.
- **House.** Horry: land use 100, 101, 102, 110 or 126 and not a horizontal property regime (no condos, townhouses or villas). Georgetown: land use ending 100, 101 or 500.
