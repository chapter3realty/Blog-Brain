## Short names for sources

- **Places.** Thirteen places, each one outline, north to south (`analyze.py` header). The 11 of /invest/where-to-buy/ (its ledger, "Areas": city or town limits for Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach and Conway; the Census designated place for Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Carolina Forest, Socastee and Little River; Longs = ZIP code area 29568 outside North Myrtle Beach; Pawleys Island and Litchfield = ZIP 29585), plus **Loris = ZIP code area 29569** (the City of Loris and the county around it) and **Aynor = ZIP code area 29511** (the Town of Aynor and the county around it). A house is in a place when its parcel's centre point is inside. Murrells Inlet's Census place is on the Georgetown County side only (where-to-buy VERIFY finding A); the Horry side of Murrells Inlet is not in it.
- **House.** Horry: land use 100, 101, 102, 110 or 126 and not a horizontal property regime (no condos, townhouses or villas). Georgetown: land use ending 100, 101 or 500.
- **Horry parcels.** Horry County GIS layer 24, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24 , pulled 2026-11-02 for where-to-buy (scratch `horry-homes.json`, `horry-points.json`); lot sizes pulled 2026-11-03 (`fetch_acreage.py`: the county's Acreage field, or Shape.STArea() / 43,560 when Acreage is 0, as on 104,642 of 130,922 house parcels; where both exist they agree within 1 percent at the middle).
- **Georgetown parcels.** Georgetown County GCGIS_Export layer 2, https://gis1.georgetowncountysc.org/portal/rest/services/GCGIS_Export/FeatureServer/2 , box -79.25,33.36,-78.98,33.62 (where-to-buy `fetch_georgetown.py`, 2026-11-02; lot sizes `fetch_gt_area.py`, 2026-11-03, Shape__Area in square feet because TotalLandArea reads 1.0 on 17,997 of 22,147 parcels).

Georgetown: the parcel's last recorded sale in the same dates, $10,000 or more (no builder flag).
- **Flood.** Horry County GIS layer 19 and Georgetown County GCGIS_Nature layer 9, special flood hazard areas (A, AE, AO, VE), as where-to-buy row 25.
- **CRM.** The site's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map , houses (type=house, status=active), counted in aggregate, pulled 2026-11-03 (`pull_features.py`, 436 calls). WTB row 14's verifier re-pulled the str set and got the same counts, so the effect there may be small, but the method should skip bands whose total is 0.
