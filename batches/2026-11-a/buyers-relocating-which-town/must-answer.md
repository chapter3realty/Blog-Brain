## The reader

Someone moving to the Myrtle Beach area from another state who does not know the towns. They searched "best place to live in Myrtle Beach", "best towns near Myrtle Beach to live", "Myrtle Beach vs North Myrtle Beach" or "cheapest place to live near Myrtle Beach". They know the name Myrtle Beach and little else: not the roads, not that "Myrtle Beach" covers about 60 miles of coast and inland towns, not that city limits change the bills.

What they want first: a short list of towns that fit their money and their life, and what each one is like to live in. What worries them: buying in the wrong place (too far from the beach, flooding, too many tourists, rules they did not expect), and the yearly cost. What they ask next: what houses cost there, what is for sale, how long the drive is.

Why they typed it (CRAFT 1, "Ask why this person typed it"): they are about to spend a weekend driving around, or about to buy from far away, and want to cut 13 places to 2 or 3 before they come.
