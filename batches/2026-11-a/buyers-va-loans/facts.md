# Fact ledger: /buyers/va-loans/

Batch 2026-11-a, Lane U. Researcher: research helper, 2026-11-01. **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** Columns 9 and 10 are for a verifier who did none of this research.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the script that computes every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/buyers-va-loans/`:
- `pull-va-condos.sh` (VA condo report, Horry and Georgetown), `pull-hud-condos.sh` (HUD FHA condo list, Horry; copied from batch 2026-10-d), `pull-listings.py` (CRM map API counts, aggregate only), `pull-acs.sh` (Census ACS 2024 1-year), `pull-drives.py` with `origins.json` (OSRM drive times), `pull-suggestions.sh` (Google suggestions). Everything they fetch is in `raw/`, with text copies of the web pages and PDFs beside them.
- `build-findings.py` writes `findings.json` (every derived number) and the chart rows `chart-va-by-kind.json`, `chart-houses-by-town.json`, `chart-houses-by-price.json`.

**Never on the page, from any row:** an interest rate, a payment amount, a down payment percentage, or a funding fee percentage. The VA funding fee chart is a table of finance-charge percentages keyed to down payment percentages; both are Regulation Z trigger terms (PLAYBOOK A14, A17; audit errors on "N% down" on a /buyers/ page). The page says who pays no fee, that it can be rolled into the loan, and that it can be refunded; the VA's own chart is linked. "No down payment" is allowed. The VA page's "competitive interest rates" line is not repeated.
