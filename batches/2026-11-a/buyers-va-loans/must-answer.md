## Why they typed it

Three readers, one page:

1. **A veteran or service member moving here, or retiring here,** who knows the VA loan in general and wants to know what is different on the Grand Strand. The likely reason behind "va loan condo approval list" plus a beach town: they want a beach condo, and they have heard VA buyers get turned away. Behind "va loan second home" and "vacation home": they want a beach place they will not live in full time. Behind "va loan requirements for sellers" and "vs fha for seller": a seller or a listing agent is pushing back on their offer.
2. **A disabled veteran** who typed "do disabled veterans pay property tax in south carolina". They want a dollar figure and the steps, and they worry the form is a trap or the rule has a catch.
3. **A local seller** asking whether to accept a VA offer ("va loan requirements for sellers", "va loans pros and cons for seller").

What they already know: the national basics (no down payment, no PMI). What they do not know: which buildings here work, what share of homes for sale take VA, what the tax exemption is worth here, where the VA clinic is, and what the appraisal flags on a coastal home.

Worry: falling for a condo they cannot buy with the benefit; a funding fee surprise; an appraisal that kills the deal; losing the tax exemption by filing wrong; being treated as a weaker offer.

What they ask next: can I see homes that take VA; is this building on the list; who checks it.
