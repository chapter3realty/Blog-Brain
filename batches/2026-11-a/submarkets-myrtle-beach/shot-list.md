# Shot list: /submarkets/myrtle-beach/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight. Landscape, plus a square crop if he can. No person as the subject. No readable house number, license plate, listing sign or building logo as the subject. Shoot from public streets, the beach and public beach accesses only. Each photo becomes ours once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.
