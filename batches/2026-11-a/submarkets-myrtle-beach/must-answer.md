## Questions every reader of a town page has (added)

- Where it is, and what is close (the beach, the airport, the hospital) in minutes. The airport is inside the city.
- What a normal week is like in each part of the city.
- What the drawbacks are, plainly (summer traffic near the ocean core; the rental ban in R zones cuts both ways; flood zones near the ocean and the Withers Swash).
- The next step: one offer, tied to the gap the page cannot fully answer (which building, which zone, the HOA papers).

## Must not answer

- "Is Myrtle Beach safe", "crime rate", "best for families", "good for retirees": fair-housing steering and the owner's rule (`not-a-party-city`). The page rates no place as safe or unsafe. It can say we do not rate places and where official data is.
- Schools by score: no school scores on a town page (judge, Garden City plan). Assignment is by address; link the district.
- Any rate, payment or loan terms ("condotel loans", "higher rates"): Chapter3 does no financing work.
