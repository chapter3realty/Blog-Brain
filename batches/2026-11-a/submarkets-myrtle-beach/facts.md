# Fact ledger: /submarkets/myrtle-beach/

Batch 2026-11-a, Lane U. Researcher: research helper, 2026-11-02 (pulls ran 2026-11-02 between 04:20 and 05:30 UTC). **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** Every row below is `awaiting verifier`. Columns 9 and 10 are for a verifier who did none of this research.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the scripts that compute every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/submarkets-myrtle-beach/` (README there). `findings.json` holds every derived number; a row that says `findings X.Y` means that key path. Raw county parcels, address points and listing pins stay outside the repo (they carry addresses); `fetch_gis.py`, `fetch_mailing.py` and `pull_listings.py` rebuild them.

Short names for sources:
- **City zoning**: City of Myrtle Beach GIS, `https://terra.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/server/rest/services/ZONING_DISTRICTS_MB/FeatureServer/0` (288 zoning polygons, field ZONING_DIS; layer found through the city's public ArcGIS Online web map e838842d2c814473a6916e89b8ddb228, owner "bnies@cityofmyrtlebeach.com_comb").
- **City STR map**: the city's map image "Zones in the Myrtle Beach City Limits that Allow Visitor Accommodations (Short Term Rentals)", "Map Prepared by: The City of Myrtle Beach - Planning & Zoning", "Date of Print: 04/05/2021", embedded on the planning page (`https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/departments/planning_and_zoning_department.php`, image `https://cms6.revize.com/revize/myrtlebeachsc/departments/images/Zoning%20Map.jpg`), saved as `raw/mb-str-zone-map-2021.jpg`. The PDF version the page links (`/ShortTermRentalZoneMap.pdf`) returned 403 to curl.
- **Our STR zone map** (`str_zones.py`): the city STR map image fitted to the city's GIS city limits (rotation, scale, shift; overlap 0.85 IoU), then each current zoning polygon measured for the share of its area the city shaded pink, with about 60 m of tolerance for the fit. Rule: RMV always counts as allowed and every other district starting with R never does (the city's own text, row 2); any other district counts as allowed when 60 percent or more of it is shaded. Output `zone-pink.json`; map file `map-str-zones.geojson`.
- **City limits**: county parcel app layer 31, NAME = MYRTLE BEACH (23.8 square miles; the city's own CITY_LIMITS_MB layer is 23.9; they differ on 0.25 square miles).
- **County GIS**: Horry County parcel app `https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer` layers 24 (parcels), 22 (address points), 19 (FEMA flood zones), 31 (municipalities).
- **Home**: a parcel with a county land-use code starting with 1 and a building value above 0 (the rule of the 2026-10-b, 2026-10-d and Conway ledgers). Condo = land use 107 or "HPR" in the legal description; house = 100, 101, 102, 110, 126; villa or townhouse = 108, 135. Location: the parcel's county address point, or a point inside the parcel outline when it has none (most condo units).
- **4 percent rate**: county assessed value divided by taxable value = 0.04 (legal residence) or 0.06 (all other), the Garden City ledger's method. **Tax bill out of state**: the parcel's owner mailing state (OwnerState) is not SC.
- **Deeds**: Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb, DEED (001), recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, kept-sale rules of affordable-facts row 18 (verified): 13,194 home sales countywide in `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json` (ships in Blog-Brain `batches/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/web.patch`). A sale is "in the city" when its point is inside the city limits. Condo = the file's condo flag ("HPR" in the legal description). "Buyer's tax bill out of state" = the file's `where` for sales whose current owner is the grantee.
- **Listings**: Chapter3 CRM public map API `https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?city=Myrtle%20Beach&type=<house|townhouse|condo>[&has=<flag>]`, every pin collected by splitting price bands (360 calls, no truncation), counted in aggregate; meta.refreshedAt 2026-11-02T04:21:21Z. "Inside the city" = our city-limits test on the pin, not the API's flag (the two disagree on 572 pins; row 37).
- **AirROI**: `https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/south-carolina/myrtle-beach`, "Updated October 1, 2026", saved `raw/airroi-myrtle-beach.html` and `.txt`. AirROI's "Myrtle Beach" market is not the city limits (str-vs-ltr row 1 verifier note): never put these figures beside "the City of Myrtle Beach".
