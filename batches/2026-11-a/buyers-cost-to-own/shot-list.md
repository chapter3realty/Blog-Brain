# Shot list: /buyers/cost-to-own/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). The page has two stand-in photos today, both U.S. government aerials: Carolina Forest house streets from above (NOAA), and oceanfront condo buildings in Myrtle Beach from a plane (USGS, 2011). Everything else is our own tables, charts and cards. Shots 1 and 2 replace the stand-ins. Shots 3 to 5 add pictures the page lacks.

Ask for at least 1200 px wide, landscape, daylight, plus one square crop of shot 1. No people as the subject, no readable house numbers or plates, no for-sale signs, no building name or logo as the subject. Ask for a written line that he took them and Chapter3 may use them on its website and marketing.
