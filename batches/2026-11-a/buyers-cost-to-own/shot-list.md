# Shot list: /buyers/cost-to-own/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). The page has one photo today: a 2011 USGS aerial of oceanfront condo buildings in Myrtle Beach (section "Is a condo cheaper to own than a house"). Every other section uses our own tables, charts and cards. These shots replace the stand-ins and add the pictures the page lacks.

Ask for at least 1200 px wide, landscape, daylight, plus one square crop of shot 1. No people as the subject, no readable house numbers or plates, no for-sale signs, no building name or logo as the subject. A written line that he took them and Chapter3 may use them on its website and marketing.
