# Fact ledger: /submarkets/conway/

Batch 2026-11-a, Lane U. Researcher: research helper, 2026-11-01 (pulls ran 2026-11-01 between 23:00 and 24:00 UTC). **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** Columns 9 and 10 are for a verifier who did none of this research.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the script that computes every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/submarkets-conway/` (README there). `findings.json` holds every derived number; a row that says `findings.json X.Y` means that key. Raw county parcels, address points and listing pins stay outside the repo (they carry addresses); `fetch_county.py` and `pull_listings.py` rebuild them.

Short names for sources used below:
- **County GIS**: Horry County parcel app, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 31 Municipalities, 24 Parcels, 22 Addresses, 19 Flood Zones) and https://www.horrycounty.org/gisweb/rest/services/Public/HazardsMap/MapServer (layer 17 Regulated Supplemental Flood Zone, 0 Florence Road Closures, 1 Matthew Road Closures).
- **City limits**: layer 31, NAME = "CONWAY" (one polygon, about 30.7 square miles). This ledger uses the county's own city limits, not the Census place outline the affordable ledger used.
- **Home**: a parcel with a county land-use code starting with 1 and a building value above 0 (same rule as the 2026-10-b and 2026-10-d ledgers). Location: the parcel's county address point, or the parcel's polygon center when it has none (624 of 8,935 city homes, mostly condo units).
- **Florence flood map**: the county's Supplemental Flood Zone (HazardsMap 17), drawn by Western Carolina University's PSDS from field flood heights after Florence (2026-10-d undisclosed-flooding ledger row 14, verified). It shows where the water reached, not which houses took water inside.
- **High-risk zone**: FEMA's Special Flood Hazard Area (layer 19, SFHA_TF = 'T').
- **Deeds**: Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb, DEED (001), recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, with the kept-sale rules of affordable-facts row 18 (verified): 13,194 home sales countywide, in `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json`. A sale is "in the city" when its point is inside the city limits.
- **Affordable ledger**: `batches/2026-10-b/facts/affordable-facts.md`, rows already verified. Reused rows keep their value so the site says one number.
