# Shot list: /submarkets/conway/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight, no people as the subject, no house numbers or license plates readable, no listing signs. Landscape and one square crop of each if possible.

1. **City Hall on Main Street** (the white building with columns, at Main Street and Third Avenue), from across the street, the whole front in frame.
2. **The Riverwalk on the Waccamaw River**, from the boardwalk near Riverfront Park, looking along the river with the boardwalk in frame.
3. **Main Street shops**, a long view down the block of brick buildings with the oak trees.
4. **A street of older houses in the downtown area** under the live oaks (for example Kingston Street, Elm Street or Fifth Avenue near downtown), from the sidewalk.
5. **A newer subdivision street west of downtown** (Rosehaven, Oak Glenn or New Castle, off US 378 or US 701), houses and yards, from the public street.
6. **A street of the Sherwood area north of downtown** (Sherwood Drive near Crabtree Swamp), from the public street, to show the low ground near the swamp. A second shot of the swamp edge or the bridge on Long Avenue over Crabtree Swamp if safe.
7. **A golf course view in Wild Wing** off US 501, from a public road or the clubhouse lot.
8. **The edge of the Coastal Carolina University campus** on University Boulevard: buildings and signage, no students as the subject.

Optional: a street with large lots far north on SC 905 (Ridgewood West or Maple Hill), from the public road.

Each photo becomes our own once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.
