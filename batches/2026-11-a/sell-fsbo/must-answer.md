## The deciding fact

The page can be written as planned only if an owner may legally sell their own home in South Carolina without a licence, and the closing rule is clear. Both hold: SC Code 40-57-240(1) exempts an unlicensed owner selling their own interest, and the Supreme Court of South Carolina requires attorney supervision of closings (facts 1, 2).
