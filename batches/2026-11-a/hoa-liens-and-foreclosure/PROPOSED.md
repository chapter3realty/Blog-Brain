# Proposed registry rows and sibling fixes: /hoa/liens-and-foreclosure/

For the lead. Nothing here is applied. Each row is from `facts.md` and waits for the verifier.

## facts/registry.json

1. **SC condo assessment lien priority.** Value: the lien ranks ahead of every other lien except property tax liens and recorded mortgages and other recorded liens (SC Code 27-31-210(a); facts.md row 3). Known wrong wording on the site: /hoa/violations-and-fines/ says the condo lien "outranks most other claims". That overstates it: the statute puts taxes, recorded mortgages and other recorded liens ahead. Suggested replacement on that page, in a later batch: "In a condo, unpaid assessments become a lien on the unit. It comes after property taxes and recorded mortgages."
2. **SC HOA foreclosure is judicial.** Value: a condo or HOA lien is foreclosed through the court (27-31-210(a); Bailey; Winrose). The fact appears on this page, /hoa/south-carolina-hoa-laws/ and /hoa/violations-and-fines/.
3. **Magistrate court limit for HOA money disputes.** Value: $7,500 (22-3-10, 27-30-160; facts.md row 23). The judge flagged that /hoa/south-carolina-hoa-laws/ gives no figure. Stale by: when 22-3-10 is amended.
4. **Horry County share of SC HOA complaints.** Value: 31% of 452, 0.38 per 1,000, calendar 2025, SCDCA 2026 report (facts.md row 21). Stale by: 2027-03-01, the next report.
