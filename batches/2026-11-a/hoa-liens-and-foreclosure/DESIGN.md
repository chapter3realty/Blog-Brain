# /hoa/liens-and-foreclosure/: design pass

Reader: an owner who is behind on dues, or got a letter, and typed "can an HOA foreclose in South Carolina". They are worried. They want the answer, the steps, how much time they have, and what to do.

Screenshots: `before/` (first-1440, first-390, slice-00 to slice-18 at 1440 x 900, phone-00 to phone-26 at 390 x 844).

## What makes the page good (keep all of it)

- The headline asks the reader's own question and answers it in the next line: "Yes, through the court, and the bill that starts it can be small."
- The short answer gives the three things a worried owner needs: yes, the bill grows, and 30 days to answer.
- Every section heading is a question people search. A reader who scans the headings gets the page.
- Real local evidence no other page has: the county's sale list as 19 squares, the debt against the home's value, the Winrose bill with its cents.
- The Winrose story told as numbers: the bill table, then the dark "what the house sold for" panel, then the court's words. That panel is the page's one dramatic moment and works.
- One real ground photo, of the building where the sale is held.
- The steps in order with the months from a real case, and the "what to do now" list in plain words.
- The offers are each tied to the section above them, and all use the same quiet box.

## What makes it look cluttered or monotonous

1. **Card on card, all the way down.** About 30 white glass cards: 8 for the steps, 5 for the actions, 3 for "the order works both ways", 3 for the bills, 2 and 2 for the side-by-side pairs, 5 figures, 4 offers, and a frame around the photo. Everything has the same weight, so nothing stands out, and the page reads as a stack of boxes rather than an article. (slice-05, slice-06, slice-10, slice-11, slice-13, slice-14; phone-07 to phone-09)
2. **Red alarm outlines on seven items.** Steps 4, 5 and 7, the third ladder row, action 1 and the "can block the loan" card all get a red border. Seven warnings in one page dilute the warning and make the page feel loud. (slice-06, slice-09, slice-11, slice-13)
3. **Boxes inside boxes.** The "who is paid first" ladder puts three bordered rows inside a card (slice-09). Each step has a grey pill inside its card (slice-05). The photo sits in its own card frame (slice-07).
4. **Ragged right edges.** Prose ends at about 665 px, the offer boxes at 720, the cards and figures at 760, the waffle grid at 520 inside a 760 card, the share bar at 620 inside a 760 card. Down the page the right edge jumps every few hundred pixels. (slice-03, slice-04)
5. **The photo breaks the steps, and the right side beside them is empty.** The eight steps run about 1,500 px down a desktop screen with 500 px of empty space to their right; then the photo sits under step 8, between the steps and the sentence that finishes them ("the bidding closes at the auction"). (slice-05 to slice-07)

## One change for each

1. **Cards only for the data figures and the offers.** The steps, actions, pairs, "both ways" and bills become open lists: no background, no shadow, separated by a thin rule or by space. The numbers and timeline line stay. The figures (waffle, share bar, bill table, ladder) keep the one card style, and the offers keep theirs.
2. **Red marks the urgent items with the number, not a box.** Steps 4, 5 and 7, action 1 and the third ladder row keep their red number dot. The "can block the loan" item keeps a red label and a red top rule. No red outlines.
3. **No box inside a box.** The ladder rows become plain rows with dividers inside the one figure card. The step time labels become small brass text instead of pills. The photo loses its card frame.
4. **One right edge.** All the page's own blocks (figures, lists, the dark panel, the photo) are 720 px wide, the same as the offer boxes. The waffle and the share bar fill their card.
5. **Photo beside the steps on a wide screen.** At 1180 px and up, the steps sit at 720 px and the courthouse photo sits to their right, staying in view while the reader goes down the steps (it is where step 7 happens). Below 1180 px it stays under the steps, as now. The sentence about the bidding then follows the steps directly.

Not changed: the hero, the order of sections, the dark Winrose panel, the quote, the FAQ, the sources, every offer. The live listings strip under the hero comes from the shared kit and is not this page's to change.
