# Shot list for the photo partner: /buyers/common-mistakes/

For the owner to send from his Gmail (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Shoot from public roads, sidewalks, the public beach and parking lots open to the public, unless a homeowner gives permission. No people as the subject, no faces up close, no signs or logos as the subject, no readable house numbers or license plates. Landscape, as wide as the camera allows, plus one vertical of each if easy.

The page now has three stand-in photos, all public domain: a Conway street flooded after Florence (2018), oceanfront towers from a 2011 USGS flight, and houses beside towers from the same flight. Entries 7 and 9 have no photo yet.
