## The reader

A buyer from out of state, often moving here to live, sometimes buying a second home or a condo to rent. They have bought a house before, somewhere else. They searched "mistakes when buying a house" or "buying a house in south carolina from out of state" because they are afraid of the thing they do not know to ask about here. They do not know the tax classes, the flood maps, the city lines or the contract terms. What they would pay an expert to tell them: which mistakes cost real money here, how much, and how to be sure they do not make them.

## What the national pages already say (and we do not repeat as the point)

The top results for "home buyer mistakes" list the same national items: get pre-approved, do not skip the inspection, do not overextend, do not make big purchases before closing, research the neighborhood. The local results for Myrtle Beach are agent blogs with the same list and no numbers. To beat them, every item on this page is local, has a dollar figure from a public record, and says what we do about it.

## The deciding fact

The H1 promises "the mistakes that cost the most". The page can keep that promise only if the costs can be ranked in dollars from public records. They can: the 4 versus 6 percent gap on the middle Horry County house is about $2,930 a year (facts 11, 12), the county's own records show how many recent buyers still pay it (fact 13), and FEMA's own policy and claims data price the flood mistakes (facts 28 to 30).

## Questions the page raises, answered on the page

- "What if I am moving here in the middle of the year?" Fact 8 (the whole year counts).
- "Can I keep my old house up north?" Fact 9 (the county asks for its tax bill with no exemption).
- "Is the house in a flood zone if the listing does not say so?" Fact 31.
- "Do I need flood insurance outside the zone?" Facts 19, 20, 29, 30 (the choice is the buyer's; the page gives the two numbers).
