# Shot list: /submarkets/north-myrtle-beach/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight, landscape, and a square crop of each if he can. No person as the subject. No readable house number, license plate, boat registration number or listing sign. A business sign may be in the frame but not the subject. Each photo becomes ours once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.

Today the page has no photo of the place. The stand-ins in PLAN.md are a 2012 Commons photo of Cherry Grove Pier, 1941 public-domain postcards of Ocean Drive, and a labelled USDA aerial of the Cherry Grove channels from 2023.

In page order:

The rules, every time: never shoot while driving; only from public roads, sidewalks, beach accesses and lots; a gated community from outside the gate.
