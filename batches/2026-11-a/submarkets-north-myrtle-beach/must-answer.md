The reason behind the top searches (CRAFT 1, "ask why this person typed it"):
- **"north myrtle beach vs myrtle beach"** is typed by someone who has visited both and wants to know if the north end is a different kind of place to own a home. Answer: a separate city with its own rules (nightly rentals allowed anywhere, a lower city tax), mostly houses and low and mid-rise condos by section, and how prices differ. Never by who lives there.
- **"north myrtle beach neighborhoods" / "best area"** is typed by someone who has heard of Cherry Grove and Ocean Drive and does not know what separates them. Answer: the four old beach towns of 1968 plus the newer areas west of US 17 and across the waterway, each with what it gives you, what a house sold for, how many houses are someone's main home, and how much of it is in the flood zone.
- **"cherry grove channel homes for sale"** is typed by a boater who wants a dock behind the house. Answer: how many houses sit on the channels, what they resold for, that nearly all are in FEMA's high-risk zone, and what a flood policy there costs.
- **"short term rental regulations"** is typed by a buyer who heard the city is changing its rules. Answer: what the rule is today (a business license; the parking number posted), what the city has discussed and not adopted, and the taxes on a week's rent.
- **"homes for sale under $200,000 / $300,000"** is typed by a buyer on a budget. Answer: where those homes are (condos, mostly, and west of US 17), counted from today's listings in aggregate.

## The deciding fact the reader needs to act

**Where in North Myrtle Beach you buy decides three things at once: what a house costs, whether your neighbors live there all year, and whether a lender will require flood insurance.** The page can prove it by part from county records (rows 8 to 14 and 17 to 19): in Cherry Grove 88 in 100 homes are in FEMA's high-risk zone and about 1 in 4 houses is someone's main home; across the waterway almost none are in the zone and about 3 in 4 houses are someone's main home. The middle resold house runs from about $410,000 in Crescent Beach to about $725,000 in Tilghman Beach. Nobody publishes this split.
