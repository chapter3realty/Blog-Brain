# Fact ledger: /submarkets/north-myrtle-beach/

Batch 2026-11-a, Lane U. Researcher: research helper, 2026-11-03 (pulls ran 2026-11-03 between 20:15 and 20:45 UTC). **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** Columns 9 and 10 are for a verifier who did none of this research.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the script that computes every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/submarkets-north-myrtle-beach/` (README there). `findings.json` holds every derived number; a row that says `findings.json X.Y` means that key. Raw county parcels, address points, outlines and listing pins stay outside the repo (they carry addresses or PINs); `fetch_county.py` and `pull_listings.py` rebuild them.

Short names for sources used below:
- **County GIS**: Horry County parcel app, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 31 Municipalities, 24 Parcels, 22 Addresses, 21 Hydrography, 19 Flood Zones, 18 Roads).
- **The city**: the county's municipal outline NAME = 'NORTH MYRTLE BEACH' (layer 31). A home is in the city when its point is inside. Atlantic Beach and Briarcliffe Acres have their own outlines and are not counted.
- **Home**: a parcel with a county land-use code starting with 1 and a building value above 0 (the rule of the Conway, Little River and 2026-10-d ledgers). Point: the mean of the parcel outline's vertices. **Kind**: condo = land use 107 or 135, or HPR or UNIT in the legal description; manufactured = 120, 121, 122 or 130; townhouse = 109; house = 100, 101, 102, 110, 126; other = the rest (Little River ledger, verified).
- **The parts** (`areas.py`): across the waterway = a line from the home toward the ocean (bearing 156 degrees) crosses the county's Intracoastal Waterway outline; west of US 17 = the line crosses US 17 (roads layer: N HWY 17, S HWY 17, HWY 17); Little River Neck = the county appraisal sub-area LRN (field MarketSubArea) for a house, or the sub-area of the 7 nearest houses for a condo; every other home takes the historic section of the nearest parcel whose recorded plat names it (legal description CHERRY GROVE or FUTCH = Cherry Grove; TILGHMAN = Tilghman Beach; OCEAN DRIVE = Ocean Drive; CRESCENT = Crescent Beach; WINDY HILL = Windy Hill). Check against the plats: 1,712 of 1,780 Cherry Grove plat parcels fall in Cherry Grove (68 in Little River Neck), 1,517 of 1,519 Tilghman, 197 of 197 Ocean Drive, 1,510 of 1,511 Crescent Beach, 816 of 817 Windy Hill. The lines between sections are ours (midway between the old plats), not the city's; the page says "about".
- **High-risk zone**: FEMA's Special Flood Hazard Area as the county carries it (layer 19, SFHA_TF = 'T': zones AE and VE), tested at the home's point.
- **Legal residence**: the county taxes the home at 4 percent (AssessedProp / TaxableProp under 0.05; where-to-buy ledger row 23, verified). Everything else (second homes, rentals, owners who have not applied) is at 6 percent.
- **Deeds**: the kept home sales of batch 2026-10-d (Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb, DEED 001, recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, 13,194 home sales countywide; method in `batches/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/facts.md` row 1). A sale is in a part when its PIN is a home in that part. "Resale" = the seller is not on that ledger's builder list. 2,029 sales are in the city.
- **Listings**: the CRM's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?city=North%20Myrtle%20Beach&type=<type>, every price band until each search returned every home, pulled 2026-11-03 20:27 UTC; 942 homes with a point, 930 inside the city outline. Aggregate only (CCMLS data; no address, firm or photo kept). Each listing takes the part of the nearest home parcel.
