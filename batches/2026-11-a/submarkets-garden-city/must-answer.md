A fifth group searches the beach day itself ("weather", "webcam", "restaurants", "pier music schedule", "tide chart"). The page answers only the parts a buyer uses: the beach rules, the pier, parking, golf carts. It does not try to be a weather or events page.

The reason behind the top searches (CRAFT 1, "ask why this person typed it"):
- "garden city sc homes for sale" and "condos" are typed by someone who has vacationed here and wants their own place. They need to know there are two very different Garden Cities: the beach strip on Waccamaw Drive and the inland subdivisions west of Business 17, at very different prices.
- "is garden city in horry county / is it part of myrtle beach / incorporated / zip code" is typed by someone who found an address that says "Murrells Inlet 29576" or "Garden City Beach" and is confused. The deciding facts: there is no town of Garden City; the county line crosses the beach about a quarter mile south of the pier; most of Waccamaw Drive is in Georgetown County.
- "short term rental / airbnb / rental rules" is typed by a buyer who heard Myrtle Beach bans rentals and wants to know if Garden City does. Answer: no county rental ordinance; the HOA or condo rules decide; the taxes and the license the county does require.
- "flooding / hurricane / renourishment" is typed by a buyer who saw water on South Waccamaw Drive in a video. Answer with the record: Hugo, Ian, the tide gauge, the flood-zone share, what flood insurance costs here.
- "is it a good place to live / retire" is typed by someone picturing winter here. The honest fact: on Census Day 2020 about four of five homes along Waccamaw Drive had no one living in them. Quiet winters, busy summers. (Never a word about who lives here; describe the place, not people.)
- "mobile homes for sale" is typed by a buyer on a budget who wants to be near this beach. The CRM shows dozens of manufactured homes for sale east of Business 17.

## The must-answer list, ranked

1. **What and where is Garden City?** An unincorporated beach community between Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet, with no town government. Name the parts: the beach strip along Waccamaw Drive (ocean side and creek side), the older streets between the marsh and Business 17, and the inland subdivisions west of Business 17. The Census place and the beach strip are not the same place.
2. **Is it in Horry or Georgetown County? Is it part of Myrtle Beach? What ZIP?** The county line crosses the beach about a quarter mile south of the pier. Most of Waccamaw Drive is in Georgetown County. Addresses use Murrells Inlet 29576 (and Surfside Beach 29575 at the north edge). Not part of the City of Myrtle Beach.
3. **What do homes cost here, by kind and by side?** Recorded sale prices for the last 12 months from county deeds: ocean-side condos, houses on the marsh, other houses east of Business 17, inland houses and condos. What is for sale now, in aggregate, including the Georgetown end and manufactured homes.
4. **Can I rent it out nightly (Airbnb)?** No county rental ordinance on either side; the HOA or condo rules decide; the county license, the state tax number, the county hospitality fee (3 percent outside city limits), the furnishings return. Georgetown side: no business license, a 3 percent county accommodations tax.
5. **What does a rental here earn, by month?** Real monthly revenue and occupancy (AirROI), replacing the invented chart. The worked example: the middle ocean-side condo, its tax bill at 6 percent, its rental revenue in money in and money out, no loan payment and no rate.
6. **What is it like to live here year-round? Who is here in winter?** The share of homes that are someone's legal residence by side; Census Day occupancy along Waccamaw Drive. The pier, the MarshWalk, the drive times to the airport, the hospital, Market Common, Huntington Beach State Park.
7. **Does it flood? Flood zones, insurance, hurricanes.** Share of homes in FEMA high-risk zones by side; what NFIP policies cost on the strip versus the rest of 29576; Hugo (up to 90 percent of homes wiped out), Ian (sand on South Waccamaw Drive); the tide gauge trend and high-tide flood days.
8. **Property tax.** 4 percent versus 6 percent in one line, with the yearly dollars for the middle ocean-side condo; link to /buyers/property-taxes/.
9. **Beach rules a buyer cares about.** Golf carts on the beach November 1 to February 28 with a permit; dogs (not 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., May 1 to Labor Day); alcohol; umbrellas and tents; Horry side versus Georgetown side.
10. **Beach renourishment.** The 2026 federal project on the Surfside and Garden City reach; what it does and does not cover.
11. **Garden City versus Surfside Beach and Murrells Inlet.** One short comparison: town government and rental rules (Surfside limits rentals to two districts), the pier, the inlet. Link to the sibling pages; do not repeat their facts.
12. **Is it safe? Schools?** We rate no place; who patrols each side (Horry County Police, Georgetown County Sheriff); which district each side belongs to (Horry County Schools, Georgetown County School District), assignment by address. No school scores on the page.
13. **Homes for sale now.** A homes row that shows Garden City homes (the CRM's Garden City area), never "Just listed near Garden City, in Surfside Beach".
