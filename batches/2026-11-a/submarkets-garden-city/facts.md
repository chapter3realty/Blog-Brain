Batch 2026-11-a, Lane U. Researcher: research helper, 2026-11-02 (pulls ran 2026-11-02 between 03:40 and 04:20 UTC). **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** Columns 9 and 10 are for a verifier who did none of this research.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the scripts that compute every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/submarkets-garden-city/` (README there). A row that says `findings.json X` means that key in that folder. Raw county parcels, address points and listing pins stay outside the repo (they carry addresses); `fetch_county.py` and `pull_listings.py` rebuild them.

Short names for sources used below:
- **County GIS**: Horry County parcel app, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 31 Municipalities, 24 Parcels, 22 Addresses, 19 Flood Zones, 18 Roads). Read 2026-11-02.
- **Census place**: Garden City CDP, GEOID 4528455, 2020 boundary from TIGERweb (`Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/26`). It lies wholly in Horry County and stops at the county line.
- **Home**: a parcel with a county land-use code starting with 1 and a building value above 0 (the rule of the 2026-10-b, 2026-10-d and Conway ledgers), whose polygon's inside point falls in the Census place. Condo = land-use code 107 or "HPR" in the legal description; house = code 101 (codes read from sample legal descriptions, not a county legend: 101 "LAKEWOOD EXT; LT 8 BL 27D", 107 "FLORAL VILLAS; APT 8 BLDG B").
- **The three parts** (our split, `zones.py`): **ocean side** = between Waccamaw Drive and the ocean; **between** = east of Business 17 and west of Waccamaw Drive (the creek side of the strip, the older beach-town streets and the marsh edge); **inland** = west of Business 17. **On the marsh** = a parcel within about 20 m of OpenStreetMap salt marsh or tidal water (natural=wetland, natural=water other than ponds). Road lines from County GIS layer 18 ("N WACCAMAW DR", "S WACCAMAW DR", "N HWY 17 BUS", "S HWY 17 BUS", "HWY 17 BUS", "BUS 17").
- **Deeds**: Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb, DEED (001), recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, with the kept-sale rules of the 2026-10-d out-of-state-buyers ledger (13,194 home sales countywide, `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json`, shipping in `batches/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/web.patch`). A sale is "in Garden City" when its point is inside the Census place. Georgetown County deeds are not in this file.
- **CRM**: https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?has=area_garden_city[&type=..] (the CRM's own Garden City area; a `city=Garden City` search returns 0). Counted in aggregate only, pulled 2026-11-02.
- **Strip blocks**: the 48 Census 2020 blocks with homes that touch a band about 120 m either side of Waccamaw Drive (OpenStreetMap ways named "North/South Waccamaw Drive"), both counties.
