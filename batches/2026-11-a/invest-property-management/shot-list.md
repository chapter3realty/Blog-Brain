# Photo partner shot list: /invest/property-management/

What a property manager costs on the Grand Strand, and when an owner needs one. Landscape, daytime, unless noted. No person as the subject. No plate, house number, or sign phone number or company name that can be read. No logo as the subject.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Replaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The front door of a one-story rental house anywhere in Carolina Forest, Socastee or Conway | A key lockbox or keypad lock on the door handle or railing, close enough to see it is a lockbox, with the door behind it. One landscape and one square crop. No house number in frame. | Shows what a far-away owner hands over: the key and the showings. Goes with "Do I need a property manager?" | Adds the first photo of the subject; nothing there now |
