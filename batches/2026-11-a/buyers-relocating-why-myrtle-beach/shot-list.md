# Shot list: /buyers/relocating/why-myrtle-beach/

For the photo partner. Daylight, no people as the subject, no company names or brokerage signs in frame. Landscape, as large as the camera allows. Each photo is used beside the sentence it proves.

1. **The Grand Strand beach on a weekday in October or November.** A wide shot along the sand with few people, to show the off season. Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach.
2. **The oceanfront boardwalk and the SkyWheel, Myrtle Beach.** From the beach side, daytime, the wheel and the boardwalk in one frame.
3. **A new-construction street in Carolina Forest.** Houses being framed next to finished houses on the same street, from a public road.
4. **Downtown Conway's Riverwalk.** The boardwalk along the Waccamaw River, with the river in frame.
5. **Ocean Drive, North Myrtle Beach.** Street level, off season, the shops and the street toward the ocean.
6. **SC 31 (Carolina Bays Parkway) at an interchange.** From a public overpass or roadside, to show the six-lane road built for the growth.
