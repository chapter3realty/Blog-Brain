**Deciding fact (checked first).** The owner's headline says "it's a buyer's market". The test is months of supply: homes for sale now divided by homes sold in an average month. Housing researchers call 6 to 6.5 months balanced (row 3). Our count (rows 4 to 8): **condos are a buyer's market at every price (about 7.5 months); houses are not (about 4 months), except above $700,000 (about 7 months).** More than half of the homes for sale have already cut their price (row 9). The headline holds for condos and expensive houses only. The page must say so, and the H1 must be scoped (PLAN.md).

**A trap found and fixed.** The 13,194-sale file every sibling uses (OSB 1) keeps only parcels with a county building value. New houses deeded in 2026 have none yet, so that file holds 2,010 builder sales where the county recorded 5,108 (NC 9). Builder sales in it fall from about 420 a month in late 2025 to about 25 a month by September 2026 (row 2). Using it for months of supply would make houses look like a buyer's market (5.0 months) when they are not (3.8). Rows 4 to 8 add the 4,998 builder house sales back from the new-construction pull. See NEEDS.md for the sibling rows this touches.
