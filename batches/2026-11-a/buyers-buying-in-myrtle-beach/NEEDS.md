# Needs from the lead: /buyers/buying-in-myrtle-beach/

Researcher, 2026-11-03. Nothing here is edited; each item needs the lead.

1. **Two values for City of Myrtle Beach condo supply.** /submarkets/myrtle-beach/ (live, ledger MB 14) says about 18 months: 1,004 condos for sale against 661 condo sales. Its analyze.py counts a sale as a condo only when the deed file's `condo` flag is set (HPR in the legal description). The site's condo rule (SHV 24, used by /sell/home-value/) is county land use 107 or HPR. With that rule the city has about 990 condo sales and about 12 months of supply (this ledger F8). The same split moves about 330 land-use-107 condos into MB's "houses, villas and townhouses" group (MB 14 houses 4.0 months, MB 16 condo median $175,000 on 658). Decide which value the site prints, and fix the other page in its next release. Until then this page prints the city condo figure only as "about a year" or leaves the number to its town table with the method in the sources line.
2. **New-home sales missing from the shared deed file.** `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json` keeps only parcels with a county building value. Houses sold new in 2026 have none yet, so builder sales in it fall from about 420 a month (late 2025) to about 25 (September 2026); it holds 2,010 builder sales where the county recorded 5,108 (NC 9). Pages that used it for new homes or for all sales:
   - /sell/home-value/ SHV 4: "new homes ... middle price about $337,000 for houses (2,010 sales)". The full file gives $330,000 on 4,998 (this ledger F11).
   - Any months-of-supply or "share of sales" figure built on it. Resale figures are not affected.
   Suggested shared rule for every later page: counts of all sales come from `data/2026-10-d/buyers-new-construction/raw/deeds-slim.json` with its verified builder rule; resale figures may keep the OSB file.
3. **Lesson candidate (CRAFT 2, "a filter on a field new items lack").** This is the same trap as batch 2026-10-b's "cheapest" list, one level down: a filter in a shared data file, reused by later pages that never saw the filter. PLAN.md proposes "Buying in Myrtle Beach: a buyer's market for condos, not yet for houses." The lead updates headlines.json only if approved.
