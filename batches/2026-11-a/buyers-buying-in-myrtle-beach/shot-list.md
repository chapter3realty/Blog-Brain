# Shot list for the photo partner: /buyers/buying-in-myrtle-beach/

For the owner to send from his Gmail (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Shoot from public roads, sidewalks, the public beach and parking lots open to the public. No person as the subject, no faces up close, no sign or logo as the subject, no readable house number or license plate. Landscape, as wide as the camera allows, plus one vertical of each if easy.

The page now uses two free stand-ins: a 2015 Wikimedia Commons photo of the beach by the SkyWheel (CC BY-SA 2.0), and a public domain NOAA aerial of Carolina Forest streets. Step 7 has no photo yet.
