# Shot list for the photo partner: /buyers/buying-in-myrtle-beach/

For the owner to send from his Gmail (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Shoot from public roads, sidewalks, the public beach and parking lots open to the public, unless a homeowner gives permission. No people as the subject, no faces up close, no signs or logos as the subject, no readable house numbers or license plates. Landscape, as wide as the camera allows, plus one vertical of each if easy.

Until these arrive, the page uses free stand-ins: the downtown beach and SkyWheel from a 2015 Commons photo (CC BY-SA 2.0), and public domain aerials already licensed for sibling pages (oceanfront towers, 2011 USGS; Carolina Forest streets from above, NAIP).

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Where it goes on the page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Downtown Myrtle Beach, the boardwalk between the SkyWheel and the 14th Avenue pier | The boardwalk and the beach beside it, wide, early morning or late afternoon, people small in the frame | The page opens with what a buyer gets for the money on the whole coast. The downtown beach is the picture every reader has in mind, and ours should be current | Under the short answer, the one photo above the first section. Replaces the 2015 Commons photo |
| 2 | Oceanfront condo towers, from the public beach in Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach | Three or more towers in a row, with the beach in front. No building name as the subject | Condos have the most homes for sale per sale of any kind here. The section "Is it a buyer's market?" says so | In the market section, beside the condo months-of-supply figure. Replaces the 2011 USGS aerial |
| 3 | A new-construction street in Carolina Forest or Longs | New houses on a finished street, with one house still framed or under roof if possible. No builder sign as the subject | Builders sold about 1 in 6 homes in the past year, and the middle new house cost less than the middle resale house. Readers need to see what that house is | In "What does a house cost here?", under the new-home sentence. Replaces the aerial |
| 4 | An older resale street in Myrtle Beach: Pine Lakes, Myrtle Heights or the streets off Kings Highway near 38th Avenue North | A street of brick ranch houses with mature trees and wide lots | The middle resale house in the City of Myrtle Beach sold for about $430,000; buyers need to see what an older in-town house looks like | In "What does a house cost here?", beside the town table |
| 5 | The Horry County Government and Justice Center in Conway, or the Register of Deeds entrance at 1301 Second Avenue, from the street | The building and its sign from the public sidewalk, no people | The last step of every purchase: the deed is recorded in Conway. A photo of the place makes the step real for a reader who will never go there | In the step timeline, at "the deed is recorded" |

Send each photo with the place, the date taken, and a line confirming in writing that Chapter3 may use it on the website. Each then gets a licence record that `tools/photos.js` accepts, with its files under `chapter3realty/images/2026-11-a/buyers-buying-in-myrtle-beach/` and its record in `data/2026-11-a/buyers-buying-in-myrtle-beach/photos.json`.
