## Who searches this

Three readers, in order of how many suggestions point to each:
1. **The condo investor or second-home buyer** who will rent the unit, all year or for part of it. Most suggestions ("for rental property", "on investment property", "on a second home", "schedule e", "airbnb") are theirs. On the Grand Strand this is most condo owners: only about 1 in 5 condo units in Horry County is taxed at the 4 percent rate for its owner's legal residence (ledger row 22).
2. **The owner who lives in the home** and hopes the dues lower their tax ("for primary residence", "count as property tax", "write off").
3. **The owner who works from home** ("home office", "if you work from home").

Why they typed it: they are pricing a condo, or doing a return, and want to know what the dues really cost after tax. The deciding fact for all three is how the home is used.
