# Shot list for the photo partner: /hoa/tax-deductible/

For `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first. Shoot only common areas and public places, and get the building's permission where you need it. Keep out faces, license plates and unit numbers. No building or association name may be readable. Shoot landscape, three frames of each.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Section it goes in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oceanfront condo buildings in Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach, from a public beach access. | Two or more buildings in one frame, from the sand or the access walkway, so no single building is singled out. | Only about 1 in 5 condo units in the county is taxed as its owner's legal residence. This replaces the stand-in, a 2009 Wikimedia Commons photo of one tower. | "Does South Carolina treat HOA fees differently?" (replaces the condo tower photo) |
| 2 | The mail room or the association office of any Grand Strand condo building, inside the common area. | The bank of owner mailboxes, or the association office door. Get close enough to show what it is, with every name and unit number out of frame or unreadable. | The page is about the bill the association sends. The reader can see where "the dues" come from. | "Where do HOA fees go on Schedule E, and what records do I keep?", above the four records |
| 3 | A condo building's pool deck or lobby on the Grand Strand, with no people. | The pool deck with loungers and the building behind, or the lobby with the elevators. No sign or logo readable. | The dues pay to keep these shared parts up. The IRS calls them the "common elements". On a rental, their upkeep is deductible. An improvement to them is spread over 27.5 years. | "What do condo HOA fees cost in Horry County?", beside "What Horry County condos for sale say their dues include" |

Before any photo goes on the page, the partner confirms in writing that it is ours (CLAUDE.md, photos). Its licence record goes in the website's `data/2026-11-a/hoa-tax-deductible/photos.json`, and the files go in `chapter3realty/images/2026-11-a/hoa-tax-deductible/`.
