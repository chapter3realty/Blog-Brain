# Shot list for the photo partner: /hoa/tax-deductible/

For `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first. Common areas and public places only, with the building's permission where needed. No faces, no license plates, no unit numbers, no building or association names readable. Landscape. Three frames of each.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Section it goes in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any Grand Strand condo building's mail room or association office (Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach), inside the common area. | The bank of owner mailboxes, or the association office door, close enough to show what it is, with every name and unit number out of frame or unreadable. | The page is about the bill that comes from the association. A real mail room makes "the dues" a thing the reader can see. The judge asked for it. | "Where do HOA fees go on Schedule E, and what do I keep?" (keep the association's statements) |
| 2 | A condo building's pool deck or lobby, Grand Strand, with no people. | The pool deck with the loungers and the building behind, or the lobby with the elevators. No signs or logos readable. | These are the shared parts the dues pay to keep up, which the IRS calls the "common elements". On a rental, their upkeep is deductible; an improvement to them is spread over 27.5 years. | "What do HOA fees come to on a Grand Strand condo?" |
| 3 | Oceanfront condo buildings in Myrtle Beach or North Myrtle Beach, from a public beach access. | Two or more buildings in one frame, from the sand or the access walkway, so no single building is singled out. | Only about 1 in 5 condo units in the county is taxed as its owner's legal residence. This replaces the 2009 Wikimedia Commons stand-in. | "Does South Carolina treat HOA fees differently?" |

Every photo needs the partner's written confirmation that it is ours before it goes on the page (CLAUDE.md, photos). A licence record goes in the website's `data/2026-11-a/hoa-tax-deductible/photos.json`, and the files go in `chapter3realty/images/2026-11-a/hoa-tax-deductible/`.
