1. **`sc-title-insurance-payer`** (facts.md row 2). Fact: "On South Carolina REALTORS Form 310 (9/2023), the buyer pays both title insurance policies (owner's and lender's) unless the parties agree otherwise in writing." Source: https://screaltors.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/310_website.pdf, paragraph 6.A(1). Owner page: /buyers/closing-costs/. Wrong pattern to scan for: the seller paying the owner's title policy as a rule, e.g. `\bsellers?\b[^.]{0,60}\bowner'?s title (?:policy|insurance)\b` unless the sentence has "agree", "negotiat" or "contract". 2. **`sc-hoa-box-default`** (facts.md row 3). Fact: "On Form 310, HOA document, transfer, estoppel and capital contribution fees are the seller's if no box is checked." Owner page: /sell/net-proceeds/. Wrong pattern: "the buyer (always|usually) pays the (HOA )?transfer fee" or "the seller (always|usually) pays the (HOA )?transfer fee" stated as a rule.
