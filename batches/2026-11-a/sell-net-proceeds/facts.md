**Deciding fact (checked first).** Who pays which closing cost in a South Carolina sale, by default? The page cannot list "seller costs" without it. The South Carolina Association of REALTORS' standard home contract, Form 310 (9/2023), answers it in paragraph 6 (rows 1 to 3). It puts the owner's and the lender's title insurance on the buyer, and deed preparation, deed stamps, payoffs, prorated taxes and the seller's attorney on the seller. HOA paperwork, transfer, estoppel and capital contribution fees go to whichever side the parties tick; **if no box is ticked, the seller pays them.** The page can be written as planned, with the who-pays table built from the form.
