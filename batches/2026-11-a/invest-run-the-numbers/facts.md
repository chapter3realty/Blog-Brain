Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the script that computes every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/invest-run-the-numbers/` (see its `README.md`):
- `pull-suggestions.sh` (Google suggestions), `pull_listings.py`, `pull_listings2.py`, `pull_medians.py` (CRM map API, aggregate counts only), `fetch_parcels.py` and `analyze_tax.py` (county parcel values for 2026 resales), `build_findings.py` (writes `findings.json` and `chart-houses-1pct-by-zip.json`).
- `raw/` holds text copies of the statutes, the 2026 levy, the county and HUD pages. PINs, deed book and page, and addresses stay outside the repo (scratch `raw/parcels-2026-sales.json`).

**Never on the page, from any row:** an interest rate, a loan payment amount, a down payment percentage, any address, PIN or owner, a value for any one address (no AVM). The worked examples are built from medians of many listings, never one listing. Sold-price figures come from county deed records, never the MLS.
