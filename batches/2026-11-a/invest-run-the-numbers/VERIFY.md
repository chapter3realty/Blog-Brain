## Notes on verified rows the writer must keep

- **Row 6.** Say "the value the seller was taxed on for 2026", and "a quarter sold at or below it" if you use the spread. Never "the price is always higher".
- **Row 8.** Say "about". The assessor sets the new value. This is the 6-percent-to-6-percent case, the one where the 25 percent exemption does apply.
- **Row 10.** "Stop more deals", not "kill". The owner's "about 3x" is a rule of thumb for the same house; this page's own figure (row 9) adds the value reset, so state each with its basis.
- **Row 12.** "In 15 Horry County ZIP codes" or "in Horry County" is fine; my rerun a day later found 2,976, which rounds the same. Asking prices, not sale prices.
- **Row 15.** Medians by count, nearest $5,000 and $25. House dues count homes with no HOA as $0. "The middle house's HOA fee", never "the typical HOA". Not a value for any home.
- **Row 17.** ZIP 29579 has a few parcels inside Myrtle Beach (19 of 1,276 sales); "outside city limits" is the safe label here too.
- **Row 19.** ZIP 29577 is mostly the city (433 of 520 sales). Label it "a condo inside the city".
- **Row 22.** "A loan officer at our preferred lender" is the story's required attribution. No down payment, rate or payment.
- **Row 23.** The Insurance Information Institute is an industry group; its page gives no year and no state. Say "nationally, about 25 percent more", beside "15 to 20 percent more in our files".
- **Row 25.** The verbatim gives no period for "over 1,200" and "600"; "a month" comes from the story bank's own summary. Keep it as the bank states it and add nothing.
- **Row 27.** SC Code 27-30-130 says governing documents must be recorded to be enforceable. It does not say rental bans are enforceable. The page must not cite it for that.
- **Row 29.** IRS Publication 527 (2025), Table 2-1: 27.5 years under GDS. Say "the building", not the land.
