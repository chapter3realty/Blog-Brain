# Fact ledger: /submarkets/little-river/

Batch 2026-11-a, Lane U. Researcher: research helper, 2026-11-02 (pulls ran 2026-11-02 between 03:40 and 05:00 UTC). **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** Columns 9 and 10 are for a verifier who did none of this research.

Raw files, the scripts that pulled them and the script that computes every derived number are in the website working copy at `data/2026-11-a/submarkets-little-river/` (README there). `findings.json` holds every derived number; a row that says `findings.json X.Y` means that key. Raw county parcels, address points, deed index rows and listing pins stay outside the repo (they carry addresses or names); `fetch_county.py`, `pull_deeds.py` and `pull_listings.py` rebuild them.

Short names for sources used below:
- **County GIS**: Horry County parcel app, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 20 Zipcodes, 31 Municipalities, 24 Parcels, 22 Addresses, 21 Hydrography, 19 Flood Zones) and https://www.horrycounty.org/gisweb/rest/services/Public (ParksRecreation layer 1 Boat Launch; Communities layer 0).
- **The ZIP**: the county's ZIP code polygon for 29566 (layer 20, CITY "Little River"), 30.9 square miles. This is the area the page means by "Little River" for county counts, because it matches the mailing address on a listing, HUD's rents and FEMA's policy data. Census numbers use the Census Bureau's Little River CDP (10.7 square miles), and each row says which.
- **Home**: a parcel with a county land-use code starting with 1 and a building value above 0 (the rule of the Conway and 2026-10-d ledgers). Location: the parcel's county address point, or a point inside the parcel outline when it has none (4,174 of 15,613, mostly condo units). **Kind**: condo = land use 107 or 135, or HPR or UNIT in the legal description; manufactured = land use 120, 121, 122 or 130; townhouse = 109; house = 100, 101, 102, 110, 126; other = the rest (rural tracts and the like). The county publishes no land-use key; the codes were read from samples in earlier ledgers (2026-10-b neighborhoods, 2026-10-d out-of-state buyers) and checked here against the listings (row 8).
- **Boat slip**: a parcel with county land use 306. All 322 in the county carry a legal description naming a marina and a slip (for example "LIGHTKEEPERS VLG HPR; SLIP O-4").
- **On the water**: a home whose parcel outline comes within 15 meters of the county's water outline (layer 21: the Intracoastal Waterway, the Waccamaw River, the Little River and three creeks). A home on a small creek or marsh the county does not outline is not counted.
- **High-risk zone**: FEMA's Special Flood Hazard Area as the county carries it (layer 19, SFHA_TF = 'T'), tested at the home's point.
- **Deeds (homes)**: the kept home sales of batch 2026-10-d (Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb, DEED 001, recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, 13,194 home sales countywide; method in `batches/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/facts.md` row 1), in `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json`. A sale is in Little River when its parcel is a home in the ZIP. "Resale" = the seller is not on that ledger's builder list.
- **Deeds (slips)**: this researcher's own pull of the same AcclaimWeb index for the same dates (30,339 DEED 001 rows, `pull_deeds.py`), kept when the deed names one slip parcel, is $1,000 or more, and is not a tax sale or marked corrective or re-recorded.
- **Listings**: the CRM's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?city=Little%20River&type=<type>, every price band until each search returned every home, pulled 2026-11-02 03:54 UTC; counted in aggregate only (CCMLS data; no address, firm or photo kept).
