3. **An investor or landlord.** "little river sc rentals / long term rentals / monthly rentals / under $1000", "townhomes", "airbnb", "short term rentals". A smaller group; the page answers them in one section and links to the investor pages.

Also typed often, and answered in one line each: "little river sc county" and "is little river incorporated / is little river a city or town", "zip code", "how far is little river from myrtle beach / the beach / the airport / north myrtle beach / sunset beach / ocean isle", "little river vs calabash", "property tax", "population", "schools", "is little river safe / crime rate", "casino boat", "blue crab festival", "things to do", "weather".

The reason behind the top searches (CRAFT 1, "ask why this person typed it"):
- "homes for sale ... no hoa" and "on the water" are typed by someone who wants to keep a boat or a truck and wants to know what Little River gives a boater that the beach towns do not. Answer: where the water homes, deeded slips and public ramps are, what a slip costs, and how few houses touch the water.
- "is little river a city" and "little river sc county" are typed by someone who saw "Little River" on a listing and does not know who runs it. Answer: it is not a town; Horry County sets the rules and the tax; a small corner of the ZIP code is inside North Myrtle Beach.
- "little river sc flooding" is typed by someone who likes a house near the waterway and remembers a hurricane on the news. Answer: most homes are outside FEMA's high-risk zone, which ones are in it, what a policy costs, and Hazel in 1954.
- "mobile homes" and "manufactured homes" are typed by a buyer on a budget. Answer: how many there are, that each sits on its own lot, and what they sell for.
