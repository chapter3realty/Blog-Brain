# Shot list: /submarkets/little-river/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight. No person as the subject. No readable house number, license plate, boat registration number or listing sign; a business sign may be in the frame but not the subject. Landscape, and a square crop of each if he can. Each photo becomes ours once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.
