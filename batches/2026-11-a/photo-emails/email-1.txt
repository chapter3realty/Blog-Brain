The rules, every time

- Never take a photo while driving. Park first, somewhere legal and safe.
- Only from public roads, sidewalks and parking, or a place you are allowed to be for this. Never use a delivery to get inside a gate. A gated community is photographed from outside the gate unless the HOA or the sales office says yes first.
- No faces, no readable license plates, no house or unit numbers. Wait for people to leave the frame.
- A sign may be in the shot, small, but never the whole subject.
- Phone sideways (landscape), the normal camera, no filters, no portrait mode, no zoom past 2x. Morning or the hour before sunset is best, with the sun behind you.
- Three of each shot.
- Send the full-size originals, in a shared Google Drive folder or as attachments, one folder per place, with one line per photo: what it shows and where you stood.

When you send them, please reply once in writing that you took the photos and give Chapter3 Realty permission to use them on its website and in its marketing. Also tell us how you want to be credited: by your name, or as "Chapter3 Realty" (the default).
