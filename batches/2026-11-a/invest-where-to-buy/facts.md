Rows that lean on a sibling ledger's verified row say so; the verifier checks the sibling's status as well as the source.

Data: website working copy `data/2026-11-a/invest-where-to-buy/` (see its `README.md`). `findings.json` holds every derived number; `chart-scorecard.json` holds one row per area for the table and charts; `areas.geojson` holds the area outlines and the county line for the map. Build every number on the page from those files, never type it.

**Never on the page, from any row:** an interest rate, a loan payment, a down payment, any address, PIN or owner, a value for any one address or listing (no AVM), an MLS sold price. Sold prices come from county deed and parcel records. Listing counts come from the CRM's public map API in aggregate. No neighborhood is rated safe, no place described by who lives there.

## Short names for sources

- **Areas.** Eleven areas, each one outline (`analyze.py` header): Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Surfside Beach and Conway = city or town limits (Census TIGERweb incorporated places, current vintage); Garden City, Murrells Inlet, Carolina Forest, Socastee and Little River = the Census designated place; Longs = ZIP code area 29568 outside North Myrtle Beach; Pawleys Island and Litchfield = ZIP code area 29585. A home is in an area when its parcel's centre point is inside. Murrells Inlet's Census place holds no Horry County home parcels in this run; it is a Georgetown County area here. Garden City spans both counties.
- **Horry parcels.** Horry County GIS layer 24, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24 , every home parcel with a building (LandUseCode 1xx, MarketImprv > 0): 227,000 parcels, read 2026-11-02 (`fetch_parcels.py`, `fetch_points.py`). House = land use 100, 101, 102, 110 or 126 and not an HPR unit; condo = land use 107 or an HPR (horizontal property regime) legal description; townhouse or villa = 108, 109 or 135. 6 percent = AssessedProp at least 0.05 x TaxableProp. Out of state = OwnerState (the tax bill's mailing state) not SC.
- **Georgetown parcels.** Georgetown County GIS open data, https://gis1.georgetowncountysc.org/portal/rest/services/GCGIS_Export/FeatureServer/2 , box -79.25,33.36,-78.98,33.62, read 2026-11-02 (`fetch_georgetown.py`). House = land use ending 100, 101 or 500; condo = ending 450, 451, 453 or 455; legal residence = land use starting with Q (method of the Pawleys Island ledger, verified there). Sales = the parcel's last recorded sale, dated 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, $10,000 or more.
- **Deeds.** Horry County AcclaimWeb DEED (001) index, home sales recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, from batch 2026-10-d's pull and filters (`data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json`, 13,194 kept home sales, price $10,000 or more, one residential parcel; builder sales flagged). Resale = not a builder sale. Joined to Horry parcels for kind and point.
- **Flood.** Horry County GIS layer 19 (FEMA flood zones), the 758 special flood hazard area polygons (SFHA_TF = 'T': A, AE, AO, VE), https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/19 ; Georgetown County GCGIS_Nature layer 9, same test. A home is in the high-risk zone when its centre point is inside one.
- **CRM.** Chapter3's public map API, https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?type=<house|townhouse|condo>&status=active[&has=str_allowed|rentals_allowed] , split into price bands until every pin came back (568 calls, pulled 2026-11-02 11:48 UTC). API totals: houses 3,690, townhouses 364, condos 2,817; of them str_allowed 450, 89 and 1,977; rentals_allowed 2,949, 294 and 2,472. Pins kept: MLS number, type, price, beds, point only. 14 price bands returned one pin fewer than their total (a pin with no point).
- **HUD.** HUD FY2027 Small Area Fair Market Rents, https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/fmr/fmr2027/FY27_safmrs.xlsx , sheet SAFMRs, extracted to `hud-safmr-fy2027.json`. Gross rent (rent plus tenant-paid utilities), by bedrooms, by ZIP.
- **Levy.** Horry County Auditor, "2026 Horry County Tax Levies", certified 7/27/2026, https://www.horrycountysc.gov/media/kufln4qp/tax-levy-2026_2.pdf (text `raw/src/horry-tax-levy-2026.txt`).
- **GT calc.** Georgetown County Property Tax Calculator, https://www.gtcountysc.gov/385/Property-Tax-Calculator (page script saved `raw/src/gt-tax-calculator.html`).
- **ACS.** Census ACS 2020-2024 5-year, tables B25064 and B25003, via https://data.census.gov/api/access/data/table?id=ACSDT5Y2024.<table>&g=<geo> (`pull_acs.sh`, `raw/acs/`).
- **STR file.** `data/str-market.json`, the site's one source for nightly-rental market figures (vendor AirROI, retrieved 2026-08-15, refresh due 2026-11-13). Owned by /invest/airbnb-income/.
