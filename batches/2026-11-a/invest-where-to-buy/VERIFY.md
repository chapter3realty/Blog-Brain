## Two findings that touch many rows

**A. Murrells Inlet and Garden City outlines.** The current Census place for Murrells Inlet lies 100 percent in Georgetown County (area share), and the Garden City place lies 99.8 percent in Horry County. In `findings.json`, Murrells Inlet holds 0 Horry homes and Garden City holds 0 Georgetown homes. So:
- The ledger's "Garden City spans both counties" and row 16's note "Garden City mixes both counties" are wrong for this page. Every Garden City figure here is Horry-side.
- "Murrells Inlet" on this page is the Georgetown side only (3,855 homes). /submarkets/murrells-inlet/ also counts the Horry side (about 8,945 homes in district 610 and 600). Every Murrells Inlet figure (rows 14, 16, 17, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25) needs the label "Murrells Inlet, Georgetown County side (Census place)", or the sibling page's figures will clash.
- Row 10's "the south end of Garden City" is not in this page's Garden City outline.

**B. Georgetown sales are not resales.** Georgetown's table has no builder flag, so Murrells Inlet and Pawleys Island and Litchfield medians are every recorded last sale, builders' first sales included. Do not call them "resold".
