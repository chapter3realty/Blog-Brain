- **The Murrells Inlet mailing area**: every home parcel whose inside point lies in the ZIP 29576 polygon of Horry GIS layer 20 (the layer covers both counties; its record reads ZIPCODE "29576", CITY "Murrells Inlet"). **Garden City is counted apart**: Horry parcels inside the 2020 Garden City Census place (GEOID 4528455) and Georgetown parcels east of US 17 Business and more than 700 m from it (the Georgetown end of the Garden City beach strip, across the inlet). The Garden City page owns those homes. "Murrells Inlet" below means the mailing area without Garden City, unless a row says otherwise.
- **Parts** (our split, `zones.py`): **Horry side** = Horry County, all of it west of the US 17 bypass (east of the bypass in Horry is Garden City). **Georgetown, west** = west of the US 17 bypass. **Georgetown, between the roads** = between the bypass and US 17 Business. **Georgetown, creek side** = east of US 17 Business, on the inlet. **On the marsh** = a parcel within 30 m of a National Wetlands Inventory estuarine polygon (code starting E) in Georgetown GIS Nature 0.
- **Home, house, condo**: Horry: land-use code starting 1 with a building value above 0; 101 = house; 107 or "HPR" in the legal = condo; other 1xx = other (villas, townhouses, manufactured). The 2026-10-b, 2026-10-d and Garden City ledgers use the same rule. Georgetown: code ending 100 or 101 with a 2024 county footprint = house; ending 500 = other (manufactured); 450 to 456 with a unit word in the legal = condo (the Pawleys Island ledger's rule, widened to 452 and 456).
- **Deeds (Horry)**: Horry County Register of Deeds, AcclaimWeb DEED (001), recorded 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30, with the kept-sale rules of the 2026-10-d out-of-state-buyers ledger (one residential parcel with a building, $10,000 or more; `data/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/out-of-state-buyers.json`, which ships in `batches/2026-10-d/sell-out-of-state-buyers/web.patch`). **Sales (Georgetown)**: the parcel layer's last sale date and price (the Pawleys Island ledger's method, verified there): a sale in the window at $10,000 or more. A parcel sold twice counts once.
