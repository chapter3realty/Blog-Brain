## The deciding fact (checked first)

"Murrells Inlet" is two places on the map. The Census Bureau's Murrells Inlet is all in Georgetown County. The Murrells Inlet mailing address (ZIP 29576) runs north across the county line into Horry County, and most of its homes (outside Garden City) are on the Horry side. The county line, not the name on the address, decides the tax bill, the schools and the rental rules. Ledger rows 1 to 4.
