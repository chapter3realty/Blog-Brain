## The deciding fact (checked first)

The page's spine is "the dock decides the value". Two things must hold: (a) a dock is not a right that comes with a waterfront lot, but needs a state permit the lot may not qualify for (rows 1 to 4); and (b) the water and the dock show in the price (rows 20 to 24). Both hold. (a) is law (R.30-12, SC Code 48-39). (b) holds in county deeds for the water itself (houses on the waterway sold for about twice the houses within a kilometre). For the dock alone the only evidence is asking prices (row 24), and it is modest; the writer must not say a dock adds a set dollar amount.
