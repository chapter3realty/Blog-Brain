# Shot list: /buyers/waterfront-homes/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight, landscape, and a square crop of each if he can. Shoot only from public land, a public landing or the water. No person as the subject. No readable house number, license plate, boat registration number or listing sign. A business sign may be in the frame but not the subject. Each photo becomes ours once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.

Today the page has stand-ins only: public-domain aerials from the sibling pages (Cherry Grove channels, the Socastee bridges, Barefoot Landing, Murrells Inlet creek docks, Garden City Point canals, Little River harbours) and the Conway riverwalk.
