# Shot list for the photo partner: /hoa/rental-restrictions/

For `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first. Shoot from public roads, sidewalks, public beach accesses or public parking only. No faces, no license plates, no house or unit numbers, no readable association or building name. Landscape, three frames of each.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Section, and the stand-in it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ocean Boulevard, North Myrtle Beach or Myrtle Beach, any block where an oceanfront condo tower stands beside beach houses. | One frame with a condo tower and a beach house in the same view, from the street or a public beach access. | The page's main point in one picture: the tower's papers and the house's papers say different things about renting by the night. | "Do most HOAs allow Airbnb here?": replaces the 2010 Air Force photo. |
| 2 | Any Carolina Forest neighborhood street of houses (public road, no gate). | A street of houses with the neighborhood's mailbox row or entrance sign small in the frame, so it reads as a planned neighborhood. No sign readable. | Shows the kind of neighborhood where 1 of 296 houses for sale says nightly rentals are allowed. | Same section: replaces the NAIP aerial, or sits beside it. |
| 3 | A driveway on a street with no association, anywhere on the Grand Strand. | A boat on a trailer parked beside a house. No plate, no house number, no person. | The boat-parking line: about 1 in 4 houses with no HOA fee list RV or boat parking, against under 2 in 100 with a fee. | "What else can an HOA restrict?": new photo; skip it if the shot only decorates. |
| 4 | The front entrance of an oceanfront resort building, from the public sidewalk. | A "Check-in" or "Registration" sign at the front desk entrance, small in the frame, with no brand logo as the subject. | Fannie Mae counts registration and daily rentals as hotel-type services that make a condo building ineligible for a standard conventional loan. | "Does a condo's rental setup affect the loan?": new photo. |

Do not shoot inside a lobby, a pool deck or any area behind a gate. That needs the association's permission.

Each photo needs the partner's written confirmation that it is ours before it goes on the page (CLAUDE.md, photos). Its licence record goes in the website's `data/2026-11-a/hoa-rental-restrictions/photos.json`, and the files go in `chapter3realty/images/2026-11-a/hoa-rental-restrictions/`.
