- **Main home**: assessed at the 4 percent ratio (AssessedProp / TaxableProp = 0.04). Everything at 6 percent is a second home, rental or other non-residence.

## Not verified

1. **Rentals between 30 days and one year in R1 and R2.** Sec. 17-396.10 defines short-term (under 30 days) and long-term (365 days or more) and names no rule for the months between. A winter monthly rental on an R1 street is not clearly allowed or barred. Tried: the code text (row 1), the town FAQ /419, the business licence guide. The page must not say monthly rentals are allowed in R1. Put it in the offer: we get the town's answer for the street before you count on it.
2. **Ocean Lakes lease terms and yearly lot rent.** Not on the campground's public pages (ol-faqs, ol-annual-lease-sales). The page can say what the buyer owns (row 34) and that a yearly site lease comes with it; not the amount or the term.
3. **Golf cart hours under Ord. 25-0990.** The minutes (row 19) prove passage and that the debate was about 24-hour running; news reports say carts with permanent lights may run at any hour and carts without lights only from 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset (WMBF, 2025-08-27). The ordinance text was not found (Municode 403; not on the town's ordinance page, which lists 2026 ordinances only). News is not a source. Do not state hours.
4. **Short-term rental permit and safety inspection.** The judge's note and the old page say the town requires an STR permit and a safety inspection. The town's pages show a business licence per rental (row 35) and a Code Clearance form with a $150 "new business inspection" and a fire inspection for businesses; the form has a "Landlord Business License?" box but does not say it applies to each rental. Not stated on the page until the town confirms.
5. **Planned developments (PD).** 447 homes are in PD districts, whose uses are set by each development's approval. Whether any PD allows nightly rentals was not checked. The map labels PD "check the parcel".
6. **Schools.** Horry County Schools' attendance-zone lookup is a script page; no school names for the town were verified. The old page's school statistics had no source. The page says Horry County Schools and that we look up the assigned schools for the address.
7. **Wikimedia Commons photo search.** The Commons API returned HTTP 429 (rate limit) for every call this session, and the fetch tool cannot reach commons.wikimedia.org. Photo candidates in PLAN.md come from records already on the site and from public-domain government imagery.
