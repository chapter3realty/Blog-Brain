# Shot list for the photo partner: /hoa/documents/

For `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first. Shoot from public roads, sidewalks or public parking only. No faces, no license plates, no house or unit numbers. Landscape, three frames of each.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not shoot inside a condo building, its lobby or its mail room. That needs the association's permission, and a notice board can carry owners' names.

Each photo needs the partner's written confirmation that it is ours before it goes on the page (CLAUDE.md, photos). Its licence record goes in the website's `data/2026-11-a/hoa-documents/photos.json`, and the files go in `chapter3realty/images/2026-11-a/hoa-documents/`.
