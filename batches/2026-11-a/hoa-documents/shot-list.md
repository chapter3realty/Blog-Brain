# Shot list for the photo partner: /hoa/documents/

For `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first. All from public roads, sidewalks or public parking. No faces, no license plates, no house numbers, no unit numbers. Landscape. Three frames of each.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Section it goes in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Horry County Government and Justice Center, 1301 Second Avenue, Conway. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1301+2nd+Ave+Conway+SC+29526) | The entrance used by the public, from the parking lot, with any sign that says "Register of Deeds" readable and small in the frame. A second frame of the whole front. No people. | The county keeps the recorded HOA papers here, and its public viewing area is the only place the pages can be read. Our only free photo is from 2006. Same shot also serves the liens page (its shot 1); one trip covers both. | "Are HOA documents public record in Horry County?" |
| 2 | Chapter3's own office, at a desk. | A printed stack of HOA papers on a desk, with no association name, address or person readable: a thick bound declaration, a budget page with the numbers too small to read, sticky tabs on the pages. Owner's office or the partner's own desk. | Shows the size of a real packet next to the copy-cost example. Our own staged photo; no listing or building is shown. | "Which HOA documents should you get?" (optional; skip if it only decorates) |

Do not shoot inside a condo building, its lobby or its mail room. That needs the association's permission, and a notice board can carry owners' names.

Every photo needs the partner's written confirmation that it is ours before it goes on the page (CLAUDE.md, photos). A licence record goes in the website's `data/2026-11-a/hoa-documents/photos.json`, and the files go in `chapter3realty/images/2026-11-a/hoa-documents/`.
