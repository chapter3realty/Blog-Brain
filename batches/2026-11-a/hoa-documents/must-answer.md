## The reader

A buyer under contract, or about to be, on a Grand Strand condo, townhouse or house in an association. Often from out of state (IRS migration: NC, NY, NJ, PA, FL, MD, VA send the most). Often expects the law back home: a resale packet that arrives by law and a few days to cancel. Worried about rules that block their plan (renting, a boat, a golf cart, a pet) and about money surprises (an assessment, thin savings). Next question: "what do I do if I find something bad?"

The reason behind the search: they want to know what can go wrong in these papers before they lose the right to walk away.

## The deciding fact

**South Carolina gives a buyer no resale packet by law and no cancel right tied to the HOA papers.** The only state duty is the seller's one-page disclosure addendum before the contract, and missing it does not void the contract. A buyer's protection is the contract's own due diligence period. Every other answer on the page follows from this. (Ledger rows 3 to 7.)
