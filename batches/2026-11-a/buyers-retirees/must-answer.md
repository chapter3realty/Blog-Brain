## The deciding fact

South Carolina's income tax changed for 2026 (Act 110, signed March 30, 2026). The state now starts from federal adjusted gross income, drops the federal standard and senior deductions, and gives its own deduction (SCIAD) that shrinks as income rises. The retirement and age-65 deductions and the military deduction stay. Under these rules a married couple, both 66, with $40,000 of Social Security and a $30,000 pension owes no South Carolina income tax for 2026 (facts 1 to 11). The live page's tax section predates the law and must be rewritten. Checked first, before anything else.
