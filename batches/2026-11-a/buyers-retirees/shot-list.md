# Shot list for the photo partner: /buyers/retirees/

For the owner to send from his Gmail (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Shoot from public roads, sidewalks and parking lots open to the public. No people as the subject, no faces up close, no signs or logos as the subject, no house numbers or license plates readable. Landscape, the widest the camera allows, plus one vertical of each if easy. Morning or late-afternoon light.
