# Shot list: /submarkets/pawleys-island/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight. No person as the subject. No readable house number, license plate, listing sign, or business sign as the subject. Shoot from public roads, the causeways and public beach accesses only. Landscape, plus a square crop of each if he can. Each photo becomes ours once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.

In page order. Today the page has two US government aerials (public domain), our two maps, and one Commons photo of a walkway from a house. Each shot below replaces a stand-in or fills a section that has no photo.
