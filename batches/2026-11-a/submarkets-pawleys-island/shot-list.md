# Shot list: /submarkets/pawleys-island/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Daylight. No person as the subject. No readable house number, license plate, listing sign, or a business sign as the subject. Shoot from public roads, causeways and public beach accesses only. Landscape, and a square crop of each if he can. Each photo becomes ours once he confirms in writing that Chapter3 may use it.

In page order. The live page has no photo.
