Short names for sources used below:
- **County GIS**: Georgetown County GIS open data, https://gis1.georgetowncountysc.org/portal/rest/services (read 2026-11-02): Boundaries 1 (Incorporated Municipalities) and 2 (Tax District); Export 2 (GC_PARCEL: land-use code, owner mailing city and state, last sale date and price, legal description); Export 0 (E911 addresses); Export 1 (roads); Buildings 1 and 5 (2024 and 2017 building footprints; 2024 has a `status` field); Nature 9 (FEMA flood zones, DFIRM 45043C), Nature 6 and 7 (SCDHEC OCRM 2018 baseline and setback lines), Nature 0 (USFWS wetlands).
- **Study area**: the envelope 79.22 W to 79.03 W, 33.36 N to 33.53 N (Litchfield's north end to DeBordieu, ocean to the Waccamaw River).
- **Zones** (our split, `analyze.py`): **island** = parcel's inside point in the Town of Pawleys Island polygon; **Litchfield** = east of US 17 ("OCEAN HWY") from 33.447 N (the north tip of the town) to 33.505 N; **behind the island** = east of US 17, south of 33.447 N, not the island, not DeBordieu; **west of US 17** = west of US 17, 33.38 N to 33.505 N; **DeBordieu** = legal description names DEBORDIEU. Island parts by where the causeways land: **north** of 33.4340 N (North Causeway), **middle**, **south** of 33.4245 N (South Causeway).
- **House**: a parcel whose land-use code ends 100, 101 or 500 and holds a 2024 county building footprint (footprint's inside point in the parcel). Codes ending 500 are used on the island and the Litchfield beach strip; 131 island parcels coded 500 have no building (dune and marsh strips, part-lots) and are not houses.
- **Legal residence (taxed at 4 percent)**: land-use code starting with Q. This is our reading of the code; no county legend was found online (qPublic returned 403). Evidence in row 8.
- **Market sale**: a last recorded deed of $10,000 or more. **Nominal transfer**: a last deed under $1,000 (the county field shows $0, $1, $5, $10 and the like). The county table keeps only the **last** deed on each parcel, so a house sold and later deeded to a trust shows only the trust deed: every "market sale" count is a floor.
- **Sale windows**: 12 months = 2025-10-01 to 2026-09-30 (the window of the sibling ledgers); 3 years = 2023-10-01 to 2026-09-30; 5 years = since 2021-11-01.
- **Census**: 2020 Census P.L. 94-171 (P1, H1) and DHC (H5 vacancy status, P13 median age), 2010 SF1, through https://data.census.gov/api/access/data/table?id=<table>&g=<geo>. Places: Pawleys Island town 4555015 (TIGERweb Places layer 25), Litchfield Beach CDP 4541830, DeBordieu Colony CDP 4518810; ZCTA 29585; Georgetown County 45043.
- **Town**: Town of Pawleys Island website, https://www.townofpawleysisland.com/<page>/ (read 2026-11-02; text saved in `raw/town/*.txt`).
- **CRM**: https://chapter3realty.com/homes/api/map?city=Pawleys%20Island[&type=..] (MLS data counted in aggregate only), pulled 2026-11-02.
