## Verifier (independent, 2026-11-04)

The verifier did not research these rows. He downloaded and read the three sources himself: A03.pdf (Art. III), A04.pdf (Art. IV) and man26/35_Rules-House.pdf (2026 manual, House rules).

| id | status | what the verifier read |
|---|---|---|
| NEW-1 | VERIFIED | Art. III Sec. 11: "a majority of each house shall constitute a quorum to do business". Sec. 18: "No Bill or Joint Resolution shall have the force of law until it shall have been read three times and on three several days in each house". Both exact. Sec. 18 goes on: either house "may provide by rule for a first and third reading ... by its title only" (House Rule 5.8 does this for third reading). The bill is still read three times, so the claim stands. |
| NEW-2 | VERIFIED (with its condition) | House Rule 1.6: "If the House be equally divided, the question shall be decided in the negative." Rule 5.7: "the question shall be the passage of the bill. Upon a decision in the affirmative ..." Both exact. 124 members, a 62-62 tie fails, so 63 if all 124 vote. The 63 holds only when all 124 vote: with fewer present (a quorum is 63), fewer yes votes pass a bill. Every sentence that uses 63 must keep "if all members vote". |
| NEW-3 | VERIFIED (with its condition) | Art. IV Sec. 21: "If after such reconsideration two-thirds of that house shall agree to pass it, it shall be sent, together with the objections, to the other house, by which it shall be reconsidered, and if approved by two-thirds of that house it shall have the same effect as if it had been signed by the Governor". Exact. 124 x 2/3 = 82.67, so 83; 46 x 2/3 = 30.67, so 31. Same condition as NEW-2: "with all members voting". Also in Sec. 21: a bill not returned within five days, Sundays excepted, has "the same force and effect as if he had signed it". So a bill does not need the governor's signature to become law. |
| NEW-4 | CORRECTED | Arithmetic from F011 (79 R) and F014 (D27 R lead, D89 D lead) is right for one flip: D27 alone gives 78, D89 alone gives 80. But "if either" leaves out both flipping, which gives 79. Correct claim: "If one recount changed the result, Republicans would hold 78 or 80 seats; if both did, 79. Each is more than 63 and fewer than 83." Assumes no other race changes before certification. |
