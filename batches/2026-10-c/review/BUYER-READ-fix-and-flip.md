## Answers

- **What I wanted first:** where to buy and what a flip makes there. The gain: first screen. Where: never.
- **Three things I liked:** real local resale numbers; the calculator that told me 82% is too tight; the tax trap (about three times what the last owner paid).
- **Still open, ranked:**
  1. Which towns or areas are worth flipping in, at what price?
  2. Who does and watches the work from 600 miles away, and what does it cost here?
  3. Insurance and flood costs on a flip near the coast.
  4. Why companies do better; should I buy in an LLC?
  5. What Chapter3 charges me, and how many investor flips it has bought and sold.
  6. Can I use my own hard money lender?
- **Would I call?** I'd ask for the free flip list, but call only once I knew who handles the work while I'm in Ohio.

**I came for which parts of the Grand Strand are worth flipping in, and the page never told me.**
