## How they were made

- **Facts:** every fact comes from a public record or listing, and a second agent that did not find it checked it. More than 500 facts were checked. Every one found wrong was corrected or taken out.
- **Cold readers:** five readers who had never seen the pages each read one as the buyer who would search for it. Each wrote what they came for and did not find. Every one of those gaps is now answered.
- **Reviews:** five independent reviews graded the pages as you would. All five pages now reach 9 of 10.
- **Fair housing:** no page describes a place by who lives there, and no page calls a place safe or unsafe.
