## How they were made

- **Your notes of 2026-10-14, applied across all five:** each place told one at a time; the first offer is a real box; life before price; no repeated sentences or one fact in every entry; no filler; "we check", never "you should check"; real roads and communities, not towns.
- **Facts:** every fact comes from a public record, the MLS or the place's own site, and a second agent that did not find it checked it. More than 700 facts were checked. When a fact was wrong, the corrected wording went on a list that the build now refuses to print again.
- **New gates from this batch:** a page fails if an entry is thin, if a fact a checker struck comes back, if a distance word has no measurement ("a short walk" must be under a quarter mile), or if a photo's labels do not sit on the place they name.
- **Reviews:** independent reviewers graded the pages as you would, over seven rounds. Four reached 9 of 10 at the last review; the gated page had its last fixes after it, and a checker confirmed them.
- **Fair housing:** no page describes a place by who lives there, and no page calls a place safe or unsafe.
- **One fix to a live page:** the Seasons at Prince Creek West page said the beach is about 10 minutes away. That ended at a walkover with no parking. It now says about 13 minutes to the nearest access with parking, on the Seasons page, its map, and the 55+ comparison table on all four 55+ pages.
