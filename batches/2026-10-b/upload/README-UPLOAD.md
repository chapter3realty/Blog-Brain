It also changes pages the site already has:

- `/buyers/first-time-home-buyer-myrtle-beach/` and `/buyers/relocating/which-town/` (with its spec, `data/relocating/pages/which-town.js`): a link to the affordable places page.

In the golf page's comparison table, the gate cells for Prestwick and Wachesaw Plantation now read "Gatehouse at the entrance" and Wachesaw East "Gate at the entrance" (they said a live guard around the clock, 24-hour security and security; no verified source shows staffing). Pawleys Plantation's cell no longer says "Gated" (no verified source shows it gated), and the HOA notes for Prestwick and Wachesaw East no longer list a guard or security.
- `/submarkets/carolina-forest/`: a link to The Farm at Carolina Forest.
- `sitemap.xml`: the five pages, with their photos (Google image sitemap). The four buyer pages are listed after the buyers hub, The Farm after Carolina Forest.
- `llms.txt`: the five pages, with their descriptions.
- `llms-full.txt`: regenerated by `node build.js llmsfull`, so it carries the five pages' text.
- The four 55+ pages (`/buyers/55-plus-communities/` Del Webb North Myrtle Beach, Del Webb at Grande Dunes, Myrtle Trace, Seasons at Prince Creek West), rebuilt from their specs: Seasons' drive to the beach is about 13 minutes, not 10. The old figure ended at a Garden City walkover with no parking; 13 minutes is to GARDEN CITY 45, the nearest access with a lot (gated ledger row 176). On the Seasons page: the at-a-glance row, the FAQ answer, the map's beach dot and label; on all four, the Seasons row of the comparison table. Nothing else on those pages changes.
- `tools/mkpage.js`: `mkpage-offer-box.patch`, the first offer as the same navy band as the last when a spec gives it a title. No live page changes.
- `build.js`: `build-place-entries.patch`, a numbered run of 3 or more place headings is not counted as section questions (audit rule A22). No live page's result changes.
- `tools/blog-brain-2026-10-b/` (new): the Blog-Brain page tools this batch was built and reviewed with (`page-kit.js`, `compare.js` and the files they load). `tools/blog-brain/`, which batch 2026-10-a's pages load, is not changed, so a rebuild of those pages gives the same HTML as today (its map data is older than this batch's).

## If a step fails

- **`git apply --check` fails.** The live branch changed after this package was built (it was built on 39036014). Nothing has been changed yet. In Blog-Brain, rebuild the package on a fresh clone: `node tools/package-batch.js batches/2026-10-b --site <fresh clone>`. Do not edit the patch.
- **`mkpage` says "Blog-Brain tools not found".** `site\tools\blog-brain-2026-10-b` was not copied. Run the Copy-Item line again.
- **`check`, `audit` or `preflight` fails.** Do not push. `git reset --hard` and `git clean -fd` put the clone back as it was; send the output to Blog-Brain.
