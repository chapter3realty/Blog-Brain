# Fact ledger: /buyers/second-home/

Buying a second home in Myrtle Beach. Reader lives out of state, wants a beach place for vacations, and may rent it when away.

Researcher: subagent, round 17. Every URL below was opened on 2026-10-26 unless the row says otherwise. Raw copies are in `scratchpad/r17/sh/` and `scratchpad/r17/zdl/`. The writer may use only rows a verifier marks `verified`.

**Deciding fact (4c).** Fannie Mae treats a home as a second home only if the borrower occupies it part of the year, has exclusive control, and does not use rental income to qualify (rows A1 to A8). A home bought mainly to rent is an investment property under the same guide. The page can be written as planned.

## Conflicts with existing pages

1. **/invest/str-rules/** says Pawleys Island "is the only coastal town here with no business license at all". The town's 2023 plan says it collects business license tax on insurance, telecommunications and brokers, and on nothing else (B14). "No business license for rentals" is accurate; "no business license at all" is not.
2. **/invest/str-rules/** says unincorporated Georgetown County "treats short-term and long-term rentals as the same residential use and has chosen not to regulate them". The county ordinance defines a "tourist home" (30 days or less) and names it only in the RC and GRR districts (B15, B16). No county text I opened says that STRs and long-term rentals are treated the same.
3. **/invest/str-rules/** says Myrtle Beach violations are "up to $500 a day". The city says "up to $500 and/or 30 days per occurrence" (B4).
