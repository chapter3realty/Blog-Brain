Verifier: independent, 2026-10-15. Every URL opened by curl; every OSRM drive re-run (public router, no traffic). Ledger and struck list not edited. Drive rounding rule (CRAFT 2): under 15 minutes to the nearest minute, longer to the nearest 5, measured to where the reader arrives.

## Row 202: verified with corrected wording

Opened: SCDNR news release (https://www.dnr.sc.gov/news/2025/Jun/jun10-lewisoceanbay.php); OSM node 5816707749 and the OSM map around it; OSRM; web search for a second source.

Found (verbatim): "Lewis Ocean Bay contains the largest assemblage of Carolina Bays in the state." "Today, the largest and only long-term viable population in South Carolina is centered within Lewis Ocean Bay." "SCDNR recently acquired 353 acres from Conway Medical Center. The tract is located across International Drive from the existing preserve." "it will be open to the public for outdoor recreational activities." The release is the owner agency's own, so it is primary. Matches Farm ledger rows 99 to 100 (same release, same words). No disagreement.

Drive: Sawgrass East (-78.929975, 33.756167) to the board: 1,006.8 s = 16.78 min, 12,231 m = 7.60 mi. Reproduced exactly. 16.78 rounds to 15 (nearest 5). Arrival point: OSM node 5816707749 is an information board named for the preserve; OSM parking node 10804601724 (surface parking) is 10 m from it. OSRM snaps to International Drive 19 m away. So it is where a reader arrives.

Problems:
1. "about 15 minutes up International Drive": the route is Carolina Forest Blvd 4.6 km, Windmill Way 0.8, Carolina Farms Blvd 0.4, Wheatfield Dr 0.6, then International Drive 4.8 km of 12.2 km. Only 40% is on International Drive. "Up International Drive" describes the road the preserve is on, not the drive.
2. Superlative "the largest group of Carolina bays": one source (SCDNR). Searches returned only the same release and pages repeating it. Needs two sources or softening.
3. Flytraps: source says "the largest and only long-term viable population", with no "wild". A second source (Coastal Conservation League, "only stable population", seen in search summary only, not opened) agrees in kind. "Largest" has the agency plus the conservation league saying only-viable, so it holds; drop "wild" (not in the source).

Safe wording:
- "Lewis Ocean Bay Heritage Preserve is about 15 minutes by car from Sawgrass East, on International Drive [OSRM, no traffic; arrival at the preserve's board and parking on International Drive]."
- "It holds one of the largest groups of Carolina bays in the state [SCDNR, June 2025; the agency calls it 'the largest assemblage']." (Writer may use "the largest" only if a second source is added.)
- "South Carolina's largest Venus flytrap population, and the only one that is long-term viable, is centered there [SCDNR, June 2025]."

## Row 204: verified with corrected wording

Opened: Lennar Woodhaven (.../birchwood/woodhaven/31138600054) and Gerson (.../gerson/31138600052) plan pages.

Found, both pages identical: "Brand-new stainless steel appliances and designer-selected cabinetry lend efficiency to the kitchen"; "Granite countertops grace the kitchen and center island"; "9' smooth finish ceiling on first floor"; "Low-E insulated and energy-efficient windows". Woodhaven is marked "Future release".

Problems:

2. "Lennar's houses here come with ...": the lists are per plan, two plans read; the source itself defers to the purchase agreement. Page says it without the limit.

Safe wording: "Lennar's plan pages for Birchwood list granite kitchen counters, stainless steel appliances, 9-foot ceilings and energy-efficient windows. The purchase agreement lists the features in each house [Lennar plan pages, Woodhaven and Gerson]."

## Row 207: verified with corrected wording

Opened: https://www.mbgyc.com/ (HOA home page) and https://www.wmbfnews.com/story/26752147/another-golf-course-announces-its-closing/ (WMBF, Oct. 10, 2014).

Found, HOA: "we relax and enjoy our pool, tennis courts, pavilion, HOA community center"; "Although we no longer have an on-site golf course or yacht club"; "Pool & Hot Tub will be closed October 15 for the winter months -- Pool Opens April 15"; "8:00-9:00 am - Lap Swimming (7 days a week) 9:00-10:00 am - Exercise (7 days a week) 10:00 am-8:00 pm or Sundown - Open Swim (7 days a week)"; "Community Watch Desk Coverage is 24 Hours. Seven days a week Gatehouse Phone: (843) 650-4109".
Found, WMBF: "The Heron Point Golf Club announced the closure of its course to local residents." "saying the management of the course has decided to close and they will be closed by December."

Problems:
1. "Inside the gate today": the HOA page says nothing about a gate in connection with the amenities. The pool and courts being inside the gate is not shown. (Gated ledger row 175 already treats gate coverage as not shown for Sandpiper Place.)
2. "keeps a watch desk at the gatehouse around the clock": the source says "Community Watch Desk Coverage is 24 Hours" and lists a gatehouse phone. It does not say the desk is at the gatehouse or that it is staffed. This is the same overstatement the gated verifier struck in gated row 259 ("staffed", "at its gatehouse"). The pages disagree on nothing else, but the affordable page repeats the struck wording.
3. "open swim until dark": the source is "10:00 am-8:00 pm or Sundown". Not the same as dark.
4. "Heron Point, which closed in 2014": WMBF (October 2014) reports the announcement that the course "will be closed by December". It does not report that it closed. The HOA says there is no on-site course now, undated. Together they support "announced it would close in December 2014" and "no longer has a course".
Also: the pool is "in season" (April 15 to October 15): fine.

Safe wording:
- "Heron Point Golf Club, the community's own course, announced in October 2014 that it would close by December [WMBF, Oct. 10, 2014]. The HOA says the community no longer has an on-site golf course [HOA website]."
- "The HOA lists a pool and hot tub, tennis courts, a pavilion and a community center [HOA website]."
- "The HOA lists Community Watch Desk coverage 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and a gatehouse phone line [HOA website]."
- "The pool and hot tub are open from April 15 to October 15, with lap swimming 8 to 9 a.m., exercise 9 to 10, and open swim from 10 until 8 p.m. or sundown [HOA website]."
