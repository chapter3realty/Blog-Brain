# Round 14 research: Carolina Forest and Socastee

Researcher, round 14, 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-15. Most new rows come from three kinds of sources. The Horry County deed index (AcclaimWeb comments search, exported to CSV) gave plats, developer sales and transfers to the associations. The Myrtle Beach Golf and Yacht Club HOA's own public website gave the gate, pool and calendar. Two WMBF stories and one Play Golf Myrtle Beach story covered the old Heron Point course. Deed images are not online ("Horry County does not provide Images of Documents to be viewed via the Internet"), so every deed row rests on the index line only: date, book and page, parties, consideration and memo.

None of the four Carolina Forest and Socastee communities has a public HOA website, except the Golf and Yacht Club. The sites named "waterwaypalmsplantation.com" and "waterwaypalmsmb.com" belong to real estate agents. They were not used.

## Waterway Palms Plantation

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** On one day, November 17, 2004, the developer recorded 145 lot sales here at $84,880 to $334,880 (row 240). After the 2008 crash, many lots went through court-ordered sales from 2010 to 2012 (row 242). That boom and bust explains why builders are still finishing houses here 20 years later. Some owners have joined two lots into one homesite (row 245).
**A normal day (notes):** Boat owners use the HOA's 2.3-acre boat ramp parcel and its separate 2-acre fenced boat storage lot (row 244). Everyone else uses the clubhouse on the lagoon (rows 97 and 155, already verified).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** Lots have been sold, foreclosed and resold since 2004, so some streets still have empty lots and houses under construction (rows 241 and 242).

## The Bluffs on the Waterway

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** The Bluffs is older than the page says. Lots were first sold on August 22, 2001, by a North Carolina company, The Bluffs on the Waterway LLC (row 247). On the same day, a "Dock Master Plan" for The Bluffs was recorded (row 248). Most lots were sold by 2003 (row 250).
**A normal day (notes):** Boat owners use the ramp, day docks and boathouse clubhouse (rows 98 and 120, already in the ledger). The deed index only shows that a dock plan was recorded; its contents are not online.
**Honest trade-off (notes):** S.C. 31 runs along one side; a 2023 county zoning case put The Bluffs 370 feet from a proposed brewery site across the highway (row 253).

## Covington Lake East

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** In 2023 Horry County recorded two trail easements across land owned by the Covington Lake East owners association (row 256). After the 2008 downturn, the developer bought lots back from a bank in 2011, then sold a block of lots to Lennar in 2014 for $1,350,000 (rows 254 and 255).
**A normal day (notes):** Nothing new from a primary source. Rows 148 and 177 (already in the ledger) remain the best description.
**Honest trade-off (notes):** Primary sources say little about Covington Lake East itself. It has no public HOA site, and the news names "Covington Lake" or "Covington Lakes", not East (see Not verified).

## Myrtle Beach Golf and Yacht Club

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** The name outlived the club. The HOA says "we no longer have an on-site golf course or yacht club" (row 257). The course was the Heron Point Golf Club, a Willard Byrd par-72 layout. On its 15th hole, "legend has it John Daly once drove the green with a 3-wood" (row 265). It closed in November 2014 (row 266). That year, the association bought two tracts of the course land from the golf club (row 267).
**A normal day (notes):** A Community Watch desk at the gatehouse is staffed 24 hours a day, seven days a week (row 259). The pool has lap swim at 8 and an exercise hour at 9 from mid-April to mid-October (row 261). The calendar has bingo, yoga, Zumba, mahjong, a book club and card groups (row 262).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** The golf course is gone. A 2016 news report described the closed course as overgrown (row 266). The houses D.R. Horton is building in Sandpiper Place have their own association (row 269).

If a verifier can open them, they would link Byrd, who later designed Heron Point, to the community's own plan. The finding aid is lib.ncsu.edu/findingaids/mc00356 (not opened).

Horton) are built on the old Heron Point course.** An agent site (myrtlenewhomes.com) says so and gives "155-acre" and "over 80 acres in August 2023". That site is not a source. County parcels place Sandpiper Place (144 parcels, about -79.058 to -79.053, 33.650 to 33.656) and Heron Pointe Ph 1 and 2 (114 parcels) next to the Golf and Yacht Club. Next step: a NAIP image from before 2014 against today's parcels, or a grantor search on HERON POINT GOLF CLUB LIMITED PARTNERSHIP.

The recorded restrictions did not appear in the comments search, and the county does not put document images online. Not used.

- **Gate details for Waterway Palms, The Bluffs and Covington Lake East** (staffed or not, hours, guest entry). I found no primary source. Listings mention a call box at Waterway Palms (row 30). This stays on the owner's question list (questions 1 and 2).
