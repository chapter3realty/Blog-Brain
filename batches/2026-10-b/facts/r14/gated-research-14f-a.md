# Round 14f research A: what the homes are like, eleven gated places

Researcher, round 14f, 2026-10-15. Rows 580 to 599. Rows 93, 147, 150, 155, 132 (affordable ledger) and 540 to 567 were read first. Fair housing: every line describes houses and lots, never people.

**The county does not publish year built or heated area.** I checked first. The Horry County parcel layer (HorryCountyGISApp MapServer layer 24) has owner, legal description, acreage, sale date and land and building values, but no year built, square feet, bedrooms or stories. The building footprint layers (layer 27; gisportal OpenData/Structures2023) give the outline only. The county's Assessors and Tables map folders answer "Token Required", and the Land Records property search asks for a login (row 599). So the county is used here only for **how many lots there are and how many have a house** (a lot with a building value above 0). Year built, size, stories, style and views come from **listings**, as the brief allows.

How the listings were read:
- **Site.** mbcoastalhomes.realgeeks.com only (RE/MAX Southern Shores' public MLS site, Coastal Carolinas MLS data). beachproteam.com was not needed. No Zillow, Redfin or Realtor.com page was opened.
- **Search.** `/search/results/?city=all&neighborhood=<MLS neighborhood>&type=res&status=sold`, then the same with `status=active`. Each MLS number on the result cards was collected.
- **Pages opened.** Each listing page `/property/<MLS#>/`, read for Property Type, Total Heated SqFt, Year Built, Levels, Style, Lot Description, Neighborhood, Project/Section, bedrooms and the description. Where a place had more than 40 listings, the first 40 in the site's order were opened (sold first, newest first as the site shows them). That order favors recent sales, so in Waterway Palms, The Bluffs and Tidewater it may tilt toward new houses. Every house used is "Detached"; for Golf and Yacht Club, the Sandpiper Place section was left out by its MLS Project/Section field (none of the 40 opened were in it).
- **Listings span.** MLS numbers 23xxxxx to 26xxxxx: houses sold or listed from 2023 to October 2026.
- **Counts of words.** "Brick", "custom", "dock", "elevator" and view words were counted in the listing descriptions by script. A description is the listing agent's words; the counts say how many listings said so, not how many houses are so.
- **Ranges.** "About A to B square feet" on the page is the middle half (25th to 75th percentile) of the opened listings' heated square feet. The full range is in the value column.
