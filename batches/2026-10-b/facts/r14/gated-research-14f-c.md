# Gated communities: club membership, and three thin entries, research 14f-c

Page: /buyers/gated-communities/. Batch 2026-10-b, round 14f. Researched 2026-10-15.

Questions: (1) for each club on the page, does buying a home come with membership, is membership required, or is it separate and optional? (2) for Waterway Palms Plantation, Seasons at Prince Creek West and Bellwood Landing, a lead only that place has, a normal day, one honest trade-off only it has, and the gate.

Method: opened club, association and developer pages with curl and copied quotes from the downloaded text. Map and lot facts come from the Horry County parcel service (MapServer layer 24), queried by legal description; lot areas were computed by script from the county's parcel outlines (projected to UTM zone 17N). Zillow, Redfin and Realtor.com were not opened. Web searches for the three thin entries returned listings only; no listing is used as a source here.

## Task 2. Thin entries

### Waterway Palms Plantation (Carolina Forest)

Lead only it has (notes, not copy): the 15-acre lake. The HOA owns a 14.98-acre parcel the county names "LAKE AREA", next to its 12.88-acre amenity parcel (row 136). No other place on the page has an association-owned lake that size at its clubhouse. Listings name a gazebo and a walking bridge on the lake; listings only, not used.

The homes: lots are small and alike. The median house lot is 0.19 acre (middle 80 percent 0.14 to 0.25 acre), and no lot is an acre. This fits the Charleston Battery plan of row 155 (row houses on narrow lots); the plan itself has no new primary source.

Trade-off only it has: building is still going on across the neighborhood. Of 746 house lots, 159 (about one in five) have no building value on the county roll. Some are held by builders (for example JAB Homes LLC holds 4). A buyer here should expect construction trucks and a house going up on a nearby lot for some years. Row 146 (listings) said this; this is the county figure.

Normal day: no primary source (no association website; waterwaypalms.com redirects to a loan officer, row 406 notes).

Gate: nothing primary beyond row 406. No row.

### Seasons at Prince Creek West (55 and older, Murrells Inlet)

Builder pages: Levitt and Sons no longer exists; the Builder Online article on Levitt's launch (found by search) returned 403 and a Cloudflare block, so it is not used. No master-association site was found; searches returned listings only.

Lead only it has (notes, not copy): the association owns a lot of land for its size. Its 18 common-area parcels add up to 106.55 acres, the largest 36.62 acres, for 446 parcels of homes. Every house lot is built: the only parcels without a building are two sewer pump stations. That matches row 124 (last lot sold 2016).

Trade-off only it has: the lots are small. The median house lot is 0.18 acre (middle 80 percent 0.15 to 0.24), against 0.44 acre at Bellwood Landing across TPC Boulevard. With lawn care in the fee (row 102), this is a place of small yards the association keeps.

Normal day and the call box: behind the owners' login (row 566). No row.

### Bellwood Landing (Prince Creek West)

Builder: not found from a primary source. One lot (Phase II, lot 52) is owned by Dawol Homes Inc and has no house yet; that shows a builder is at work there, not who built the neighborhood. The earliest deeds held by current owners date from 2005.

Lead only it has (notes, not copy): bigger lots than its gated neighbor. Of 102 lots, the median is 0.44 acre; 17 lots are an acre or more, the largest 2.7 acres. Seasons next door is 0.18 acre.

Trade-off only it has: 11 of 102 lots have no house yet, so a buyer may see a custom house built next door. Unlike Waterway Palms, these are scattered single lots, mostly held by private owners.

The park it shares (rows 103, 160) and the gate (row 550, policy terms unknown): nothing new.
