## Myrtle Trace

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** The back gate is so effective at keeping out through traffic that delivery drivers get stuck at it: the residents' newsletter asks owners to tell online stores to use Burning Ridge Road, so packages are not left at the back gate (row 280). The land was a high ridge that locals knew, crossed by a dirt track called "The Switch Road" where Myrtle Ridge Road is now (rows 285, 286).
**A normal day (notes):** Morning exercise at the pool, evening volleyball, and a poolside potluck at "Music and Moonlight" to close the summer; the 2026 pool season logged 4,408 visits (row 282). News comes in a monthly newsletter that residents write (row 281).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** Pickup trucks still may not be parked in driveways, and the board says a covenant change cannot be tried before the ten-year cycle ends in 2033 (row 284). Alligators turn up in the stormwater ponds (row 283).

## Prestwick

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** When the Jackson Companies ended club memberships in 2015, it offered the pool and the 13-court tennis center to the owners association for $1 each, and the owners took them over (row 293). The course was named the 2025 Myrtle Beach Area Golf Course of the Year (row 289), and the Gatlin Brothers once lived here and ran a charity tournament (row 291).
**A normal day (notes):** A round on a Pete and P.B. Dye course with pot bunkers and wooden bulkheads along the water (row 290), a stop at the clubhouse restaurant after (row 296), and the pool and hard courts that come with the owners association (row 293).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** The course has been fully public since January 2016, so tee times are open to anyone (row 294).

## Seasons at Prince Creek West

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** In 2013 Where to Retire magazine named it one of the 50 best master-planned communities in the United States (row 298). The association's own site has a resident outreach group, Seasons Outreach Services (SOS), and a health and wellness section (row 299).
**A normal day (notes):** Too thin from public sources this round: the association's site keeps its calendar and lifestyle pages behind a login (row 299). Use the existing clubhouse rows (102, 179).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** Although Seasons is on TPC Boulevard, no Seasons lot borders the TPC Myrtle Beach course; the nearest lot is about 560 meters from it (row 300).

## Bellwood Landing

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** Fourteen of the 102 lots run down to Collins Creek (row 301). The course next door is the only one on the Grand Strand that Golf Digest has given five stars (row 304), and in May 2026 it hosted the PGA Tour's Monday qualifier for the Myrtle Beach Classic (row 305).
**A normal day (notes):** About 30 of the 102 lots back onto a pond (row 302); the owners association keeps about 9 acres of common land (row 303).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** "Next to TPC" means across the road, not on the course: no Bellwood lot borders the golf course; the nearest is about 100 meters from it (row 306).

## Not verified

- **Burning Ridge Golf Club (next to Myrtle Trace).** Search results say Gene Hamm designed it, it opened in 1980 with 36 holes, and the West Course was turned into housing about 2005. Every page found was a golf-travel or tee-time site (myrtlebeachgolftrips.com, golfn.com, golfscape.com). The club's own site (burningridgegolf.com) failed to connect through the proxy (502). Not used.
- **Myrtle Trace "Decade" paper and the "Singles on the Go" group.** The 40th anniversary decade timeline is national news by year, nothing about the community. The newsletter's singles group was left out (describes people, not a feature).
- **Prestwick gate.** No POA website or gate policy was found; FirstService manages the POA (row 297) but its community site needs a login. How guests and deliveries get in, and guard hours, remain listings-only (row 19).
- **Prestwick tennis today.** Whether the tennis club still runs all 13 courts, and who operates it, was not found on a current primary page. A 2017 MyHorryNews column says the 2016 operator moved to Wachesaw; directory sites list "Prestwick Tennis and Swim Center" at 1375 McMaster Dr. Not used.
- **Seasons at Prince Creek West.** The association's calendar, lifestyle, access and outreach pages need a login. The archived charter (web.archive.org) reset the connection and was not re-read. Listings describe a 29,000 square foot clubhouse, a library, water aerobics and clubs; not used (listing sites). Pond count from OSM gave zero lots, which is not believable (OSM likely lacks the Seasons ponds), so it was dropped. County building footprints around the clubhouse could not be tied to the clubhouse with confidence.
- **Bellwood Landing water access.** A listing says 8 lots have water access and 2 are private with docks allowed; listing sites are not sources, and no HOA document was found. The 2024 gate policy (row 16) text is still not online. No Bellwood HOA website was found.
- **Prince Creek West history.** What the land was before (timber, plantation) and who developed the master plan were not found in a primary source this round. The Toll Brothers May 2026 release calls it a 2,700-acre master plan; not checked.
