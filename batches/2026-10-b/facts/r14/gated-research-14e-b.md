# Gated communities: what is distinct, and how the gate works, research 14e-b

Page: /buyers/gated-communities/. Batch 2026-10-b, round 14e. Researched 2026-10-15.

Question: for five places, one thing a gated buyer would pay to know that the ledger (rows 1 to 533) does not have, and how the gate works, from a primary source.

Method: opened the association, club and management pages with curl and copied quotes from the downloaded text. Map facts come from OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) and the Horry County parcel service (MapServer layer 24). Zillow, Redfin and Realtor.com were not opened. Web searches returned only listings for the gate questions; no listing is used as a source here.

Water distances: measured by script along the OpenStreetMap centerline of the Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway (ways 45434433, 45434484, 1218791777, 45434607, joined end to end), from the point on the line nearest each feature. Each feature sits 75 to 135 meters off the centerline, so the figures are rounded down to the half mile or mile. Bridges on the route were not checked (the bridge query timed out twice).

## 1. Waterway Palms Plantation (Carolina Forest)

Distinct (notes, not copy): the ramp. OpenStreetMap shows a slipway (way 905183670) inside the HOA's boat landing parcel (county PIN 42009010024, "W/W PALMS PLANTATION; BOAT LANDING PAR A&B", 2.30 acres, owner Waterway Palms Plantation HOA Inc). A second slipway just southwest (way 905183671) is on a different parcel, owned by Carolina Waterway Plantation HOA, so it is not Waterway Palms'. Along the waterway, Waterway Palms' ramp is about 1.7 miles southwest of (behind) The Bluffs' ramp, so a Waterway Palms boat runs past The Bluffs to reach Grande Dunes, Barefoot and Little River. This is what separates the two entries on the water.

Charleston Battery plan (row 155): no new primary source. The only one is still the 2014 Kuester page, which also calls the boat launch "proposed". The ramp parcel was platted in 2010 (row 244), so the launch was built after that page. Owners' events: no association website exists (waterwaypalms.com redirects to a loan officer, row 406 notes). Nothing found.

Gate: nothing primary beyond the 2014 Kuester line "a planned gated community" (row 406). Whether it is staffed, and how a guest gets in, is not in a primary source. Row 30 (listings) says a guest calls the owner's name to be buzzed in. OpenStreetMap marks no gate on Waterway Palms Boulevard.

## 2. The Bluffs on the Waterway (Carolina Forest)

Distinct (notes, not copy): the boat ramp tagged "Private Boat Ramp" in OpenStreetMap (way 905220805, already cited in row 98) is on a 6.33-acre parcel owned by The Bluffs on the Waterway HOA Inc (PIN 42004010027). It is the nearer of the two to Grande Dunes Marina: about 2.5 miles by water, against about 4 for Waterway Palms.

Boathouse clubhouse (what is in it, what owners do there): no primary source. No association website, portal or management page exists (also found in G1). Listings describe a kitchen and a functions area; that is listings only and not used. Custom stucco homes (row 147) and Black Creek woods (row 156) have nothing new from a primary source.

Gate: nothing primary found. No rows.

## 3. Wachesaw East (Murrells Inlet)

Distinct (notes, not copy): the club's own event calendar (wachesaweast.com/events/) lists happy hours at the Toasted Tee Pub & Grub from April to May 2025, with live music, $5 small plates and drink specials. It lists no event after May 23, 2025, and none coming. That is the trade-off only this place has on the page: the social life is a public golf club's grill (owned by a vacation-ownership company, row 334), and its calendar has been empty for 17 months. The club site has no pool, amenity or social page (/pool/ and /amenities/ return 404). The HOA pool and clubhouse (row 105, listings) have no primary source.

Gate: nothing primary. The club pages say nothing about the gate or how a public golfer gets in. No row.

## 4. Seasons at Prince Creek West (55 and older, Murrells Inlet)

Club or event: none found from a primary source. Every page of seasons55.com but the public Contact page and the Policies page asks for a login: info.php pages 35, 36, 68, 560181bbd9ca7c (Access / Security), 662bc879a4bcbf, 96a0c41f557107 and 96abea948380a4, and the home page sections "What's New", "Lifestyle Office & Ambassadors". Web searches turned up club names (garden, book, cooking, beer and wine) only on listing and agent sites; not used.

Call box: nothing primary. The Access / Security page is behind the login.

One public fact a buyer can use: the HOA does not answer sales questions.

## 5. The Reserve (Pawleys Island)

Gate: the association's TownSq page and the golf club's About page both say the community is gated, and nothing more. TownSq pages 0 to 5 were reopened: no gate hours, guest rule or vendor rule; rules are behind the owners' login. The Litchfield by the Sea guest guide says guests to the Reserve follow the host's instructions (row 5). A web search turned up one listing saying the Reserve gate is attended 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. and overnight guests go through the Litchfield by the Sea 24-hour gate. That is listings only and is not a row; if the writer wants it, it must be asked of Waccamaw Management (843-237-9551, the managing agent on the TownSq page).

OpenStreetMap shows no gate or guardhouse at the Reserve's entrance (bounding box 33.47 to 33.52 N, 79.10 to 79.16 W, query timed out once; not confirmed).
