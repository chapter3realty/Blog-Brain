# Round 14 research: North Myrtle Beach and Grande Dunes

Researcher, 2026-10-14. Every URL below was opened in this session. Rows 200 to 232. None repeats a fact already in a row of `gated-communities-facts.md`; where a row adds to an older row, the claim says so. Listings, aggregators and agent sites were not used as sources.

## Tidewater Plantation

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** Golf Digest ranked Tidewater Golf Club No. 39 in America's 100 Greatest Public Courses for 2023 to 2024 (row 200), and nine of its holes play along the tidal inlet at Cherry Grove (row 202). The course is public: anyone can book it, and its memberships are open to anyone who lives or owns property in Horry, Georgetown or Brunswick County, not just Tidewater owners (row 201).
**A normal day (notes):** A round on the inlet holes, then lunch or dinner at Joey's Clubhouse Grille, a public restaurant and sports bar that looks out on the course, open from 11 a.m. every day (row 204).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** The course is not a private club. Owners share it with the public and pay the same membership rates as other local residents (rows 201, 203).

## Seaside Plantation

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** Under the 2025 state golf cart law, a permitted cart from a gated community may be driven within four miles of the community's entrance, by day, on roads posted 35 mph or less, unless the city sets its own rules (row 207). This is what makes Seaside's carts-to-Main-Street life work. The owners association owns about 13 acres of lake parcels in a neighborhood of about 160 homes (row 205).
**A normal day (notes):** A golf cart ride from the gate to Main Street by day, within the state's four-mile rule (row 207); back home, the lakes behind many houses belong to the owners association (row 205).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** No source run by the association could be found, so the gate's hours and guest rules are still unknown; the recorded gate rules (row 15) are not posted (Not verified).

## Barefoot Resort: Dye Estates and Leatherleaf

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** The Dye Course, which surrounds Dye Estates, hosts the yearly Monday After the Masters pro-am that Hootie & the Blowfish run (rows 208, 209). The Dye Estates rules ban contractor work that day (row 210). Dye Estates has a guard at the main gate 24 hours a day, plus a second gate where a guest can call a resident from the keypad and the resident presses 9 to let them in (rows 211, 212).
**A normal day (notes):** In summer, call the free shuttle 15 minutes ahead and ride to the oceanfront cabana, which has key-fob parking, an elevator, showers and a pet-washing station (rows 216, 217). Leatherleaf owners also have the Residents' Club, with a pool, a weight room, a library, courts and a lifestyle director. Dye Estates is not on that club's list (row 215).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** The lakes and ponds in Dye Estates are for looking at, not for use. Swimming, boating and fishing are banned, and owners may not build docks on them (row 219). Building lots are still for sale with no deadline to build, so construction can go on next door for years (row 213).

## Grande Dunes (gated sections)

**Best non-obvious thing (notes, not copy):** The private Members Club course was designed by Nick Price, a PGA Hall of Famer, with Craig Schreiner (row 220). In 2019 its greens were changed from bentgrass to ultradwarf bermudagrass, a heat-tolerant grass. Its clubhouse has two Trackman simulators for playing more than 260 courses indoors (row 221). The marina's floating docks take boats of 120 feet or more (row 225).
**A normal day (notes):** The Ocean Club pool, with towel and bar service, and member cabanas on the beach (row 224); a book club, a wine dinner or trivia at the Members Club (row 222); a walk on the two-mile Waterway Trail (row 229).
**Honest trade-off (notes):** The Members Club costs extra on top of the HOA. Homeowners get the Ocean Club, but the Members Club is a separate private membership, and the course now takes limited outside play (rows 223, 224).
