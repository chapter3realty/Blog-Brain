Researcher round for the best-neighborhoods page (places 1 to 3) and the affordable page (places 4 to 7). Date opened: 2026-10-21 for every row. Nothing here is page copy. Facts that a sibling page owns (SIBLING-OWNERS.md) are left out: Tidewater's golf course, Grande Dunes' marina, Ocean Club and Waterway Trail, the Avocet course, The Farm's rec center, library, Bike and Run Park and greenway, and the Golf and Yacht Club's Enterprise Landing, Heron Point, watch desk and Sandpiper Place.
