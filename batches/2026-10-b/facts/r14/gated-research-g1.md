# Gated communities: how the gate works, research G1 (round 14c)

Page: /buyers/gated-communities/. Batch 2026-10-b, round 14c. Researched 2026-10-15.

Question: for each place, how the gate works (guard and hours, or keypad, call box, tag or app), how guests, deliveries and contractors get in, whether there is a roving patrol, and whether the HOA fee pays for gate staff.

Method: searched for each association's own website, portal, recorded rules, owner guides and gate forms, and for news. Opened every URL below myself with curl or a fetch and copied the quote from the downloaded text. No listing, agent site or aggregator is used as a source. Zillow, Realtor.com and Redfin were not opened. Rows already in the ledger (14 to 31, 82, 149, 200 to 299) were read first, and nothing here repeats them.

Most of these associations keep their gate rules behind a resident login. What is public is thin, and the gaps are listed under "Not verified".

## 2. Leatherleaf, Barefoot Resort (North Myrtle Beach)

Gate (notes, not copy): Leatherleaf's own covenants (2000, posted on the Barefoot association's website) let its association run "guarded or electronically-monitored gates" and let in a visitor who shows the owner gave permission. Which kind of gate Leatherleaf has today (guard, keypad or tag) is not stated in any public document. The Dye Estates gate form (rows 211 and 212) is for Dye Estates only. ## Summary

Eight rows, 400 to 407, all pending verification.

- Tidewater Plantation: residents' cars use RFID tags read hands-free, in place of key fobs; three entrances (400, 401). Source is the gate reader maker's undated case study.
- Leatherleaf: covenants allow guarded or electronically monitored gates and let in a visitor with the owner's permission (402); the gate is no promise of safety (403); contractor hours 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. weekdays, and rule breakers can be barred (404).
- Grande Dunes: Allied Security, three guard houses and a roving officer, per the master association's February 2026 contacts list (405). Which guard house serves the three gated sections is not stated.
- Waterway Palms: a 2014 manager's page calls it gated (406, context only).
- Prestwick: golfers drive through the guard gate to reach the public course (407, from 2016).
- The Bluffs on the Waterway, Seasons at Prince Creek West and Bellwood Landing: nothing new from a primary source.

For the page: no primary source gives guard hours, a guest procedure or a delivery rule for any of the eight. The page should describe the gate kinds the sources do show (a staffed guard house with a rover at Grande Dunes, resident RFID tags at Tidewater, a gate allowed by covenant at Leatherleaf), say "listings show" for the rest, and tell the buyer to ask the association for the gate rules and the fee budget before buying.
