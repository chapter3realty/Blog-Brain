**Correction for the coordinator first.** The brief names SC Code 56-2-105. That section was repealed on May 22, 2025 (2025 Act No. 64). Golf carts are now in Section 56-2-90. The rules are the same in kind (daylight, secondary roads at 35 mph or less, 4 miles), and the new section has a clause for gated communities (row 541). Any page text that cites 56-2-105 should cite 56-2-90.

How the data was pulled:
- **State law.** scstatehouse.gov, Title 56 Chapter 2 page, fetched with curl and read as text.
- **City rules.** nmb.us "Golf Cart Laws" page and the city's June 24, 2025 news release (fetch tool; curl was reset by the city's server).
- **Roads.** OpenStreetMap: a box query on Overpass (it answered once, then reset), then each way read from api.openstreetmap.org (`/api/0.6/way/<id>/full.json`). Road distance summed along the way nodes (haversine).

## Seaside Plantation (North Myrtle Beach)

**The thing a gated buyer would pay to know (notes):** Yes, an owner can legally drive a golf cart to Main Street, in daylight, with a state permit decal. North Myrtle Beach's own rule (Chapter 21, amended June 2025) allows permitted carts on the city's secondary roads with a posted limit of 35 mph or less, sunrise to sunset; carts may only cross Ocean Boulevard, 27th Avenue South, Highway 17 and Sea Mountain Highway (row 540). The state law behind it is 56-2-90: a $5 DMV permit decal, proof of insurance, a driver 16 or older with a license, and children under 12 belted (row 541). The city's ordinance replaces the state's default 4-mile limit, so the 4 miles do not decide this trip; the state default would allow it anyway, and it measures from a gated community's entrance (row 541).
**The route (notes):** From the entrance where Sea Island Way meets 8th Avenue North, south on 8th Avenue North about 120 m to Hillside Drive North, then southwest on Hillside Drive North (state road S-26-199, a secondary road) about 0.6 mile to Main Street. About 0.67 mile in all by road (row 542). Main Street's limit is tagged 25 mph at the Hillside end and 35 mph toward Highway 17 in OpenStreetMap (both within the city's 35). Hillside Drive North and 8th Avenue North carry no speed tag in OpenStreetMap, so their posted limit is not shown by any source read. No primary road has to be crossed on this route.
**The gate (notes):** Still not shown. The association has no public website found, and the four recorded gate documents are titles only (rows 15, 522). OpenStreetMap has no gate node on Sea Island Way. Gate kind (guard, call box, keypad) and how a guest gets in: not found.

## Tidewater Plantation (North Myrtle Beach)

**A trade-off only Tidewater has (notes):** Two layers of association. The association's own site calls Tidewater "a master association" of 664 houses "with 6 sub-associations" (row 545). A buyer should ask whether a house is also in a sub-association, and what that one charges and decides, on top of the master fee. The site does not say which streets are in which sub-association.
**Also from its own site (notes):** It is inside the city, so owners have the city's water, sewer, trash pickup, police, fire and rescue (row 546). The site counts 4 pools, 1 fitness and amenity center, 3 tennis and pickleball courts (4 in another line) and 1 beach cabana for owners.
**The gate today (notes):** Not on the association's public page. Its site (run on FirstService Residential's Connect Resident portal) says only "This gated community"; forms, documents and the calendar are behind a login. A 2011 association rules manual turned up in a search, hosted on an unrelated foreign domain; it was not opened as a source and is 15 years old, so it is not used. The guard house on Little River Neck Road is from listings only (row 20).
