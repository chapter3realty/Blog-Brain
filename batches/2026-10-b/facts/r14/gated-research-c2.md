# Round 14c research: what the clubs cost, and the Ocean Club in three Grande Dunes sections

Researcher, 2026-10-15 (round 14c). Rows 480 to 502. Rows 201, 203, 220 to 224, 320, 341, 342, 352, 385 and 106 were read first. Nothing here repeats them. Every URL below was opened in this round. Golf directories, agent sites, listings and aggregators were not used. Several of them print "initiation" figures for these clubs, with no source behind them.

## A. What joining the club costs

The short answer: none of the private clubs prints an initiation fee, dues, a food minimum or a capital assessment on its public pages. Each one sends the reader to a form or a phone number. The only published prices are for public or semi-private programs: Tidewater (rows 201, 203), the Founders card at Willbrook (row 352), and the Legends programs that cover the Heritage Club course (rows below).

### Wachesaw Plantation Club
- Membership page (https://www.wachesaw.com/Membership) lists the categories and no prices. Row 320 already covers the 12-month commitment and the Interim Equity terms.
- Categories, as printed. Equity: Oaks (unlimited green fees), Interim Equity, Magnolia (50 rounds of green fees), Young Adult (special pricing under 40, unlimited green fees), Social Sport, Social. Non-equity: Social Sport, Social.
- "Wachesaw Plantation Club reserves the right to change membership offerings and pricing at any time without notice."
- No rate sheet, brochure or newsletter is linked from the public site. A search of wachesaw.com and the local news found no fee story.

### The Reserve Golf Club (McConnell Golf)
- Membership page and McConnell Golf's membership page print no prices. Row 342 already has the two categories, "No operating assessments" and dependents to age 25.
- New: the page invites a tour and gives a phone number instead of a price, and describes "a price and model compatible to the leisure demands of today's consumer." That phrase is marketing, not a figure; it is recorded so nobody reads a price into it.

### DeBordieu Club
- The club's pages print no prices (row 385 has the four levels). The 2026 Welcome Packet of the DeBordieu Colony Community Association says the Colony is two entities, the association and The DeBordieu Club, Inc., and that buying property makes you a member of the association only. It sends owners to the club by phone for membership information.
- The covenants on the association site name no club fee. The association's newsletter and budget are behind the members' login.

### Grande Dunes Members Club and Ocean Club
- The Members Club's Membership Categories page lists four categories: Full Golf, Young Professional (ages 21 to 39), Nonresident Golf (primary home outside Horry, Georgetown and Brunswick counties) and Social. No prices. Memberships cover the member, spouse and children under 26.
- The Full Golf and Young Professional memberships include the Ocean Club and its private beach access. This is the club's own page. It matters for Question B: the Members Club is one way to reach the Ocean Club without owning a Grande Dunes home.
- The Ocean Club prints no fees. Its "Property Owner Membership Agreement Form" page sends a visitor without a login to the home page.

### The Dye Club at Barefoot and the Barefoot Residents' Club
- The Dye Club's own membership tab prints no price; it is a contact form. The Dye Club membership includes the resort's other three courses.
- The resort membership (Barefoot Resort & Golf, non-equity; Social, Golf and Social, Seasonal) does publish prices on its FAQ page: $205 a month for Golf and Social, $85 for Social, a $2,750 transfer fee on a home with a membership attached, $15,000 to $20,000 initiation on a home without one, $3,000 for an additional owner, no food minimum "at this time". It is sold only to owners of named developments, and some properties are excluded. Rows 486 to 488. Which neighborhoods are "Centex/Pulte Homes Developments" is not on the page; do not tell a Dye Estates or Leatherleaf buyer the membership comes with the home.
- The Snowbird winter program ($325 to $375 a month, November 1, 2026 to March 15, 2027) excludes property owners and full-time local residents, so it does not apply to a buyer. Not a row.
- The Residents' Club has no separate fee: owners in its ten neighborhoods use it if their HOA dues are paid. Row 490. Row 215 already lists the club and its neighborhoods.
- barefootgolf.com returns 429 to curl. Those quotes were read with the fetch tool and should be re-read by the verifier.

### Tidewater Golf Club
- Rows 201 and 203 already give the 2026 annual and Players Card prices. New: Players Card renewals ($169, or $79 online in December 2026; $179 in 2027), a $13 nine-hole cart fee, and that the page lists no initiation fee and no food minimum. Row 491. Fetch-tool reading; tidewatergolf.com returns 429 to curl.

### Heritage Club
- heritageclubgolf.com did not answer (the proxy returned 502). The course is now one of the five courses of Legends Golf Resort, whose membership page lists it.
- Legends prints two local programs with prices: the Royalty Club at $49.95 a year and the Players Club at $39.95 a month. Both need a home in Horry, Georgetown or Brunswick County. At the Heritage Club course, a Players Club member pays $41 Monday to Wednesday and $46 Thursday to Sunday, or $16 and $19 after 1 p.m.; a Royalty Club member pays $41 and $46, or $30 and $31 after 1 p.m. All plus tax.
- The Heritage Club Membership itself (all five Legends courses, league play and member events at Heritage Club) has no printed price: "Please complete the form below to receive current rates and availability details."
- The Heritage Plantation association's public page names the course but no golf cost; its documents need a login.

### Willbrook Plantation and Founders Group
- Willbrook prints no membership price; it gives a phone number. Founders Group lists club benefits ("Low Initiation Fee", "Low Annual Dues", carts from $20) with no figures. Row 494.
- New beside row 352: the Prime Times card has other tiers (Basic $139, Honors Club $599, Honors Couples $999, Winter $75). Row 495.

### TPC Myrtle Beach and Prestwick Country Club
- TPC Myrtle Beach sells single and family memberships, has a waiting list and prints no price. Row 496.
- Prestwick sells no memberships. It ended them on December 31, 2015, and became a daily-fee course (Club + Resort Business, December 2015). Row 497. Row 123 already says the club ended memberships; this adds the date and the source.

### Reserve Harbor (Safe Harbor) Harbor Club, and the Litchfield Plantation marina
- Safe Harbor's pages print no Harbor Club price. The Reserve Harbor page describes the club as a venue with a pool and food, and Safe Harbor's "membership" is a slip or storage license. Row 498. Agent listings call it a "Harbor Club Social Membership"; they are not a source.
- Litchfield Plantation marina slip fee: no allowed source found. See Not verified.

## B. The Ocean Club in Calais, Palermo and Villa Venezia

What each source says:

1. **Grande Dunes Master Association, Amenities page** (TownSq): "As a Grande Dunes homeowner, you receive a complimentary household membership to the Grande Dunes Ocean Club." The page names no section and no exception.
2. **Master Association, Neighborhoods page**: lists 21 neighborhoods "in" Grande Dunes. Calais and Villa Venezia are on the list. Palermo is not named. Golf Village is.
3. **Master Association, GD Neighborhood Association Contacts PDF**: Calais and Villa Venezia are each managed by Waccamaw Management, which also manages the Master Association. Palermo is not listed by name. The Golf Village is.
4. **Horry County parcel records**: the three Palermo common areas are owned by "GOLF VILLAGE POA" (deed 3364/2401). The Villa Venezia common areas and its private road are owned by "THE GOLF VILLAGE POA INC C/O WACCAMAW MANAGEMENT LLC" (deeds 4759/2907 and 4759/2912). The Calais common areas are owned by "CALAIS POA" and "CALAIS PROPERTY OWNERS' ASSOCIATION". So Palermo and Villa Venezia sit under The Golf Village Property Owners' Association, which is on the master list, and Calais has its own association, also on the master list.
5. **Master Association, Documents page**: the TownSq instructions say that "As a Grande Dunes homeowner," Waccamaw creates a neighborhood account and "A second, separate account number" for the Grande Dunes Master Association when it receives the closing documents. That is how a buyer becomes a Master Association member. It names no section.
6. **Ocean Club, Homeownership page**: "Residents of Grande Dunes enjoy access to our spectacular Ocean Club." No section named.
7. **Members Club, Membership Categories page**: the Full Golf and Young Professional memberships include the Ocean Club. This is a second route, not a homeowner benefit.

What I could not open: the recorded Master Declaration and any supplemental declaration that annexes Calais, Palermo or Villa Venezia. The county's deed site posts index lines only, not documents, and its search needs a browser session. The CC&Rs on TownSq need an owner's login. The Ocean Club's Property Owner Membership Agreement needs a member login. No source I opened names an exception for any section, a build date or a type of home. None says in words "Calais owners," "Palermo owners" or "Villa Venezia owners" get the Ocean Club either.

Side note for the writer: the county lists 14 Villa Venezia units as a horizontal property regime ("VILLA VENEZIA HPR; UNIT 2-1102 PH" and others). Villa Venezia is not only detached houses. Row 96 calls the three sections "neighborhoods of houses"; that holds for the listings it used, not for every home in Villa Venezia.
