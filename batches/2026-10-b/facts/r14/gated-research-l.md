How the county data was pulled:
- **Deed index.** Horry County AcclaimWeb, comments search (accept the disclaimer, then "Contains" or "Starts With", record dates 1/1/1970 to 10/15/2026), exported with the site's own CSV button. Searches: "SEASIDE PLANTATION" Starts With (5 rows) and Contains (1,010 rows); "HERON POINT" Contains (1,702 rows); "SANDPIPER" Contains (621 rows).
- **Parcels and flood zones.** Horry County GIS, HorryCountyGISApp MapServer: layer 24 (Parcels) and layer 19 (Flood Zones, which carries FEMA's fields FLD_ZONE and SFHA_TF). Each lot polygon was tested against each flood polygon (shapely, polygons in WGS84; distances in a local flat projection at latitude 33.78). FEMA's own NFHL server (hazards.fema.gov) did not answer from this session (timed out twice), so the county's copy of the FEMA layer was used. See Not verified.

## Seaside Plantation (North Myrtle Beach)

**Best non-obvious thing (notes):** The gate has written rules, not just an arm. Besides the 2019 Gate Access Rules (row 15), the owners association recorded three more policies with the county in 2019: a parking policy, an entrance gate and security policy for contractors and service providers, and one for real estate agents (row 522). That tells a buyer the association decides, in writing, who comes through and how. The texts are not online, so what they say is still not known.
**A Saturday there (notes):** Nothing new from a primary source. The owners' days are in rows 94 and 153 (lakes, clubhouse pool and hot tub, golf cart to Main Street). The association has no public website found.
**The gate (notes):** Recorded gate rules for residents (row 15), contractors and service providers, and real estate agents (row 522). Gate kind (guard, call box or code) is still not shown by any source.
**Honest trade-off only this place has (notes):** None new. The flood trade-off is row 187.
**Land and builder (notes):** One developer, East Coast Developers LLC, sold the lots from August 2000 to 2007 (row 520). The first plats call Seaside "Parcel III" of a tract recorded in the name of THF Gator Hole Development LLC (row 521). This fits Seaside's place next to Gator Hole Plaza (row 153), but no record read says what the land was before.

## Myrtle Trace (55+, Conway)

**Best non-obvious thing (notes):** Being on the ridge means no lot touches any FEMA flood zone, not even the back edge of a lot by a pond; the nearest high-risk zone is about half a mile from the nearest home lot (row 524). The 55+ guide counted address points (lot centers); this test uses the whole lot.
**A Saturday there (notes):** League bocce or shuffleboard (already in the 55+ guide), then the clubhouse, where owners keep a library and a computer, and two owners notarize papers for neighbors at no charge (row 526).
**The gate (notes):** The back gates on Myrtle Ridge Road keep out cut-through traffic; the front on Burning Ridge Road is open (row 13). A gate problem goes to owner volunteers listed in the community directory, not to a guard or a company (row 527). Packages: row 280.
**What the volunteer HOA does (notes):** Volunteers do the work a management company would: gates, clubhouse bookings, library, notaries, a Neighborhood Watch contact (rows 526, 527). Past board presidents advise the board four times a year (row 528). Yard care is the owner's: owners keep their own list of trades their neighbors have used (row 529).
**Honest trade-off only this place has (notes):** The help is volunteer help. Nobody is paid to answer a gate call at night (row 527), and the HOA itself says it has not checked the trades on its referral list (row 529).
**Land (notes):** The HOA's paper says "Burning Ridge" is named for a fire that burned for weeks (row 525).
