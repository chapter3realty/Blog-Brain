## Best fact per area (my pick)

- Socastee: row 7, the bridge that finished the Waterway in South Carolina (1935), with the opening party held there on April 11, 1936.
- Garden City Beach: no clean non-storm fact found. Row 11 or 12 (Hazel) if the owner accepts a storm fact; the pier burger and golf cart parade are already on the page.
- Myrtle Heights: row 26, the largest intact group of historic buildings in Myrtle Beach, begun in 1933 by the founders of Myrtle Beach Farms.
- Windy Hill: row 14, built by Conway investors after the war and one of the four towns that became North Myrtle Beach in 1968.
- Surfside Beach: row 18, the first autism-friendly travel destination (2016).
- Conway: row 20, City Hall is an 1825 courthouse that still holds court.
- Aynor: row 22, the Galivants Ferry Stump.
- Loris: row 24, "Gate City", the first town south of the state line on the railroad.
- Murrells Inlet north end: row 27 (Spillane) once the bill is opened; row 28 as a second.
- Little River: the festival crowd (rows 3 to 5); row 31 if a storm fact is wanted.
