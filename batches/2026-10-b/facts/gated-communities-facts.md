## How to open the sources

- **Listing sites (marked as listings, never a primary source).** The Beach Pro Team, ERA Real Estate Modo, `https://www.beachproteam.com/listings/view/<address-slug>-sc-real-estate-<MLS#>.html` (robots.txt allows /listings/, asks for a 10-second crawl delay, which was honored), and RE/MAX Southern Shores, `https://mbcoastalhomes.realgeeks.com/property/<MLS#>/` (robots.txt names ClaudeBot and Claude-User under "Allow: /"). Both are fed by the Coastal Carolinas MLS. Each listing used is in the evidence tables at the end. "Gated on both sites" means the RE/MAX Southern Shores field "Amenities" includes "Gated" and the Beach Pro Team fields "Assoc Amenities" include "Gated" or "Security Features" include "GatedCommunity". The listing search used is `https://mbcoastalhomes.realgeeks.com/search/results/?city=all&neighborhood=<MLS neighborhood>&type=all&list_price_min=all&list_price_max=all&area_min=all&beds_min=all&baths_min=all`, which shows listings for sale on 2026-10-13. Never Zillow, Realtor.com or Redfin listing pages.
- **Register of Deeds (AcclaimWeb).** Open https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/ and accept the conditions. A Book/Page opens at `https://acclaimweb.horrycounty.org/AcclaimWeb/details/JumpToBookPage/1/<book>/<page>`. The county posts the index entry, not the document text.
- **Sale prices, Horry County.** The method of batch 2026-10-a (seasons ledger row 73): every DEED (001) recorded 10/1/2025 to 9/30/2026 (AcclaimWeb search by Doc Type, month by month; 30,339 deeds countywide, the same total row 73 verified) whose index parcel number is one of the community's parcels in Horry County GIS layer 24 (`https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer/24/query?where=<filter>&outFields=PIN,LegalDescr,LandUseCode&returnGeometry=false&f=json`, filter given in each row). Not counted: consideration under $10,000, corrective or re-recorded deeds, deeds on parcels the county codes as vacant land (land use 100), and deeds whose seller is a builder or company named in the row (reported apart). Quartiles use the inclusive method. A check: this code reproduces seasons ledger row 73 and myrtle-trace ledger row 81 exactly (19 sales, median $499,000; 35 sales, median $300,000).
- **Sale prices, Georgetown County.** AcclaimWeb covers Horry County only. For Georgetown County the county's own open data has a parcel table with the last sale on each parcel: `https://gis1.georgetowncountysc.org/portal/rest/services/GCGIS_OpenData/FeatureServer/7/query?where=<filter>&outFields=*&f=json` (layer "PARCELATTRIBUTES", fields SaleDate, SalePrice, LandUseCode, LegalDescription). It holds only the **last** sale of each parcel and no seller name, so a home sold twice counts once and builder sales cannot be taken out. Rows using it say so. Houses are land use codes ending in 100 or 101.
- **Drive times.** OSRM public router (OpenStreetMap roads), fastest route, no traffic. End points: S.C. Department of Environmental Services beach access layer, `https://gis.des.sc.gov/gisserver/rest/services/BeachAccess/MapServer/0/query?where=OBJECTID%3D<id>&outFields=*&returnGeometry=true&outSR=4326&f=json`, records with ACC_TYPE "PUBLIC BEACH ACCESS" or "BEACH PARK" and PARKING "Yes" (a car must be able to park). For each start, an OSRM table to the 40 nearest such accesses by straight line; the fastest kept, then routed alone. Minutes up to 10 are rounded to the nearest minute, over 10 to the nearest 5 (the rule used in batch 2026-10-a). **Start point:** the county address point nearest the average position of the community's home address points (Horry County Addresses layer 22, by the PINs in the price row), or for Georgetown County the home parcel nearest the average of the home parcel centers (layer 2, `https://gis1.georgetowncountysc.org/portal/rest/services/GCGIS_OpenData/FeatureServer/2`). Each row names its start address.

Library page: `https://library.municode.com/sc/horry_county/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH18LADERE`.
