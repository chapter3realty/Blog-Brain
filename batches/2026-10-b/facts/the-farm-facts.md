# Fact ledger: /submarkets/carolina-forest/the-farm/

Batch 2026-10-b, page 4. The Farm at Carolina Forest, Myrtle Beach (unincorporated Horry County), ZIP 29579. Researcher pass 2026-10-13. Every row is "(added 2026-10-13)". The verifier columns are empty.

One row per claim. **The writer may use rows marked `verified` only.** The researcher filled columns 1 to 8. A different agent fills 9 and 10.

## Deciding fact (reported first)

1. **Extent.** County parcel records put 1,571 homes in The Farm: 1,292 detached-house lots (land use 101), 207 Orchards townhouse lots (land use 109) and 72 Planters Point condominium units (land use 107, a horizontal property regime). Another 63 parcels are HOA open space, common areas, wetlands, a pump station and one HOA-owned land parcel (1,634 parcels in all). The legal descriptions name phases 1 to 26 (no 8, 22 or 23A under that name), Brookberry 24A and 24B, The Orchards Ph 2 and Planters Point. Every one of the 1,571 carries a building value. Rows 1 to 4.
2. **HOA fee.** The HOA's posted, approved 2026 budget sets regular assessments that divide to exactly **$107.00 a month per home** for all 1,571 homes (2025: $100.00). The Orchards and Planters Point pay the same $107 to The Farm HOA and also belong to their own sub-associations, so their listings show higher totals. 2026 MLS listings agree: 38 of 51 detached-house listings show $107. Rows 5 to 9.

## Method notes for the verifier

- **Name collisions.** "The Farm at Timberlake" (near Hwy 707, tax district 600) and "The Farm at Fox Bay" (near Loris, district 220) are other subdivisions. They are excluded by the county query in rows 1 to 3. "Carolina Forest Association, Inc." (carolinaforestinc.com) is a separate association; The Farm's declaration names no master association (the OCR text has no such clause).
- **County GIS.** HorryCountyGISApp MapServer, https://www.horrycounty.org/parcelapp/rest/services/HorryCountyGISApp/MapServer (layer 18 Roads, 22 Addresses, 24 Parcels). Municipal limits: https://gisportal.horrycounty.org/server/rest/services/OpenData/Municipalities/MapServer/0 . Owner names appear in layer 24; this ledger names organizations only.
- **Parcel set (rows 1 to 4, 45, 79).** Layer 24, `where` = `(LegalDescr LIKE 'FARM @%' OR LegalDescr LIKE 'FARM AT%' OR LegalDescr LIKE 'THE FARM%' OR LegalDescr LIKE 'BROOKBERRY%' OR LegalDescr LIKE 'THE ORCHARDS PH 2%' OR LegalDescr LIKE 'PLANTERS POINT%') AND LegalDescr NOT LIKE '%TIMBERLAKE%' AND LegalDescr NOT LIKE '%FOX BAY%'`. Homes = that set AND `LandUseCode IN ('101','107','109')`. The GeoJSON outline URL is in `batches/2026-10-b/data/the-farm.json`.
- **Reference point.** The Farmers Rest Drive entrance from Carolina Forest Boulevard: lon -78.875215, lat 33.754869, the shared end point of the FARMERS REST DR and CAROLINA FOREST BLVD segments in county roads layer 18. No source names a "main" entrance. This is the entrance nearest the large pool (201 Carolina Farms Blvd) and the activity center (190 Farmers Rest Dr). The other road connections are Mill Street at Carolina Forest Boulevard (-78.883857, 33.75808) and Wheatfield Drive at International Drive (-78.866827, 33.768257). Farm Lake Drive ends about 140 m short of Carolina Forest Boulevard (OSRM nearest and county roads; no connection).
- **HOA documents.** farmatcarolinaforest.com (robots.txt disallows only /admin/, /conlib/, /layout/, /lib/, /mh/, /vendor/). The documents page https://www.farmatcarolinaforest.com/documents.html is public but not in the site menu. Most PDFs are scans; quotes were read by OCR (tesseract 200 dpi) and checked against the page image where a number matters. File names on the server contain spaces; encode them as %20.
- **Newsletter.** The HOA home page links "October 2026 Newsletter" to https://www.farmatcarolinaforest.com/uploads/newsletter/September.pdf (the file name says September; the content covers October 2026: Halloween, the October 15 pool closing, an October 24 food drive).
