## Round 14: who searches, what they would pay to know, and the top results (2026-10-15)

**Who searches "The Farm at Carolina Forest".** Search suggestions and the Search check above: the HOA, the HOA fee, homes for sale, the pool, the rules, the map. The searcher is (1) a buyer who has seen a listing in The Farm, often from out of state, who wants to picture a normal week there and decide whether to look; (2) a buyer comparing Carolina Forest neighborhoods by what the fee buys and what is close; (3) an owner who wants to rent the home out; (4) an owner or buyer who heard about the pool repair or the old bombing range.

**What they would pay to know, ranked.**
1. What a normal day is like: the pools, the fitness room, the events, the beach, the groceries, the parks and what is next door.
2. What it costs to own a house a year, beyond the mortgage, and what the $107 fee really buys (cable TV, trash pickup, the pools; not internet).
3. What the homes are and what they sell for, by kind (house, townhome, condo).
4. The rules that change daily life: renting, fences and sheds, parking and boats, pets, no private pools.
5. The schools every home is zoned for, and how close they are.
6. What could go wrong: the large pool's repair and a possible one-time charge; the second fee in The Orchards and Planters Point; the bombing range.
7. What is next door that will not change: the state preserve land on the north edge.

**The top three results, read 2026-10-15 (Google order as returned to our search tool; none is a source).**
1. Redfin's neighborhood page for The Farm at Carolina Forest. It refused our request (HTTP 405), so it was not read. Redfin's neighborhood pages are listings with a median sale price; this project does not use Redfin as a source.

## Not verified

1. **Recorded originals.** AcclaimWeb does not put document images online. Every covenant, amendment and rule quoted here is from the HOA's posted scans (OCR). The December 23, 2025 amenity rules (5028/1742) and architectural handbook (5028/1692) were not read (row 59).
2. **ARC handbook.** The FAQ says the ARC handbook is online "under the "Documents" section"; it is not on the public documents page. Specific fence heights, shed sizes and materials are therefore unread.
3. **Sub-association fees.** The Orchards and Planters Point HPR associations publish no budget or fee. Only listings give totals (rows 8, 9). The Orchards' 2019 rules (4172/239, 44 pages) are not online.
4. **Brookberry section.** Whether the Brookberry lots (Phases 24A and 24B) pay anything beyond the $107 now that the beach club agreement was terminated (row 22) is not shown. The termination notice text was not read.
5. **"Main entrance."** No source names one. The reference point is the Farmers Rest entrance (row 27).
6. **Tax districts 100 and 800.** Both bill 201 mills (row 75). What separates them is not stated on the bills or the levy sheet. The bills for HOA land show a "Fees" line ($722.63 on the 18.78-acre amenity parcel); the fees on a home's bill were not checked (private owners).
7. **Lease minimum.** The declaration says the board "may require a minimum lease term of up to six (6) months"; the 2011 rules say any minimum "in no case shall ... be shorter than six (6) months". Whether the board has set a minimum is not stated anywhere read (row 50).
8. **Fishing.** The 2011 rules allow shore fishing with a license; the FAQ says the insurer advised prohibiting recreational use (row 66). Current practice is unknown.
9. **McLeod ER street number.** McLeod's pages give no address. The 107 McLeod Health Blvd figure comes from WBTW news; the county has address points 101, 103, 107, 111 and 115 on the campus. The drive time uses 107 (row 30).
10. **"Nearest hospital."** McLeod's new hospital had not been confirmed open on 2026-10-13 (row 31). Grand Strand Medical Center (5.6 mi) and Conway Medical Center (9.1 mi) are measured; no state list was checked to prove no closer licensed hospital.
11. **Bombing range status.** News coverage (Post and Courier, WBTW, WMBF, MyHorryNews) describes U.S. Army Corps of Engineers work on the former Conway Bombing and Gunnery Range in Carolina Forest, with conflicting accounts. No USACE primary page was found. Which Farm lots came from the 17.342-acre Parcel A (Plat Book 214, Page 141) is not known (row 23).
12. **Hurricane Florence (2018).** No official source found that names The Farm. Searches returned SCO and news items about the Waccamaw River areas only. Not used.
13. **Listing amenity claims.** "8,000-square-foot main pool", "barn-themed clubhouse", "miles of bike paths" appear only in agent listing text. Not used.
14. **Golf carts in The Farm.** No HOA rule found beyond the FAQ's list of (older) state rules (row 70).
15. **Huger Park.** The county parks list shows "Huger Park Under Construction" with no address; Huger Park Ave runs near The Farm. Location and opening not verified.
