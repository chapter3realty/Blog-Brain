## Method notes for the verifier

- **Scratch scripts.** Python, run 2026-10-13. Every query URL is in its row. The deed pull returned 30,339 DEED (001) rows for 10/1/2025 to 9/30/2026, the same total as the 2026-10-a ledgers.
- **Owner names.** The Treasurer bills and deed index show private owner names. This ledger cites PINs and county legal descriptions only.
- **Town points.** USGS GNIS populated-place points (the traditional town center), except Carolina Forest (no GNIS place point; a county park parcel on Carolina Forest Boulevard). These are not addresses.
- **Three geographies.** Zillow "city" areas are Zillow's; Census places are Census boundaries; tax districts are the county's. They overlap but are not the same set. Each row says which one it uses.
- **Never record:** interest rates or payment amounts (the SC Housing documents state the lien rate and payment terms; left out on purpose), who lives anywhere, crime, schools by rating. ACS income was pulled but not recorded, because it describes people, not homes.
