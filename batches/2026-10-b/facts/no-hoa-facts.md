## Deciding fact (reported first)

**About 1 in 4 homes for sale in the nine areas shows no HOA fee, and a condo with no fee is almost never for sale.** On 2026-10-13, 798 of 2,758 homes listed in the nine areas (28.9%) showed no HOA fee; 716 of 2,676 (26.8%) when 82 homes on leased campground land are left out (rows 1 and 19). The share runs from none in Carolina Forest (0 of 258) and 11% in Little River to 55% in North Myrtle Beach and in Surfside Beach and Garden City, 60% in Loris and 67% in Aynor (rows 3 to 11). Of 2,742 listings typed "Condominium", 1 showed no fee (row 13); South Carolina law makes condo owners share the costs of the building (rows 30 to 33). The page can be written as planned.

## Method notes for the verifier

1. **Listing source.** mbcoastalhomes.realgeeks.com (RE/MAX Southern Shores' Real Geeks site; data from the Coastal Carolinas Association of REALTORS MLS). Its robots.txt allows `/search/results/` and `/property/` for every agent and names Claude agents as welcome; it disallows `/search/details/`, which was not opened. Beach Pro Team (beachproteam.com) was used for the cross-check only, 10 seconds apart, as its robots.txt asks ("Crawl-delay: 10"). Zillow and Redfin were not opened.
2. **Searches.** `/search/results/?city=<city>&type=res` (the site's "Home" type) and `&type=con` (its "Condo" type) for ten mailing cities: Myrtle Beach, North Myrtle Beach, Conway, Surfside Beach, Garden City Beach, Murrells Inlet, Little River, Loris, Aynor and Longs. The site shows at most 250 results ("only 250 can be displayed"), so larger searches were split with `list_price_min` and `list_price_max`, first in $50,000 steps, then $10,000 steps. Each split was paged 30 at a time, the MLS numbers were de-duplicated, and each city and type reached the site's own total. Search pages were read 01:41 to 01:52 UTC on 2026-10-13 (the evening of October 12, Eastern time).
3. **Fee field.** Each listing's `/property/<MLS#>/` page, 01:38 to 02:34 UTC. "With an HOA fee" means the page shows "HOA Fees (Calculated Monthly)" with a number; "no HOA fee" means the field is absent. No listing showed 0. Beach Pro Team shows "Hoa: Yes" and "Hoa Fees:" when the MLS field is filled and neither line when it is not. Both sites repeat one MLS field, so their agreement (row 21) checks copying, not a second source. The field is what the listing agent typed.
4. **Areas.** Area = the MLS mailing city, which is not a town limit (a "Myrtle Beach" address can be outside the city). Myrtle Beach listings whose MLS Region is "10b Myrtle Beach Area--Carolina Forest" are counted as Carolina Forest. Surfside Beach and Garden City Beach are combined. Murrells Inlet (136 of 382) and Surfside and Garden City (14) include Georgetown County listings. Longs is reported in row 12 but kept out of the nine-area totals.
5. **Types.** "Homes" = the site's Home type (Property Type Detached 3,073, Semi-Detached 31, Other 22, blank 2, Garage 1, all ten cities). "Detached houses" = Property Type "Detached". "Condo type" = the site's Condo type (Condominium 2,764, Townhouse 319, Apartment 16). Status ACTIVE (6,167) and ACTIVE UNDER CONTRACT (61) are both counted; active-only figures are in the data file and change no share by more than 1 point.
6. **Leased land and remarks.** The description of every no-fee listing (859) was read again from its property page, 01:54 to 02:35 UTC. Listings whose remarks say the home is on leased land, and listings in the same MLS neighborhood or in a neighborhood named "Campground", are marked leased land (82: Ocean Lakes 80, Apache Campground 1, Bermuda Bay 1). Remarks were sorted by text pattern into: say no HOA, silent, voluntary association, mention an HOA or dues. The four "mention" listings were read by hand (row 20).
7. **Data file.** `batches/2026-10-b/data/no-hoa.json` holds the totals by area, the quartiles, the region and neighborhood counts, the fee-includes counts, the 42 no-fee condo-type listings, the 81 cross-check readings, and one line per listing (MLS number, area, type, status, list price, monthly fee, MLS region, MLS neighborhood, county, leased-land flag, remarks category). Any listing can be re-opened at `https://mbcoastalhomes.realgeeks.com/property/<MLS#>/`. Listings change daily; a re-count on a later day will differ a little. Re-check a sample, not the whole set.
8. **Medians.** Medians and quartiles (25th and 75th percentile, linear between values) of list prices and of the monthly fee field. List prices are asking prices.
9. **Statutes.** Read on scstatehouse.gov chapter pages (Title 27, Chapters 30, 31 and 50), current text with history notes. Court opinions from sccourts.org.
10. **Rules kept.** No row draws a conclusion about a named HOA, building or community. Named places in rows 19 and 20 are quoted from listing remarks only. No row records anything about who lives anywhere. No interest rate or payment amount is recorded.
