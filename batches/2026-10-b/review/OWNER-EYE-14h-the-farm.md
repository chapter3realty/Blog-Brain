### Low

**1. The short answer and the day say the gym, library and trails twice.**
- Short answer: "The county's gym, library and trails are minutes away by car."
- Day: "the county library and the Carolina Forest Recreation Center share one campus, about 3 minutes away by car ... For a longer outing, the county's Bike and Run Park has 7 miles of mountain bike and hiking trails, about 4 minutes by car."
- Instead: end the short answer on the price line, or give it a fact the day does not, such as the two pools and cable TV in the $107 fee.

**2. The preserve's meaning ends on a verifier's caveat.** "State law bars any private use of a heritage preserve, which rules out building homes there once the land is dedicated. The state has said it will dedicate it." The first sentence carries the meaning. The second is a status note.
- Instead: keep the first sentence and move the dedication status to the sources line, if the verifier allows. If it must stay, fold it in: "... which rules out building homes there once the state dedicates the land, as it has said it will."

**3. The homes section describes the houses but does not sell them.** "Houses for sale here have one or two stories, and most have about 1,450 to 3,200 square feet. A typical house lot is about a fifth of an acre." 14d called this an inventory; it is unchanged, and later rounds let it stand. It is not a field list, but it is the one place on the page with nothing a buyer could not guess.
- Instead: research what the houses give an owner: which streets back onto ponds or the state woods, the common floor plans and what they have (a first-floor bedroom, a screened porch, a two-car garage), and what changed in the houses built about a decade later. Add one or two of those, from verified rows. When the photo partner's pictures come, put a street of houses here.

**4. The location map's caption promises drive times; the map shows one.** "Where The Farm is, with drive times from the entrance nearest the large pool." The only time on the map is "Emergency room, about 5 min". Instead: "Where The Farm is. The drive to the emergency room starts at the entrance nearest the large pool." Or add the beach minutes from the tile to its label and take them off the tile.

**5. Carried from 14f and 14g, still open:**
- "The HOA can raise it by up to 20 percent a year." A cap with no history reads as a threat. Give how the fee has moved in recent budgets, then the cap.
- The overview aerial's orange edge is a hairline at phone width (`q390-04.png`). Use the location map's thicker line.
- The rules cards: the label ("Cars and boats") is the same size and weight as its text, and on desktop "The pools" sits alone on a second row (`q1280-09.png`). Bold the label and use a two-by-two grid.
- "To compare, see the best neighborhoods near Myrtle Beach ..." still ends the bombing range section. Give it its own line above the FAQ.

**6. Row 122 (the round 14g rewrite check) is pending verification.** Verify it before the page ships. It covers every changed sentence, including the two lines in findings 1 and 2.

## Best three things (keep them)

1. **The preserve, told once, with its payoff.** "Conway Medical Center planned a $160 million healthcare facility on the 353 acres along The Farm's north edge. The state bought the land instead". Then "South Carolina's largest wild population of Venus flytraps", then "rules out building homes there".
