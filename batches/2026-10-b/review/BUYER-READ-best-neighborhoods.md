## Answers

**What did I want to know first?** "Where should I look, and roughly what will it cost me?"

- Where to look: first screen (three boxes) and the short answer. Good.
- Rough cost: middle (about 1,900 px down, "Houses usually sell for about $350,000") and then in the tables. Not on the first screen.
- What the monthly neighborhood fee is and what it covers: never.
- What it is like to live there all year, not on vacation: never.

**Three things I liked about this place, from the page.**

1. Cherry Grove: a beach entrance about 0.1 mile from the middle of the neighborhood, so I could walk to the sand every morning like Lisa.
2. The ocean side of Surfside Beach: small streets between Business 17 and the beach, about 0.4 mile to a pay-lot beach entrance, and a Piggly Wiggly 3 minutes away.
3. The Farm at Carolina Forest: houses about $350,000, two pools, clubhouse and fitness room, not in a flood zone. It is 10 minutes to the beach, a reasonable price for what I get.

**Questions I still have, ranked.**

1. What will it all cost me per month: taxes, insurance (flood, and wind from storms), the neighborhood fee? The page gives sale prices and a fee, nothing else. No taxes, no insurance sizes.
2. Hurricanes. I have vacationed here and seen the signs. The page talks flood zones but never says storms.
3. What is it like to live here in January when the tourists are gone? In July in traffic?
4. What does the monthly neighborhood fee pay for?
5. Which of these is a neighborhood where people live all year, and which is rentals and vacationers?
6. Can I rent my place when I am not here?
7. How far is the airport, and which of these is closest to a hospital for my parents?
8. Am I too young for the 55+ ones?
9. What is Conway and why is it on a Myrtle Beach list?
10. Safety and crime. Nothing.

**Would I call this company?** Probably yes, to ask for "three neighborhoods", because they said plainly what I would get and I do not know where to start; but I would want to know what it costs me before I give a phone number.
