## Changes I would ask for, most important first

All low. None would make the owner send the page back.

1. **Low.** Pine Lakes. The table says "About $95 for a house". The text says "Homes for sale on the older streets near the club are listed with no HOA fee." The FAQ says the same. A reader asks which is true. Wanted: "The older streets near the club, outside the outlined parts, have no HOA fee."
2. **Low.** Two new-home prices sit below the typical price with no reason. Wild Wing: "About $600,000", then "Builders sold new homes here for about $430,000". Waterbridge: "About $630,000", then "about $555,000". Name the group in the sentence if a row says why, or keep only "builders are selling new homes here".
3. **Low.** Round where a person would round aloud: "townhomes $398,000" to "about $400,000"; "$300,000 to $356,000" to "about $300,000 to $355,000"; "About 415 homes and lots" to "About 400".
4. **Low.** "If no one in your home is 55 yet, skip this part." It talks about the page. Cut it.
5. **Low.** "We chose these 15 by what each one has: home prices, the HOA fee, the drive to the beach, golf and pools." A method sentence inside the short answer. Cut it and keep the HOA definition after it.
6. **Low.** Overview map: dots 5, 8, 9, 11 and 15 (Waterbridge, Plantation Lakes, Berkshire Forest, Forestbrook Estates, Prince Creek) are numbers only. The key under the map is right. Name them where they fit.
7. **Low.** "Is Myrtle Beach a good place to live?" is in PLAN.md's searches and is not in the FAQ. One answer from what the area has (the beach, prices, drive times, nothing closes in winter but attractions), never who lives there.
8. **Low.** The story's first sentence: "Megan wants a house near the beach that costs less, so she can drive to the beach every morning." The "so" reads as if a lower price lets her drive. Wanted: "Megan wants a house that costs less, a short drive from the beach. She plans to drive to the beach every morning."
9. **Low.** "The highways we use, a few miles inland, avoid the stop lights on the main road along the coast." It names no road the reader can use. Cut it, or name the roads if a row supports it.
10. **Low.** "The middle of each is under half a mile from a public path to the beach." "The middle of each" is an odd phrase. Wanted: "The center of each is under half a mile from a public path to the beach."
11. **Low.** "FEMA's flood map" is the one abbreviation not spelled out. The live Myrtle Trace page says "the government's flood map". Use that wording. (Same on no-HOA and The Farm.)
