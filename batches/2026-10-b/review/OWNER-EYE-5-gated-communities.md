## Low findings (for the list; they do not hold the page)

1. **"A house in Myrtle Trace, near Conway, fits her budget."** The story names her budget, then she buys in Seasons. The page shows Seasons costs more: about $500,000 against $300,000 in the tables, and about $6,100 a year against $2,300 in the fee section, which names these same two places. A careful reader may wonder if the agent moved her up in price. The story does not need the budget. Write "She finds a house in Myrtle Trace, near Conway." It is a four-word change and can go in with the upload.
2. **Litchfield by the Sea condo fees are higher than the page's range.** Every closed condo sale in rows 90 to 92 shows a monthly fee of about $580 or more. The first screen says "HOA fee in most: About $90 to $400 a month". "Most" keeps that true, and the verifier ruled out a fee figure. If the verifier allows it, one sentence would help an ocean condo buyer: "Condo fees there are higher than in the other neighborhoods on this page." Do not add it without a verifier's yes.
3. **"whole condos".** "In the last year, whole condos there sold for..." A reader does not know what a condo that is not whole would be. The sources line already says shared-week sales are left out. "condos" is enough in the sentence.
4. **The first map still has no "Myrtle Beach" label.** The caption says where the city is. Carried from round 3.
5. **"Gatehouse: A building at the gate. Ask us about hours."** If no source gives hours, write "Hours differ by neighborhood." Carried from round 3.
6. **Table cell wording.** The verifier's corrected cell for row 92 was "Differs by section and by unit"; the page says "Differs by section". Both read plainly. The verifier should confirm the shorter one.
