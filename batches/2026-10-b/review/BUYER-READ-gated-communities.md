1. Are these places actually safe? Does a gate or guard change anything? Which have a real person at the gate and which have only a keypad?

**Would I call this company?** Maybe: the page was clear and honest about the price and the back-gate trap, so I would trust it, but I would want one clear answer on safety and what a monthly bill really is before I called.
