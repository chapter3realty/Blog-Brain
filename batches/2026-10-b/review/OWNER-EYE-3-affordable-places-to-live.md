## Changes I would ask for, most important first

All low.

1. **Low.** Each town section repeats the table: value, drive to Myrtle Beach, drive to the beach, tax. The FAQ repeats Loris and Aynor a third time. CRAFT 5: "Say each fact once, where it belongs." Keep the value as each section's first sentence. Cut the drive and tax sentences from the town sections, and keep the hospital, the flood line and what is different.
2. **Low.** The down-payment section has about ten numbers. The Homebuyer Program needs its numbers; they decide who qualifies. The other two do not. Wanted: "SC Housing also runs Palmetto Home Advantage and County First, with similar help. The lender tells you which one fits." Cut "On a $250,000 loan, that is up to $10,000."
3. **Low.** Aynor is the only one of the five with no picture. A government aerial of an ordinary street of houses on wide lots in Aynor, like the one on the no-HOA page, would show "the most room" without words.
4. **Low.** "This page covers Horry County and the Georgetown County coast." It is about the page. Cut it.
5. **Low.** "Few homes in Aynor sell each year, so there are few to choose from, and the typical value is less certain." "Less certain" is a hedge about our own number. Wanted: "Few homes in Aynor come up for sale each year."
6. **Low.** The two offer boxes end differently: "the programs that fit" and "the down-payment programs that fit". Use one.
