## Medium

**Medium 1. Litchfield by the Sea gives the reader no price or fee to plan with, and one of its sentences is the verifier's.** Entry 18:
- "The HOA fee and the prices differ from section to section and from unit to unit." This is the `use` text of `gated-communities-struck.json`, row 92, pasted. It would be true of any place with more than one building.
- "In the last year, condos sold for anywhere from under $200,000 to over $2 million, and houses in its Oystercatcher Island section for about $750,000." Row 90's struck pattern bans "its condos sold for about $180,000"; the page reworded the same range around it. A range from $200,000 to $2 million tells a buyer nothing.
- Table: "Condos under $200,000 to over $2 million | Differs by section and unit".

Round 11 named this exact fault: "a verifier's sentence pasted in", and "facts weakened, not fixed". It is the only entry of the twenty with neither a usable price nor a trade-off. The owner's words: "surface level", and CRAFT 5, "Give a number the reader can plan with."

Want:
- Cut the fee sentence. In its place, what we do: "The HOA fee differs from building to building, so we find out the fee for any home you like before you offer." (Row 92's verifier allows no fee amount, so this is the honest answer, in our voice.)
- Price: row 90 holds the middle condo sale, about $645,000. If the verifier allows it, "Condos here usually sell for about $645,000, and houses in its Oystercatcher Island section for about $750,000." in the facts line, and "Condos about $645,000; Oystercatcher Island houses about $750,000" in the table. If not, keep the range once, in the facts line, without "anywhere from".

I checked both suggested sentences against the struck list; neither matches.
