## What the page forgot that a buyer searches for

- **House or townhome.** Only four of the twelve are houses (Birchwood, Golf and Yacht Club, Indigo Grove, Polo Farms). A first-time buyer who wants a yard has to read every entry to find them. The chart labels could say "house" or "townhome" in every row.
- **Room to grow.** It is in the audience brief and still thin. Polo Farms' fenced yards and Birchwood's quarter acre touch it. Research the kind of fact that shows it: lot sizes, lofts, plans with a fourth bedroom, garages that fit a workbench.
- **Insurance.** The page warns about flood and the Amber Bluff assessment. A budget buyer also searches what insurance costs on a townhome versus a house here. Name it as a cost we find out, in the final offer, if no verified figure exists.
