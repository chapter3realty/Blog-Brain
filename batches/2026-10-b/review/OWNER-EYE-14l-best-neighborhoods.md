### Medium

**M1. Grande Dunes and The Farm sell no day.**
- Grande Dunes gives a building style and a course award from 2009, then "Most houses for sale here are new or not yet finished."
- The Farm: "A buyer can choose a one-story or a two-story house here" is true of anywhere. "Our guide to The Farm ... has its pools, its schools and its HOA rules" sends the reader away.

Do: for Grande Dunes, research what owners use day to day (the club, the waterway, a walk, a place to eat) that the gated page does not own. For The Farm, bring in one line of daily life from its guide's rows, and cut the one-story sentence.

**M2. Tidewater sells borrowed places.** It is the short answer's golf pick, and its guide line is "houses on the fairways". The entry leads with "a neck of land between the Intracoastal Waterway and Cherry Grove Inlet". It then gives a paddling trail that "runs 30 miles, from Socastee to Little River" and a park "about 17 minutes from Tidewater by car". Both are true of any house on that waterway.
Do: lead with the golf. Research what the marsh and the inlet give an owner from their own street. Cut the trail and the park.

**M3. A new slot: "The trade-off is", in 6 of 15 entries.**
- Cherry Grove: "flood insurance"
- Tidewater: "of a public course"
- Barefoot: "the condo fee"
- Market Common: "a townhome's HOA fee"
- Forestbrook: "the yard"
- Avalon: "age"

`repeat-check` does not see it. Two are fees that the price line and the table already give (14k L6).
Do: keep a downside only where it changes a choice (Cherry Grove's flood insurance, Tidewater's golfers, Deerfield's building site). Say it the way an agent would, with no label. Cut the two fee trade-offs.

**M4. A new sibling repeat.** Tidewater: "Vereen Memorial Gardens, a free county park, has about three miles of nature trails and boardwalks." No-HOA: "Vereen Memorial Historical Gardens ... has about 3 miles of trails and boardwalks."
Do: leave it to the no-HOA page (M2 cuts it here), and record that in SIBLING-OWNERS.

**M5. The top grew.** The dek is longer: "Fifteen neighborhoods near Myrtle Beach, grouped by what each does best, with the usual price and HOA fee." The top went from 2.22 to 2.32 screens.
Do: cut the dek.

**M6. Forestbrook is the thinnest entry (73 words) and has a reporter's line.** It leads with a garden plot by the pool. Its middle line is "Lennar developed it from 2018 on, and every house the county approved is now built: 500 houses, with about 105 townhomes". The guide lists it under "New houses", but the entry says building is finished.
Do: find a lead a buyer would choose it for, and write "built since 2018". If it stays under "New houses", say why a buyer gets a newer house here.
