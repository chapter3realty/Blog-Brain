These match:

- The house fee of $95 and about $1,100 a year (no-HOA).
- The share with no fee: Loris 60% and Aynor 67% (no-HOA) against "about six in ten" and "about two in three" (affordable). Conway 23% with no fee against "most have an HOA fee" (affordable).
- "Rush hour adds 5 to 10 minutes" on three pages.
- Myrtle Trace's HOA fee plus tax of about $2,300 (gated) agrees with the live page ($95 a month plus about $1,200 tax).

Mismatches to fix:

1. **Grande Dunes price and fee.**
   - Best neighborhoods: "Grande Dunes, some sections | $1.2 million to $2.9 million | About $200 to $400".
   - Gated: "Grande Dunes: Calais, Palermo and Villa Venezia | ... | About $200 to $375 a month | About $1.5 million".
   - Both say "three sections". Use one set of sections with one price and fee, or name the different sections on best-neighborhoods.

2. **Tidewater's gate.**
   - Best neighborhoods: "Tidewater Plantation has a guard house at its gate, where visitors check in."
   - Gated: "Gatehouse: A building at the gate. Ask us about hours. Tidewater Plantation, ..."
   - "Guard house" promises a guard that the gated page does not verify. Use "gatehouse" on both.

3. **The 55+ rule.**
   - Best neighborhoods: "A 55+ community is for homes where at least one person is 55 or older. Federal rules require that in at least 80 percent of its occupied homes, and Myrtle Trace requires it in every home."
   - Gated: "In a 55+ neighborhood, at least one person in each home must be 55 or older."
   - The gated sentence is wrong as a general rule. Use the best-neighborhoods wording.

4. **The Farm's yearly cost against its own rows (one page, two pages affected).**
   - The Farm: "plan on about $2,600 a year" covers the fee and tax, then lists a third row: "Stormwater fee: About $90 a year more".
   - Best neighborhoods: "The Farm house... In all, a year: About $2,600".
   - The totals match, but a reader who adds The Farm's rows gets $2,700. Fold the fee into the tax row or leave it out, and keep $2,600 on both pages.

5. **Two kinds of price for one town.** These are not wrong, but a reader moving between pages sees different numbers for one place without being told why.
   - No-HOA: "about $370,000 in Loris and Aynor" (asking price of houses with no fee).
   - Affordable: "A typical home in Loris is worth about $255,000" and Aynor "about $290,000".
   - Inside affordable: Conway is "worth about $290,000", and "a house usually sells for about $265,000".
   - The no-HOA page should name its measure in the sentence ("houses for sale with no HOA fee are listed at about $370,000"). Affordable should fix Conway (see its file).

6. **Barefoot Resort to the beach (low).**
   - Best neighborhoods: "Barefoot Resort... about 6 minutes from the beach by car."
   - Gated: "Leatherleaf (Barefoot Resort) | About 10 minutes".
   - Different sections, so both can be true. Name the point each is measured from, or give the gated row as "Leatherleaf".
