## Medium

**Medium 1. Indigo Grove reads like a builder's catalog and a sales count.** Entry 10:
- "Lennar sells it as The Overlook and sold about 145 houses here in about ten months."
- "Lennar lists nine floor plans for The Overlook. The smallest, the Beckman, is one story, with three bedrooms in about 1,260 square feet. The largest, the Columbia, has five bedrooms in about 2,360. Most lots are about 0.2 acre."
- "None of the lots sold here last year is in a high-risk flood zone."
- "North Myrtle Beach is about 12 minutes away by car, and downtown Conway about 35."

"Lennar lists" is the attribution CRAFT 5 bans ("the builder says"). Floor-plan names mean nothing to a buyer. The sales count and "lots sold here last year" are the records voice round 10 asked to leave. The second paragraph has six numbers; CRAFT 5 allows three. The entry still has no trade-off line and nothing an owner does on a normal day; it is the only one of the twelve without either. Want, from rows already held:
- "Indigo Grove is new: Lennar, which sells it as The Overlook, built most of it in the past year."
- "Its houses run from a one-story house with three bedrooms to a two-story house with five, on lots of about a fifth of an acre." (Say "two-story" only if the row does.)
- "Indigo Grove is outside the high-risk flood zone."
- One line on daily life: what the 22 acres of open space are (ponds, a path, a park), or Griffin's IGA on SC 90, which the aerial already labels.
- One trade-off line: "The trade-off is the drive to Myrtle Beach: Coastal Grand Mall is about 35 minutes away." (the schools table has it), or no pool, if the HOA has none.
