### Medium

**M1. 2. The Fairways at Wild Wing sells a press release.** "The club now has a renovated pro shop, upgraded practice facilities and a refreshed clubhouse." That is the club's news, not a day there, and "now" will date. Do instead: one thing an owner does at Wild Wing that no other entry has (kinds of fact: a league or event at the club, where owners eat). Keep "so a Saturday can go to golf instead of yard work".

**M2. 4. Birchwood still has no place in it.** The builder list became "FEMA maps every lot in Birchwood as an area of minimal flood hazard. High-speed internet is included in the monthly dues." Useful, but no day near Longs. Keep the value line about the 1980s house in the Golf and Yacht Club. Do instead: add one thing living off SC 905 gives an owner (kinds: a river landing or park, a place to eat or an event in Longs).

**M3. 3. Sawgrass East leads right, then stops.** The new lead is what 14i asked: the fee pays the building insurance, lawn, water, sewer and trash, so "An owner then has no water or trash bill of their own". Then the day is "a Publix is in the Forest Square center", and a link sends the reader to a policy guide. The chart label still says "garage, lawn care in fee". Do instead: label it with the insurance and bills the fee covers. Replace the Publix clause with one fact only this spot has (kinds: what owners use the clubhouse and lakes for, what the recreation center offers).

**M4. 5. Golf and Yacht Club: the name promises golf, and the day is the pool's hours.** A budget buyer reads the name and assumes a course. The gated page has the row: "there is no golf or yacht club anymore." The day paragraph is "From mid-April to mid-October, the pool opens at 8 a.m. for lap swimming, then an exercise hour, then open swim until 8 p.m. or sundown." He said to gloss the pool. Do instead: one clause that the name is historical, from the gated page's row; cut the pool hours; let Osprey Marina carry the day.

**M5. The top is 2.29 screens and says "half" twice.** The dek ("Affordable places where homes sell for about half the typical price of a Myrtle Beach house") repeats the short answer, and the chart title says it again. Do instead: cut the dek. That saves about 100 px and the repeat.
