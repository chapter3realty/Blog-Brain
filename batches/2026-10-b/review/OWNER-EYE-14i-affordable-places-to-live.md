**M2. Three entries sell the house, not the place.**
- **3. Sawgrass East.** The lead is a spec line, and it starts the same word twice: "Many Sawgrass East townhomes have three bedrooms. Many of the three-bedroom ones have a one-car garage and the main bedroom downstairs." (This came from the 14h fix for "lately".) Then a field list in prose: 255 townhomes, mid-2000s, three lakes, a pool, a clubhouse, the boulevard, 2.5 miles to US 501. The last paragraph joins two unrelated sentences: "The county's Carolina Forest library and recreation center are on the same boulevard. Sawgrass East's HOA fee is the second highest here, after Wynbrooke's." Do instead:
  - Lead with the thing in the entry a budget buyer could not guess: the fee pays the insurance on the buildings, plus the lawn, water, sewer and trash. Say what that means for the buyer's own bills, from a verified row.
  - Then the home in one sentence: three bedrooms, a garage, the main bedroom downstairs.
  - Then a normal day. Kinds of fact to research: what owners use the clubhouse and lakes for, the drive to work or to the beach, what is on Carolina Forest Boulevard besides the library.
  - That turns "second highest fee" from a complaint into a sell. Then the trade-off can be something else, or the fee once in plain words.
- **4. Birchwood.** The empty line ("Long Bay Club, a golf course, is in Longs too") is gone, as 14h asked. The new line sells value well: "A new house here sells for about what a 1980s house does in the Golf and Yacht Club, closer to Myrtle Beach." Keep it. But the place still gets only "off SC 905, southwest of Longs, and North Myrtle Beach and its beaches are about 20 minutes by car", then the trade-off that Myrtle Beach is far. The builder's list stays: "granite counters, stainless steel appliances, 9-foot ceilings on the first floor and energy-efficient windows". That is true of most new houses. Do instead: replace the builder's list with one thing only living near Longs gives an owner. Kinds of fact to research: a river landing or park near SC 905, what the town of Longs has (a place to eat, an event), the course in Longs and what it is known for.
- **2. The Fairways at Wild Wing.** Unchanged since 14g. The lead ("inside Wild Wing Plantation, a golf community with 27 holes, and some are near the course") is what the name already says. The place line is "Conway Medical Center is about two miles from the Fairways, and an Aldi is closer still." Do instead: give Wild Wing one thing only it has, without the best page's Avocet facts. Kinds of fact: what residents get at the club, the clubhouse or an event there, what is on that stretch of US 501. Keep "so a Saturday can go to golf instead of yard work"; it is the best line in the entry.
