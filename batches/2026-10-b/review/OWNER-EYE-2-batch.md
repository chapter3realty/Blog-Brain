Mismatches, all low:

1. **Public beach access, two definitions.**
   - Best neighborhoods: "A public beach access is a public path to the beach. Some have parking and some do not."
   - Gated: "a public beach access, a spot where anyone can park and walk onto the beach."
   - The gated wording is wrong where an access has no parking. Use the best-neighborhoods sentence on both.

2. **The 55+ "every home" sentence.**
   - Best neighborhoods: "Federal rules require that in at least 80 percent of its occupied homes, and Myrtle Trace requires it in every home."
   - Gated: "...and Myrtle Trace and Seasons at Prince Creek West require it in every home."
   - Both are true (Seasons ledger 2026-10-a, row 5), but a reader of both pages may take best-neighborhoods to mean Seasons does not. Use the gated sentence on both.

3. **The Farm's stormwater fee.** The Farm says "plan on about $2,600 a year", then adds "a stormwater fee of about $90 a year". Best neighborhoods says $2,600. Either both say about $2,700 with the fee in the tax, or neither mentions the $90.

4. **Three numbers for "Surfside Beach" (each names its own group, so this is allowed).**

   - No-HOA: "Surfside Beach and Garden City... $985,000" (houses listed with no HOA fee).
   - Affordable: "Surfside Beach $360,000" (typical value of all homes).
