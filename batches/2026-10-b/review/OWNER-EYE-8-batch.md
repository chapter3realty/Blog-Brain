## The root cause, again

ROUND-8 fixed the old repetition (the gate in every paragraph, the same question per area). The writers then reached for the next uniform field and repeated that:
- gated: rice plantations (13 times, one sentence word for word in two entries) and "[designer] course opened in [year]" in ten entries;
- affordable: inside street names in nine of twelve entries, and a builder-sale sentence in five;
- best neighborhoods: a flood line in seven entries, "[park] is [distance] away" in nine, "on this page" price comparisons in six;
- no HOA: Aynor, Loris and Conway are one paragraph three times; a county boat landing in four entries;
- The Farm: the sunk pool and the special assessment four times.

CRAFT 5 already says it: "A device can repeat too ... Use a device where it is most striking, at most two or three times a page." The writers did not run that test after the round 8 rewrite. A suggestion: before handing a page on, list the first fact of every entry and every entry's second paragraph side by side. If two share a shape, one changes.

## SEO and AEO, across the set

- Every H1 is the searched question. Every numbered H2 names the place. Good.
- ItemList is on all four list pages, with every anchor present. The head (Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList) comes from mkpage at build; the preview strips it.
- Weak first sentences, the ones answer engines lift: best neighborhoods opens with "depends on what you want close by"; gated answers "where" with "from North Myrtle Beach to Pawleys Island"; no HOA opens with a share, not a place. Each page file gives a replacement that names places.
- A few FAQ answers do not say yes or no (gated Surfside, no-HOA campground, best "Carolina Forest or Myrtle Beach better").
- What would make the set rank or be quoted more: first sentences that name places; one history fact per entry (the quotable lines are already the history ones: Cherry Grove's 1950 inlet, Pine Lakes and Sports Illustrated, Seaside's shag festivals, Victory Point's speedway); FAQs that use the names people search ("Is Barefoot Resort gated?", "cheapest places to live near Myrtle Beach").

## Guarding against stretching his words

Each finding above quotes the page and ties to his words or to CRAFT. I did not ask for new sections. Most fixes cut words: mediums on repetition, filler and devices shorten the two longest pages. The depth findings (best H1, gated M2, affordable M4, The Farm M4) ask for facts from rows already held where they exist (The Farm's history is on the sibling page) and new rows where they do not. The owner's test, "is it simpler for the reader?", passes for each: one fact once, in its place, and something new in every entry.
