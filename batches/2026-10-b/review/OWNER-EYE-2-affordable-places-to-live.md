## Changes I would ask for, most important first

1. **Low.** Each town section repeats the table word for word: the value, the drive to Myrtle Beach, the drive to the beach and the tax. Then the FAQ repeats Loris and Aynor a third time ("How far is Loris from Myrtle Beach?"). CRAFT 5: "Say each fact once, where it belongs." Keep the value as the first sentence of each section (answer engines lift it). Cut the drive and tax sentences from the town sections, and keep the hospital, the flood line and what is different about each place.
2. **Low.** The down-payment section has ten numbers. The Homebuyer Program needs its numbers, because they decide who qualifies. The other two programs do not. Wanted, in one line: "SC Housing also runs Palmetto Home Advantage and County First, with similar help. The lender tells you which one fits." Cut "On a $250,000 loan, that is up to $10,000."
3. **Low.** Aynor is the only place with no picture. A NAIP aerial of an ordinary street of houses in Aynor, like the one on the no-HOA page, would show "the most room" without words.
4. **Low.** "This page covers Horry County and the Georgetown County coast." It is about the page, not the homes. Cut it.
5. **Low.** "Few homes in Aynor sell each year, so there are few to choose from, and the typical value is less certain." "Less certain" is a hedge about our measure. Wanted: "Few homes in Aynor come up for sale each year."
6. **Low.** In the Georgetown harbor photo, the boat at the right edge shows part of a name on its hull. Crop a little off the right.
7. **Low.** The two offer boxes end differently: "the programs that fit" and "the down-payment programs that fit". Use one.

## The story, read once

"Dana wants a house in Conway but thinks she must save for years, so her agent shows her the state's down-payment program and puts her in touch with a lender that works with it, and she starts looking now." One read was enough. No numbers, no "if", five sentences. The agent refers her to a lender and does not arrange the money, which keeps Chapter3 a brokerage. It has its own arc in the batch and copies no live story.
