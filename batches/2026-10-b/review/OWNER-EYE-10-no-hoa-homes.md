2. "In the late 1800s it was also a farm center for the country around it." It comes after "became a town in 1902", so the order runs backward. Put it before the railroad sentence, or cut it.
3. "The typical asking price is about $520,000 with no HOA fee, against about $415,000 with a fee. Many of them are near the ocean, on the ocean side of US 17 Business, the coast road." "Many of them" has no clear subject. "Many of the houses with no HOA fee are on the ocean side of US 17 Business, the coast road, which is why they cost more."
4. "The typical house for sale here is about 2,400 square feet, the largest typical size on this list." The only ranking line on the page; "about 2,400 square feet, the largest of the eight areas" reads cleaner, or drop the ranking.
