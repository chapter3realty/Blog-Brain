## Changes I would ask for, most important first

All low.

1. **Low.** "Plan on about $2,600 a year ... That covers the HOA fee and the county property tax." Then: "The county also adds a stormwater fee of about $90 a year to the tax bill." A careful reader gets $2,700. The live Seasons page counts the same county fee inside its tax ("the county's drainage fee included"). CRAFT 2: a total on two pages is built from the same parts. Wanted: "Property tax: about $1,400 a year, with the county's stormwater fee", a total of about $2,700, and the same on best neighborhoods.
2. **Low.** "The Defense Department or a later owner may keep monitoring that land." A reader asks what that means for them. Cut it.
3. **Low.** The sunken pool and the one-time charge are told four times: things to do, the cons, the story and the FAQ. Cut "The board has not said what the work will cost. By a majority vote, it can add a one-time charge..." from the things-to-do section.
4. **Low.** "The large pool has sunk into the ground more than it should, which engineers call settling." Wanted: "The ground under the large pool has sunk, and the pool has sunk with it."
5. **Low.** "To add a gate, about three out of four owners would have to agree to ask a court first." It answers a question nobody asks after "No". Cut it.
6. **Low.** "FEMA's flood map". Use "the government's flood map", as the live Myrtle Trace page does.
7. **Low.** "about 1,450 to 3,200 square feet" to "about 1,500 to 3,200 square feet".
8. **Low.** The pool aerial: the "Large pool" label sits on the dark pond, and only its right end touches the pool. The blue pool with the palm islands is to the right of it. Move the label onto the pool deck, or add a leader to the pool.
9. **Low.** The street and pool aerials are winter frames, brown and bare. True, but not inviting. A summer frame of the same view would look better.
