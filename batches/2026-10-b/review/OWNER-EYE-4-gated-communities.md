## What I would send back, most important first

1. **Medium. Litchfield by the Sea: "HOA fee: Ask us" and "Homes usually sell for: Ask us".**
   - The first tile on the first screen and the first "Which one fits you" card both send an ocean buyer to Litchfield by the Sea. The reader taps it and lands on a card where both money lines say "Ask us". The owner's words: "the answer is just go find out." DeBordieu, the other ocean place, has both numbers.
   - The ledger says why: the MLS returned nothing under "Litchfield by the Sea", and its sections list under their own names, which were "not attempted" (`facts/gated-communities-facts.md`, the not-verified notes). So there is a lawful source no one has tried yet.
   - Wanted:
     - Closed sales from the MLS for the last 12 months, searched by Litchfield by the Sea's section names, checked by a verifier. This community has condos and villas as well as houses, so one typical price could mislead. Give a range in the cell, labelled by kind of home if that helps, such as "Condos about $X; houses about $Y".
     - The HOA fee from those listings, if two listings agree for the kind of home shown.
     - If no verified number exists by the end of the round, do not print "Ask us" twice. Leave both cells empty, and under the table add one plain sentence: Litchfield by the Sea has many sections, from villas to houses on the ocean, each with its own fee and price. The "Get this week's list" box that follows the table already offers the homes for sale.

2. **Medium. The story has no problem in it, so it shows no skill.**

   - The owner asked for a story "showing Chapter3's skill" with "some tension" (2026-10-11). ## Low findings (for the list; they do not hold the page)

1. **The Reserve has two values.** The table says "About $1.35 million"; the chart says "$1.4 million". It comes from 13 sales spread from $850,000 to $2.69 million, so "$1.35 million" is more precise than the sample supports. Use "About $1.4 million" in the table. This is a one-word fix and should go in with the two mediums.
2. **Leatherleaf and The Bluffs have two values.** The text says "the few homes that sold went for about $700,000 to $975,000"; both table cells say "About $700,000 to $1 million". Each comes from 4 or 5 sales. Use the text's range in both cells, so the page gives one figure. The Bluffs' own sales start at $730,000; "about $700,000" covers it.
3. **The first map still has no "Myrtle Beach" label.** The caption says where the city is. Best neighborhoods labels it on the map.
4. **"Gatehouse: A building at the gate. Ask us about hours."** If no source gives hours, write "Hours differ by neighborhood."
5. **"Read what a gate does and does not do before you choose one."** It talks about the page. The "On this page" list already links the section.
6. **The DeBordieu photo's "Beach" label has no leader line to its dot.** The label sits on the homes behind the beach, and the dot sits on the sand to its upper right, unconnected. "DeBordieu Colony" has a leader. Add one to "Beach", or move the label onto the sand.
