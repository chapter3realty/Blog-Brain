## High

**H1. Grande Dunes and Forestbrook Estates do not sell the place.**
- **Grande Dunes** opens with a caveat: "Most houses for sale here are new or not yet finished." The Mizner paragraph then ends flat: "Most of the new houses listed for sale here are plainer, with fiber-cement siding."
- **Forestbrook Estates** opens with "Every home in Forestbrook Estates is outside the high-risk flood zone, and the houses are built around ponds." That is the pond he told us to gloss over. The entry is 64 words.

Do: open each with an owner's normal day there, then the thing only it has. For Grande Dunes, research daily life the gated page does not own. For Forestbrook, research what is close by: the road to the beach, parks, shops. Move the flood line after the life.

## Medium

**M1. Tidewater is 82 words and shares its lead with the gated page.** This page says "About seven of every ten houses ... border the golf course". The gated page says "About seven in ten of the house lots touch the course".
Do: give one page the lot share. Give this page its own angle on daily life by the inlet and the marsh.

**M2. Listing data in the copy.** For example:
- "Most of the houses listed for sale here are raised beach houses."
- "The condos listed for sale here have four bedrooms, and most come furnished."
- "Houses listed here in recent years usually have about 1,900 square feet."
- "of about 640 houses in Prince Creek outside the Seasons section, about 19 ..."

Do: say what the homes are like, as an agent would. Put the method in the sources line.

**M3. Several entries never say where the place is.** These are Plantation Lakes, Waterbridge, Forestbrook, Pine Lakes and Prince Creek. The FAQ says "The four Carolina Forest neighborhoods here" but never names them.
Do: add one clause per entry naming an area the reader knows.

**M4. The top says one thing three times.** The dek says "Fifteen neighborhoods ... grouped by what each does best". The guide's heading asks "What does each neighborhood do best?" The short answer names six picks that the guide names again a few lines lower.
Do: cut the dek. Trim the short answer to two or three picks.

**M5. Barefoot Resort has no trade-off, and it never says which sections it means.** It says "these sections" and "these Barefoot sections" without naming them. "A second round the same day at a lower rate" is a fact for a visitor, not an owner.
Do: name the sections once, give an owner's day, and add one honest trade-off.
