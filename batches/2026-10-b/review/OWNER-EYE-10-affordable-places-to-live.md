## Medium

**Medium 1. The prose says again what the facts block above it says.**
- Parkview. Block: "Townhomes from 2004 and 2005, 2 or 3 bedrooms". Prose: "The townhomes date from the middle 2000s, many with two bedrooms".
- Sawgrass East. Block: "Townhomes from 2005 and 2006, 2 or 3 bedrooms". Prose: "Built in 2005 and 2006, the townhomes are around small ponds ... Some have only two bedrooms."
- Forestbrook Village. Block: "New two-story townhomes, 3 bedrooms". Prose: "Each townhome is two stories, with a one-car garage, and internet is included."
- Villas at Sandridge. Block: "New two-story townhomes, 3 bedrooms". Prose: "Each is two stories, with a one-car garage."
- Polo Farms. Block: "One-story houses from 2005 to 2017, resales" and "About $20 a month". Prose: "The houses date from 2005 on, every sale last year was a resale, and the monthly fee is small."

Forestbrook Village and Sandridge also share one sentence shape. Want: the prose keeps only what the block does not say (the one-car garage, internet included, the ponds, the pool), and the age and bedrooms live in the block alone.

**Medium 2. Errand drive times became the repeated device, and they push the number count up.** `repeat-check`: "about [number] minutes by car" in three sections; "drive" in 7 of 12 entries, "minutes" in 6.
- Wild Wing: "Errands are short: an Aldi is about three minutes by car, and a Walmart Supercenter about five. Horry-Georgetown Technical College and Coastal Carolina University are each a seven- or eight-minute drive."
- Sawgrass East: "They sold for less than the new townhomes at Victory Point, eleven minutes away by car to the southwest. A Publix is six minutes west by car, and the Carolina Forest Recreation Center six minutes east."
- Villas at Sandridge: "It is about five minutes by car to the southwest, across the highway interchange. Marinas on the Intracoastal Waterway are six to eight minutes east by car. A Food Lion, an Aldi and a Kroger are six to eight minutes away."
- Wynbrooke: "Groceries are close: a Lowes Foods and a Publix near SC 707 are each about two minutes away by car, to the northwest. The TPC Myrtle Beach golf course is a five-minute drive west, and the Murrells Inlet MarshWalk about five minutes to the southeast."
- Forestbrook Village: "Forestbrook Grocery is about a minute away by car. Forestbrook Middle and Forestbrook Elementary take four to five minutes to reach, driving west."

Each entry already has a beach drive in its block, and the table after the list gives the mall drive. Two or three more drive times per entry, each with a compass direction, read as a data sheet. CRAFT 5: "Three numbers in a paragraph at most", and a device "at most two or three times a page". Want: one errand per entry, the one that matters to this buyer, without the direction ("A Publix is about six minutes away."). Cut the Victory Point comparison in Sawgrass East; the chart ranks. Give the freed sentence to what an owner does there.

**Medium 3. Indigo Grove and Polo Farms describe what is around them, not what living there is like.**
- Indigo Grove: "Coastal Grand Mall, on the other hand, is a long drive. Of the three of the twelve near Longs, Indigo Grove has the shortest drive to the beach. Before the houses, this land was fields and woods." and "Riverside Elementary is the zoned elementary school." (the table after the list says it again).
- Polo Farms: "Around it are woods, pine plantations, fields and a few homes along SC 905. Todd Swamp, a stream with wooded wetland along it, is about a mile to the southwest. Horry County Fire Rescue's Red Bluff station is under two miles away by road."

"Before the houses, this land was fields and woods" is true of almost every new neighborhood on the page (Birchwood says "fields, woods and older homes" too). A fire station two miles off and a swamp a mile off are map facts, not a reason to live there. ROUND-10 asked each entry for "what living there is like: what is nearby, what an owner does on a normal day, the history of the land". Want, from new rows: for Indigo Grove, what the Lennar houses come with and whether it has a pool or a park ("The Overlook" may have a sales page with the amenity list); for Polo Farms, the lot sizes (a small fee usually means bigger lots and no shared pool; say so if the parcels show it), who built it, and that the low fee means no pool or clubhouse. Keep the flood trade-off, which is real.

**Medium 4. The Golf and Yacht Club's gate is stated on one page and doubted on the other.** This page: "We check the HOA papers for what the fee covers, including any gate and security." The gated page, entry 9: "a gatehouse at the entrance on Bay Road". A reader who follows the link reads "any gate" here and a gatehouse there. Want: "A gatehouse stands at the entrance on Bay Road; our guide to gated communities covers the neighborhood's gate." and keep "We check the HOA papers for what the fee covers."

## Low

1. "Drive times are from the middle of each neighborhood with no traffic. In the owner's experience, rush hour adds 5 to 10 minutes." A reader does not know which owner. The Farm page says "In our experience". Use that.
2. The map numbers Coastal Grand Mall as 13, like a thirteenth neighborhood, and dots 6, 7 and 9 carry no name. Use a different marker for the mall.
3. The first screens say "Twelve" three times: the hero ("Twelve neighborhoods, the roads to them, and what a home costs."), the lede ("Twelve affordable neighborhoods near Myrtle Beach ...") and "These are the twelve neighborhoods within about 20 minutes of a beach ...". The lede can give the price range alone.
4. "You apply for that rate; it is not automatic." The one reader to-do left. "We remind you to apply after closing" if the agents do it.
5. "The county records it as Village at Sandridge, and new townhomes here were still selling in September 2026." A date in the body copy and a records note. "Dream Finders Homes is still selling new townhomes here." If the county name matters for a search, put it in the sources line.
6. The down-payment section has many numbers in five short paragraphs ($10,000, 15 years, about $115,000, about $134,000, 640, $450,000, 4 percent, 10 years). Keep the $10,000, the income limit and the price cap; link the rest to the programs page.
7. Three aerials show the land before the homes: Birchwood, Forestbrook Village and Indigo Grove. The captions say so honestly, but a field does not show what you get. Put these three first on the photo partner's list.
8. Indigo Grove's facts block says only "New houses"; every other block gives the bedrooms.
9. "For a buyer who works in Myrtle Beach or has children in school". It speaks to the reader, so it is lawful, but the site avoids family words. "For the drive to work in Myrtle Beach, and the zoned schools for each place".
10. "The beach in North Myrtle Beach is much closer than Coastal Grand Mall; the table after the list gives both drives." Talks about the page. Give the two minutes or cut.
11. The facts-block labels ("Fee and tax a year, besides the mortgage and insurance") are set smaller than body text on a phone (`aff-8-15.jpg`). CRAFT 4: labels a reader must read are body size.
