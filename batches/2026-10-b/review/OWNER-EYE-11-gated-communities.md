This is the `use` text from `gated-communities-struck.json` (row 161), word for word. Whose outpost, of what, when? The reader cannot picture it. CRAFT 5: "Take the fact from the row, then write what it means for the buyer." Want: state the part the row fully supports with its subject ("The bluff where Kimbel's restaurant stands was a colonial trading post", only if the row allows those words), or cut the sentence. The entry is strong without it.

**Medium 2. Willbrook has two prices on one page.** Entry 16: "In the last year, homes here sold for about $630,000 to $1.5 million." Table: "16. Willbrook Plantation | About $1 million".
