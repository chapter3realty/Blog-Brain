What the website needs from the CRM

From the Chapter3 website team, October 28, 2026. Part 1 is for Abdulla. Part 2 is for Abdulla's AI.

Part 1: the short version, for Abdulla

UrgentListing photos stop loading on the website.

The MLS photo server (Cotality Trestle) only allows 240 photos a minute and 9,000 an hour for the whole account. Every website visitor shares that limit. Each photo is also the full-size file, about 750 KB.

A few people browsing at the same time use up the 240, and then photos fail for everyone until the next minute. Buyers see blank boxes, and the site feels broken.

We already changed the website to ask for far fewer photos at once. That helps, but it cannot fix the limit. Only you can.

Please pick one of these three and tell us:

  1. Ask Cotality to raise the photo limit on the account.
  2. Let the website keep a short-term copy of each photo at Cloudflare. It would be a small version (about 40 KB instead of 750 KB), kept for 24 hours and then thrown away. The site would get each photo from Trestle once a day instead of once per visitor. Today the contract says "never store photos" (rule 7), so this needs your OK.
  3. Have the CRM serve the photos through its own cached, smaller links.

Options 2 and 3 also make every page load much faster.

The owner asked about letting people sign back in with only their phone number. We advise against it: anyone who knows a phone number could open that person's saved homes. Please keep phone plus first name, as it works today.

Part 2: for Abdulla's AI

This is the Chapter3 website team. The website reads the CRM through the IDX contract in docs/website-listing-api.md (contract v1, Ticket 471 filters). Do this work on a new branch and open a pull request. Do not merge to main. The owner and Abdulla decide that.

1. UrgentPhotos hit the Trestle media limit

Photo links (https://api.cotality.com/trestle/Media/...) answer with minute-quota-limit: 240 and hour-quota-limit: 9000, shared by every website visitor and anything else on the account. Each photo is the full-size JPEG, about 750 KB, with cache-control: public, max-age=10. Under normal traffic the minute limit runs out and photos fail for everyone.

Contract rule 7 says photos are hotlinked only (never download, store or re-host), so the website cannot fix this alone. Pick one and tell us which:

  1. Raise the media quota with Cotality.
  2. Approve an edge copy: Cloudflare keeps each photo for 24 hours, resized (about 480 px wide for cards, about 1200 px on listing pages), fetched from Trestle once a day. Nothing in a database; the copy expires by itself. This changes rule 7.
  3. Serve photos from the CRM through cached, resized URLs, and put those URLs in photo and photos[].url.

2. Sign in with Google: POST /api/idx/google

Same shape as /api/idx/claim.

  • Request: { "credential": "<Google ID token>", "termsAccepted": true, "termsVersion": "<current>", "page": "/homes/map/" }
  • Verify the token on the server with Google's public keys (for example google-auth-library, verifyIdToken): aud equals GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID (an environment variable; the owner creates it in Google Cloud); iss is accounts.google.com or https://accounts.google.com; not expired; email_verified is true.
  • Find the lead by that email, or create one (source "Website, Google sign-in"). Record the terms acceptance as /claim does.
  • Answer { "ok": true, "token": "<visitor token>", "firstName": "..." }, the same visitor token as /claim. Errors: bad_token, terms_required, rate_limited.
  • Rate limit by IP, as /returning is. Never store the Google token. A Google sign-in carries no phone number and no text consent; the lead has none until the visitor gives them.

3. Keep /api/idx/returning as it is

Phone plus first name. A phone number alone would let anyone who knows a number open that person's saved homes and act as that lead. If you see a safe way to make it shorter, tell us and the website will match.

Let the website team define filters as rules in one file, for example config/custom-filters.json. Read it every 6 hours with the rest, list each rule in /api/idx/filters, and apply it in the search like any other key. We send changes as pull requests for Abdulla to review.
