## Rules that keep the profile safe

- **Photos:** real photos of the business only. That means the team, the office, the sign and our own photos of the area. No stock photos, no listing or MLS photos, and no photos of other firms' listings.
- **Reviews:** ask every closed client, the same way, and never offer anything for a review. Reply to every review within a week. Never name a client in a reply.
- **No rates, payments or loan talk** anywhere on the profile. Chapter3 does no financing.

## Description (650 of 750 characters)

Chapter3 Realty is a Grand Strand real estate brokerage in Murrells Inlet, South Carolina, for buyers, sellers and investors from Little River to Pawleys Island. Our Broker-in-Charge, Tim Nash, has sold real estate here for over 30 years, the third generation of his family in this market. On our website you can describe the home you want in your own words, and Chapter3 AI finds it. Before you offer, our agents get the HOA papers, a rental analysis and an insurance quote. Selling? We show you the price range for a listing and what a cash buyer would pay, side by side, and you choose. Consultations, rental analyses and pricing reviews are free.

## Services (name, then the description box)

1. **Buying a home.** Tell us what you want. We find the homes, then get the HOA papers, a rental analysis and an insurance quote before you offer.
2. **Selling a home.** A free pricing review. We show you the price range for a listing and what a cash buyer would pay, side by side, and you choose.
3. **Investment property search.** We search for homes that rent well and leave out communities that ban renting.
4. **Free rental analysis.** The rent, the costs, the cash flow and the return on a home you are looking at.
5. **Vacation rental (Airbnb) analysis.** What a nightly rental keeps after the HOA fee, the property tax and the platform's cut, and whether the town and the HOA allow it.
6. **Condo and HOA document review.** The bylaws, the budget and the reserve study, and whether an old HOA debt follows the home.
7. **Relocation help.** For buyers moving from another state: the towns, the homes and the timing, by phone or video.
