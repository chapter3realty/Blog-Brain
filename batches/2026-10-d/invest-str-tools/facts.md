**Deciding fact, checked first (researcher 4c).** On the most common kind of nightly rental for sale, a two-bedroom condo in an oceanfront building inside Myrtle Beach, the HOA fee plus the 6 percent property tax plus insurance (about $20,100 a year) is more than the average Myrtle Beach two-bedroom Airbnb keeps after its running costs (about $15,800). It needs about 105 booked nights to pay those bills; the average two-bedroom books about 87 (rows 9 to 12). The result holds under both readings of AirROI's per-size revenue (row 12 note). So the page leads with the building's fee and the calculator starts from it.

**On the AirROI rows.** AirROI is a data vendor, not an agency; no public agency publishes nightly rental income. The site already uses AirROI as its one source for these numbers (`web2/data/str-market.json`, `/invest/airbnb-income/`, `/invest/rental-returns/`), and /invest/str-vs-ltr/ in this batch uses the same pages read the same day. The rows quote AirROI's published page and say which group each figure covers. AirROI's numbers have moved since the site's August read (row 1 note); see NEEDS.md.
