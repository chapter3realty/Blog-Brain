# Shot list: /invest/str-tools/

For the photo partner. Send from the owner's Gmail with `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first.

Public roads, sidewalks, piers and beach accesses only. No faces, no plates, no house numbers. No signs or logos as the subject.

The page shows what one Grand Strand Airbnb keeps after its HOA fee, its tax and its costs, town by town. Its three photos today are 2011 aerials from the U.S. Geological Survey. Each shot below replaces one of them, or fills a section that has no photo.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Section, and what it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Myrtle Beach, a public beach access in the south end (near Springmaid Pier or the 20s Avenues South) | A row of oceanfront condo towers from the sand, wide, morning light. If the street of houses behind them shows from the access path, take that frame too. | The HOA fee on these towers is the biggest bill an Airbnb pays here. | "Why does the HOA fee matter so much" (`#condo-fee`). Replaces the 2011 USGS aerial of the towers. |
| 2 | The same Myrtle Beach access, same spot and angle | One frame on a weekday in January and one in July. | Shows the season in one look: the average Airbnb earns about five times as much in July. | "How slow is the off season" (`#season`). New pair, under the month chart. |
| 3 | Springmaid Pier, Myrtle Beach | The pier from the beach, any month. | NOAA's water temperature in the month chart is measured at this pier. | `#season`. Replaces the 2011 USGS aerial of the pier (the pier has been rebuilt since). |
| 4 | Garden City Beach, Atlantic Avenue area | A street of raised beach houses, from the street, with the marsh in view if possible. | Garden City Beach and Murrells Inlet book the most nights of the eight towns. | "Which Grand Strand town" (`#towns`). Replaces the 2011 USGS aerial of Garden City. |
| 5 | Murrells Inlet, a marsh-side street | Houses with the marsh behind, from the road. | Murrells Inlet's Airbnbs are mostly houses and earn the most a year. | `#towns` or `#season`, beside the Murrells Inlet line. New. |
