## The reader and why they typed it

1. **A buyer looking at a Grand Strand condo.** Why they typed it: a listing, an agent or a friend said "watch out for special assessments", or the HOA papers mention one. They are afraid of a five-figure bill a month after closing.
2. **An owner who just got the notice.** Why: the board voted one. They want to know if it is legal, whether they can refuse, whether they can pay over time, and whether insurance or taxes help.
3. **A seller with an assessment pending.** Why: they want to know if they or the buyer pays, and whether they must disclose it.
4. **An investor.** Why: an assessment eats a year of rent; they want to know if it is deductible and how to spot one before buying.
