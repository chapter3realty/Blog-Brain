# Needs: /hoa/special-assessments/ (batch 2026-10-d)

Shared helpers this page would use. The spec works around each one inside itself until the lead decides.

1. **A line chart in `tools/glass.js`.** The lien series (`liens-by-year.json`, 2010 to 2026) is a trend; `G.bars` draws it as 16 horizontal bars. A flat line with the latest value labelled and a table of values below would read faster. Workaround: `G.bars`, or inline SVG in the spec.
2. **A timeline or ruler helper** (years on one axis, labelled marks). For HUD's part lives (`eul-subset.json`) against a building's age. Workaround: inline SVG in the spec, or `G.bars` with years as values.
3. **A three-step "who pays first" flow** (insurance, reserve fund, owners) in `tools/illustrations.js`. Workaround: inline SVG in the spec.
4. **CRM: a search of listing remarks** (for example `remarks=special assessment`) in the public map API, counted only, no text returned. The judge's best idea for this page ("how many condos for sale mention a special assessment, and how big") cannot be done without it. The description is behind registration and `q=` matches subdivisions only.
5. **The closing offer band turns white on white when the site adds a block after it** (found 2026-11-01 in the preview, writer stage). `tools/glass.js` styles the top offer bar as `main#main>section[style^="background:var(--navy)"]:not(:last-child)`. When the edge adds the MLS disclaimer after the closing band, the closing band is no longer the last child, so it gets the light top-bar panel and its ivory H2 and text become nearly invisible. The same happens on /invest/strategies/fix-and-flip/ in the preview. Fix in glass.js: select the top bar by its content (a band whose first child is a `<p>`, or `:not(:has(>.wrap>h2))`) instead of `:not(:last-child)`. Workaround in this spec: a rule that keeps a band with an H2 dark (`:has(>.wrap>h2)`).
6. **The listing strip's AI search box is wider than a 320 px screen** (critic, review round 1). `div.hx-fb-ai` is 341 px wide and its "Find houses" button is cut off, so the page scrolls sideways to 364 px. /invest/strategies/fix-and-flip/ does the same. Fix in the shared strip CSS: `box-sizing:border-box; max-width:100%; min-width:0` on `.hx-fb-ai` and `.hx-fb-q` at 340 px and below. Workaround in this spec: that rule, in the page's own style block.
7. **The site header is 5 px too wide at 1024 px** (critic, round 1). `div.header-ctas` (SEARCH LISTINGS and the phone number) ends at x=1029. fix-and-flip and no-hoa-homes do the same. Fix in the shared header CSS: a smaller gap, or hide `.hdr-phone` between about 1000 and 1060 px. Workaround in this spec: hide `.header-ctas .hdr-phone` from 1000 to 1060 px. The phone number is still in the dark bar under the header.
8. **`creditsList` in tools/page-kit.js repeats itself on a one-photo page.** After the photo's own changes line ("resized the photo and cut ... crops") it adds "Chapter3 Realty resized these photos and cropped some of them." Print the general line only when no record has its own changes line, and say "photo" for one. Workaround in this spec: the line is removed from `ph.credits()` output.
9. **For the lead, not a helper:** the footer says "The Myrtle Beach investor-focused brokerage ... for serious real estate investors". A buyer of a condo to live in reads it as not for them (reader, round 1). The "On this page" box is one column of 10 links at 1440 px and two columns on a phone (critic, round 1). Both are shared.
