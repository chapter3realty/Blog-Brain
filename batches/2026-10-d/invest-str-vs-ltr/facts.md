**Deciding fact, checked first (researcher 4c).** Two parts.
1. For the reader: on the typical two-bedroom in Myrtle Beach, nightly renting grosses about 1.1 times a year's lease ($20,100 against $18,048) and, self-run, keeps about $2,850 less than the lease after its running costs (rows 2, 9, 13, 14). Only the top quarter of listings gross 2 to 3 times the lease (row 3). The live page's title claim, "Two to three times the gross", is true only of the top quarter.
2. For the page to be written as planned: in the City of Myrtle Beach, R zoning districts other than RMV do not allow stays under 90 days (row 17), and in buildings of more than two units used as visitor accommodation between Kings Highway and the ocean, stays of 90 days or more are not allowed (row 18). So for most oceanfront condos inside the city the choice is already made, one way, and for most houses it is made the other way. The page can be written as planned, with the rule leading.

**On the AirROI rows.** AirROI is a data vendor, not an agency. No public agency publishes nightly rental revenue. The site already uses AirROI as its one source for these numbers (`web2/data/str-market.json`, `/invest/airbnb-income/`, `/invest/rental-returns/`). The rows below quote AirROI's own published page, read today, and say which group each figure covers. AirROI's pages are dated "Updated October 1, 2026" and cover the 12 months to September 2026. `str-market.json` still holds AirROI's August read (retrieved 2026-08-15, stale by its own 90-day rule on 2026-11-13); see NEEDS.md. A second vendor is not used: `/invest/airbnb-income/` shows the vendors disagree by almost double because each counts a different set of listings.

**Siblings.** The rules city by city belong to /invest/str-rules/; the lodging tax table to /invest/str-rules/ and /invest/accommodations-tax/; the federal 7-day and 14-day tax rules to /invest/str-tax-treatment/ and /invest/14-day-rule/; stays of 90 days or more to /invest/mid-term-rentals/; market-wide Airbnb income to /invest/airbnb-income/; rent levels to /invest/rent-prices/; condotel loans to /invest/condotel-financing/.
