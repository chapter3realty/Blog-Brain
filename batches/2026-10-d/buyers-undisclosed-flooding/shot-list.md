# Shot list: /buyers/undisclosed-flooding/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Batch 2026-10-d. The page shows how we find out whether a house has flooded before. It has one stand-in photo today: floodwater on a Conway-area street after Florence (SC National Guard, public domain), in "Can a house flood if it is not in a flood zone?". The page's clues have no picture yet. Each shot below shows a clue or a fact the page already states.

Rules for every shot:
- Public road or sidewalk only.
- No faces, no license plates, no house numbers.
- No sign or logo as the subject.
- Landscape, wide. Three of each, so we can pick.

Send the files with the street name, so the caption can be exact and we can check the lot with the county before we caption it. Once he confirms in writing, they go under `chapter3realty/images/2026-10-d/buyers-undisclosed-flooding/`, each with a licence record `tools/photos.js` accepts.
