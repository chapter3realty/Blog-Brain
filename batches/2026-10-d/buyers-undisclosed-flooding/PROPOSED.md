## Proposed registry rows (each fact is, or will be, on two pages, or was wrong once)

1. **Waccamaw River at Conway, record crest: 21.16 feet, Hurricane Florence.** Source: NWS Wilmington, https://www.weather.gov/ilm/HurricaneFlorence ("The Waccamaw River at Conway crested at a record 21.16 feet, breaking its Hurricane Matthew crest by over three feet."). Already in the website's `research/relocating/local-facts.md`; likely on /buyers/relocating/hurricanes/. Known-wrong value to catch: "17.87" called the record today (it was Matthew's crest, a record only until Florence).
2. **Share of Horry County paid NFIP flood claims outside FEMA's high-risk zone: about 1 in 4 (23 percent, all years); Florence 45 percent.** Source: OpenFEMA NfipClaims v3, our count (`data/2026-10-d/buyers-undisclosed-flooding/claims-findings.json`). /buyers/coastal-insurance/ says "FEMA's own numbers: more than 1 in 4 flood claims come from outside the high-risk zones" (national). Both can stand, but each page must say which group: national or Horry County. Stale by 2027-05.
3. **Who can get a house's FEMA flood claims and CLUE report: the owner (and, for CLUE, an insurer or lender), not a buyer.** Sources: FEMA FAQs About NFIP Data; Utah and Tennessee insurance departments. Known-wrong pattern to catch: a page that tells a buyer to "pull" or "order" the CLUE report themselves (the live page did).
4. **SC disclosure form flood questions cover only "during your ownership" (questions 20, 25, 26, 27).** Source: SC REC form, effective 6/2023, updated 6/2025. Any page that says sellers must disclose "past flooding" without that limit is wrong. Stale by 2027-02 (the commission revises the form).
