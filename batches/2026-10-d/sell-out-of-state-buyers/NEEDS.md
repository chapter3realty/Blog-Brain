## Fixer, review round 1 (2026-11-01)

8. **The closing offer band is unreadable on glass pages (glass.js COMPACT_RULES).** `TOP = ...section[style^="background:var(--navy)"]:not(:last-child)` also catches the last band once the edge writes the IDX disclaimer after it, so the band turns light and its ivory heading and line vanish. /invest/strategies/fix-and-flip/ has the same fault. Workaround in this spec only: `PAGE_CSS` gives the navy band with no later `section` sibling (`:not(:has(~section))`) the dark panel back. 9. **The listing-search box overflows at 320 px (shared listings-widget CSS).** `.hx-fb-ai` and `.hx-fb-q` are flex items with `min-width:auto`, so the box is 340 px wide in a 320 px window. Workaround in this spec: `main#main .hx-fb-ai,main#main .hx-fb-q{min-width:0;max-width:100%}`. Every page with the box needs the same rule in the shared CSS.
10. **The header's phone number runs 5 px past a 1024 px window (shared header CSS).** Workaround in this spec: between 1000 and 1120 px, `.hdr-phone-num` is hidden and the phone icon stays (the link keeps its aria-label with the number). Shared fix: the same rule, or a smaller header gap.
