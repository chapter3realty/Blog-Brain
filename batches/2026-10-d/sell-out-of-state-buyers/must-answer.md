## Why they typed it

- A seller who lives in New York or North Carolina and owns a condo or house here wants to know if they must fly in, who signs what, and how much the state keeps at closing.
- A local seller heard that out-of-state buyers pay more and wants to know if that is true, how many there are, and how to reach them.
- Both want to know if the timing (fall, winter) hurts them.

## The list, ranked

1. **Can I sell my Grand Strand home without coming back?** Yes: how the deed is signed in another state (notary, two witnesses), the attorney closing, the power of attorney option, what cannot be done by video. Rows 12 to 17.
2. **What does South Carolina keep at closing if I live elsewhere?** The buyer withholds by law; the I-295 affidavit lowers or ends it; a main home inside the Section 121 exclusion owes none; the money comes back on a South Carolina return. The rate and worked example live on /sell/capital-gains/; this page gives the rule and links. Rows 18 to 21.
3. **How many buyers here come from out of state, and from where?** Our count of 12 months of recorded deeds: about 3 in 10 buyers have their tax bill sent to another state; more than half of condo buyers; North Carolina first, then New York, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Virginia. Rows 1 to 6.
4. **Do out-of-state buyers pay more?** Tested on recorded deeds: in the same building or subdivision, they paid the same middle price as South Carolina buyers. The old page's claim is not true. Row 7.
5. **When do they buy? Is fall too late?** Recorded sales by month: out-of-state buyers closed every month; the fewest in January, the most in October and May. Row 8.
6. **How many people move here?** Census: about 14,200 more people moved into Horry County from elsewhere in the US than moved out in the year to July 2025; the metro grew second fastest of 387. IRS: 12,877 households moved in from other states in one tax year, led by Suffolk County, NY. Rows 9 to 11.
7. **Why does a buyer from those places find this market good value?** Census ACS: median home value and median property tax paid in Horry against the top origin counties. Row 22 (with the 4 percent and 6 percent rule, row 23, so a second-home buyer is not misled).
8. **What must I tell a buyer about a home I rarely visit?** The disclosure statement: what it covers, the "no representation" option, the duty when you do know, and the rental management contract line. Rows 24 to 26.
9. **How do I keep my sale money safe when it is wired to me?** IC3 2025 real estate losses; call back on a known number. Rows 27 and 28.
10. **What do you (Chapter3) do for a seller who is not here, and to reach buyers who are not here?** Only what the owner confirms: owner questions in PLAN.md. The story bank supports the ad spend on relocating buyers and one remote-seller story.
11. **Foreign (Canadian) owners.** One line: FIRPTA, with the link to /invest/canadian-buyers/. Row 29.
