# Shot list: /sell/out-of-state-buyers/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Batch 2026-10-d. Most wanted first. Same rules as every request: public places only, no faces as the subject, no readable car plates, no building name as the whole subject. Landscape, the longest side at least 3,000 px, plus one upright frame of each.

## 1. Oceanfront condo towers in North Myrtle Beach, from the beach

- **Place:** the public beach in front of the three tall blue-and-yellow condo towers between Windy Hill and Crescent Beach, about 2701 South Ocean Boulevard, North Myrtle Beach. Reach it from the nearest public beach access. Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2701+S+Ocean+Blvd+North+Myrtle+Beach+SC
- **What to shoot:** the three towers side by side from the sand, the dunes and pools in front, the whole height of the buildings in frame. A second frame from farther down the beach with more of the row of towers.
- **Why:** the page says more than half of condo buyers had a tax bill in another state, and about two of every three in North Myrtle Beach and Myrtle Beach. A current photo of the condos those buyers buy shows it.
- **Replaces:** the 2011 USGS aerial of the same towers in "How many Myrtle Beach home buyers come from out of state?" (stand-in now).

- **Why:** step 6 says the deed is recorded at the Register of Deeds in Conway. A seller who lives far away has never seen it.
- **Replaces:** nothing yet; the section has only the drawn diagram. The photo goes under step 6, in "Can you sell a Grand Strand home without coming back?" (Commons was rate-limited all day on 2026-11-01, so the 2006 Commons photo was not used.)
