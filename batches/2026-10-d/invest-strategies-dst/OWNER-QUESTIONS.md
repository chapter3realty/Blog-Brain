# Owner questions: /invest/strategies/dst/

Each question has the answer Claude proposes. The page is planned on the proposed answer; a different answer changes only the parts named.

## 1. The guest author (audit item 10, open since 2026-10-01)

The live page is a guest post by Scott Efrusy, CFP, of Horizon Advisers in Plymouth, Michigan, with his paragraph, his phone number, his website and his firm's securities disclosure. The audit called it "a promotional guest paragraph from an out-of-state financial planner, with his phone number, on a securities product". The judge (2026-10-31) called his paragraph "the least precise text on the page": it says a DST lets you "shield and defer any taxation" and compares a DST to a REIT. We cannot rewrite his words.

**Proposed:** rebuild the page as Chapter3's own, with the same byline as the 1031 page (Devin Day, reviewed by Tim Nash). Remove his paragraph, his contact block and the Cetera disclosure that only his content needed. The page then does not mention him. If you want to keep him, the page can carry one short boxed line under his name with his disclosure, and nothing he did not write.

Changes if you say keep him: one box near the end; the byline stays ours.

## 2. Fees from DST sellers

FINRA warned in 2005 that a securities firm paying a real estate agent a referral fee on tenant-in-common shares sold for 1031 exchanges, a product similar to DSTs, may be breaking its rules (NASD Notice 05-18).

**Proposed answer:** Chapter3 takes no fee, referral payment or commission split from any DST sponsor, securities firm or adviser, and refers clients to no particular one. The page says so in one sentence, because a reader of a decision page should know how we are paid (page shapes, decision guide).

Changes if this is wrong: the sentence comes out, and the page needs a disclosure the owner and counsel write.
