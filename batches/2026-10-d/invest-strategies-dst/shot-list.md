# Shot list: /invest/strategies/dst/

For the photo partner. Send from the owner's Gmail with `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first.

The page is about Delaware statutory trusts (DSTs): a landlord sells a rental and buys a share of a large building a trust owns. These photos show readers that trusts of this kind already own buildings here.

Rules for every shot: from a public road or sidewalk only. Landscape. No faces, no license plates, no house or unit numbers. A sign may be small in the frame, never the subject. The page will not name the community, the trust or the sponsor.
