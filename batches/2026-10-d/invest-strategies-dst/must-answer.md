## The reader

A landlord who owns a rental on the Grand Strand, often for many years and often from out of state. He is tired of managing it, or of paying someone to. He has heard that a DST lets him sell, put off the tax and stop being a landlord. He wants to know if that is true, what it costs him, what he gives up, and whether he even qualifies. A second reader is the 1031 buyer whose replacement search is going badly and who has heard a DST can be a backup.

What he would pay an expert to tell him: is this a good deal for me, or a sales pitch? What will I really pay, and what will I not get back?
