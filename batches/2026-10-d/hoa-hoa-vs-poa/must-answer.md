## The deciding fact (checked first)

South Carolina's Homeowners Association Act defines an association by what it does, not what it is called: any entity that manages a planned community **or a horizontal property regime** under a recorded declaration that requires owners to pay assessments (S.C. Code 27-30-120(6)). The state's own disclosure form for sellers puts "a homeowners association, a property owners association, a condominium owners association, a horizontal property regime, or similar organizations" under one name, "owners association". So the page can say plainly that HOA and POA are the same thing in law here, and that the real difference is between a neighborhood of lots and a condo regime under the Horizontal Property Act.
