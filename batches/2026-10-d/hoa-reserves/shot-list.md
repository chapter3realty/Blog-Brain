# Shot list: /hoa/reserves/

For the photo partner (`templates/photo-request-email.md`). Send it in one email with the /hoa/special-assessments/ list. These shots do not repeat that list (balcony repair, rusted railings, a roof job, a row of towers).

All from public ground: sidewalk, street or a public beach access. Landscape. Three frames of each. No faces, no license plates, no unit numbers, no rental or management company signs in focus. Captions will not name the building.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Where it goes on the page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An older oceanfront tower, S Ocean Blvd, Myrtle Beach (https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=S+Ocean+Blvd+Myrtle+Beach+SC) | The top floors and roofline from the beach, with the box above the top floor (where the elevator machinery sits) in view. A long lens if he has one. | Shows the part with a 20-year life that a 30-year-old tower has already outlived once. | "Why do reserves matter more on the Grand Strand?" Replaces the 2011 USGS aerial (the stand-in), beside the HUD lives sentence. |
| 2 | An older tower next to a newer one, N Ocean Blvd, North Myrtle Beach (https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=N+Ocean+Blvd+North+Myrtle+Beach+SC) | Both buildings in one frame, close enough that the age shows: balcony style, windows, finish. | Pictures the chart of condos for sale in buildings 30 years or older. | Same section, under the age chart. Second choice for the stand-in if shot 1 fails. |
