**Deciding fact (4c).** Can public records give an owner a real first number for their area and type of home, with no MLS sold data on the public page? Yes. The Horry County deed index records a price for every sale, and the county parcel layer says what each property is. 11,047 resales recorded in the 12 months to September 2026 give a median and a middle range for 20 areas (rows 1 to 4). The page can be built as planned.

**Corrections to the live page found here.**
- FAQ "What is my home worth in Myrtle Beach?" answers "It depends". The page now has the numbers (rows 2 and 3).
- "Start anywhere you like, including the online estimates. They are a useful first look." Keep the idea, but give the local miss: in Horry County, Redfin's own published figures put half of its estimates for homes not on the market more than 6.6 percent from the price that followed (row 8).
- "In this market those details move prices by tens of thousands of dollars." Now shown: asking price per square foot doubles from two miles inland to the first row (row 17); the county's value missed the price by more than 10 percent on half of homes sold (row 9), which is about $35,000 on a $350,000 house.
- "Spring sales move fastest here." Not checked here, and the out-of-state page found closings in every month (that page owns month counts). Cut it, or leave it to the selling page.
- "Free home valuation from a licensed broker": South Carolina lets a licensee give a valuation only if it is not called an appraisal, and the licensee must give a set disclosure sentence (row 19). The form and the offer must carry that sentence.
- The listing-search box still has the old sentence "including off-market homes before they hit the public sites" (batch SWEEP.md, "For the other helpers"). A page rebuilt with mkpage gets the new sentence from its donor.
