## The reader

An owner in Horry County who typed "what is my home worth", "home value myrtle beach" or "myrtle beach home prices". Most are thinking about selling in the next year. Some want the number for insurance, a refinance, a divorce or an estate, or are watching the market. They have usually seen an online estimate already ("zillow", "redfin", "chase", "bank of america" all appear in the suggestions), and many have opened the county's property search ("horry county property value search", "assessed value"). They are worried that the estimate is wrong, that prices are falling ("home prices dropping" appears three times), and that an agent will flatter the number to win the listing. They will ask next: what would I walk away with, and when should I list.

Why they typed it (CRAFT 1): to get a first number without calling anyone, to check whether the online number or the county number is right, and to decide whether selling now makes sense.

## The deciding fact

Can public records give a reader a real first number for their area and type of home, without MLS sold data on a public page? Yes. The Horry County deed index records the price of every sale; 11,047 resales in the 12 months to September 2026 (facts row 1). The page can be written as planned.
