# Shot list for the photo partner: /buyers/programs/

For `templates/photo-request-email.md`. Most wanted first. All from public roads or sidewalks. No faces, no license plates, no house numbers, no unit numbers. Landscape. Three frames of each.

| # | Place | What to shoot | Why | Section it goes in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A subdivision street within a few minutes' drive of downtown Conway, on the Conway side, not the Carolina Forest side. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Conway+SC+29526) | A street of several similar one-story houses with front yards, the street in view. | Most houses for sale under SC Housing's $450,000 limit have a Conway address, and the photo must show a street inside USDA's area. Before we use it, we test the street against `usda-map.json`. | "Can you buy with no money down in Horry County?" It replaces the stand-in photo of Main Street, Conway. |
| 2 | A new-home street in Longs or Loris. [Longs](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Longs+SC+29568), [Loris](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Loris+SC+29569) | One finished house and one under construction on the same street. | About four in five houses for sale in the inland towns are under the limit, new homes included. | "Which homes count, and where are they for sale under $450,000?", under the town chart. That section has no photo now. |
| 3 | Seagate Village Condominium, Myrtle Beach, from Mallard Lake Drive. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mallard+Lake+Dr+Myrtle+Beach+SC+29577) | The buildings and the street. | Seagate Village is one of the 9 condo projects on HUD's FHA-approved list for Horry County. The caption will name it as on the list, nothing more. | The condo part of "Which homes count", beside the two number tiles. That part has no photo now. |
| 4 | A row of oceanfront condo towers on Ocean Boulevard, Myrtle Beach, from a public beach access or the sidewalk. [Map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Ocean+Blvd+Myrtle+Beach+SC) | Several towers in one frame. No single building as the subject, and no hotel sign in focus. | The page says buildings with a rental program are usually non-warrantable. The photo shows the kind of building the sentence means, without naming one. | The condo part of "Which homes count", beside the non-warrantable sentence. |

Every photo needs the partner's written confirmation that it is ours before it goes on the page (CLAUDE.md, photos). A licence record goes in the website's `data/photos.json`, and the files go in `chapter3realty/images/2026-10-d/buyers-programs/`.
